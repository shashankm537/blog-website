**Dixon Technologies Surges as Vivo Joint Venture Secures Government Green Light**

Dixon Technologies India witnessed a significant boost in its share price, climbing 4% to Rs 14,027 on Friday. This upward movement follows the crucial government approval granted to Chinese smartphone giant Vivo Mobile India for a joint-venture partnership with Dixon for domestic smartphone manufacturing. The deal, which had been awaiting regulatory clearance, is poised to reshape the landscape of electronics production in India.

**A Strategic Alliance for Indian Manufacturing**

The long-awaited joint venture, formalized by a binding term sheet in December 2024, sees Dixon Technologies holding a majority 51% stake, with Vivo India retaining 49%. This strategic alliance is particularly noteworthy as it required government approval under Press Note 3 of 2020, a regulation impacting investments from countries sharing a land border with India. The newly formed entity will operate as an original equipment manufacturer (OEM), focusing initially on producing smartphones for Vivo Mobiles within India. Importantly, the venture also opens avenues for manufacturing electronic devices for other brands, showcasing its broader potential.

**Expert Opinions Signal Strong Growth Prospects**

Financial experts are largely optimistic about the implications of this joint venture for Dixon Technologies. Emkay, for instance, has raised its target price for Dixon shares to Rs 15,200, a 13% increase from its previous Rs 13,477 target, while reiterating a "Buy" rating. The brokerage highlights that the regulatory approval effectively removes a significant hurdle, paving the way for scaled-up Vivo smartphone production. Emkay has consequently revised its Vivo production estimates upwards, projecting 6.5 million units for FY27 and 18 million units for FY28, leading to substantial upgrades in their earnings per share forecasts for those years.

Furthermore, Emkay points to Dixon's already dominant position in India's smartphone manufacturing sector, accounting for an estimated 45-50% of the nation's capacity. The Vivo joint venture is expected to solidify this leadership. The brokerage also anticipates continued government support for domestic electronics manufacturing, citing the proposed Mobile PLI 2.0 scheme as a key catalyst for future growth. Dixon's track record of strong returns further underpins this positive outlook.

**What To Watch**

Investors will be closely monitoring the initial production ramp-up and the diversification into manufacturing for other brands. The impact of the Mobile PLI 2.0 scheme on Dixon's operations and market share will also be a key point of observation in the coming quarters.
