## Nuro Inches Closer to Driverless Rides on California Roads

Silicon Valley startup Nuro has cleared a significant regulatory hurdle, securing a permit to test its autonomous technology in a more advanced vehicle on public California roads. While this move brings Nuro and its partner Uber closer to a driverless robotaxi future, the company isn't ready to deploy just yet.

### A New Breed of Driverless Testing

The California Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) has updated Nuro’s existing driverless permit, now allowing the company to test Lucid Gravity SUVs equipped with its self-driving systems. This is a major step up from Nuro's previous permit, which was limited to a smaller, low-speed delivery vehicle. The expanded permit means these Lucid vehicles can now operate on public streets without a human safety driver at the helm, signaling a shift towards more sophisticated autonomous capabilities.

### Paving the Way for Uber's Robotaxis

This development is particularly crucial for Uber, which plans to integrate Nuro's technology into its premium robotaxi service. While Nuro has been testing the Lucid vehicles with human safety operators for some time, and even allowing Uber employees to hail rides in them, the ability to conduct driverless testing is a critical precursor to a wider public rollout. The company anticipates commencing this driverless testing phase later this year, marking a tangible progression towards its ambitious autonomous ride-hailing goals.

### Navigating the Road Ahead

Despite this important permit, Nuro still has several regulatory checkpoints to clear before Uber's driverless service can become a reality for the public. Beyond the DMV's approval for testing, Nuro will need to secure a driverless ride-hailing permit from the California Public Utilities Commission and a final deployment permit from the DMV. These additional steps are standard for autonomous vehicle companies aiming to operate commercial services within the state.

### What To Watch

Keep an eye on Nuro's progress with driverless testing as the year progresses. The company's ability to demonstrate safe and effective autonomous operation in the Lucid vehicles will be key to obtaining further regulatory approvals. Furthermore, watch for announcements from Uber regarding the timeline and specific regions for the planned launch of its premium robotaxi service.
