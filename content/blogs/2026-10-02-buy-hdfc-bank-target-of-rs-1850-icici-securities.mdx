## HDFC Bank Stock: Analysts See Opportunity After Recent Dip

Following a period of significant stock price correction, a leading financial services firm is signaling a strong endorsement for HDFC Bank. ICICI Securities has issued a "Buy" recommendation, setting an ambitious target price that suggests a substantial rebound is on the horizon. This positive outlook comes after a period of notable changes for the banking giant.

### Navigating Post-Merger Dynamics

The recent merger has led to some significant shifts in HDFC Bank's financial structure. Analysts point to an increased loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) and a reduced share of low-cost current account savings account (CASA) deposits, alongside a greater reliance on borrowings. While the merger has boosted the bank's market share in loans, its deposit market share growth has been more moderate. ICICI Securities anticipates impressive deposit growth in the coming years, forecasting an annual increase of INR 4 to 5 trillion. Despite this robust deposit accretion, the bank is expected to manage its LDR and borrowing mix strategically, leading to a recalibration of loan growth estimates.

### Revisiting Growth Projections and Valuation

While the bank's net interest margin (NIM) in the last quarter showed a slightly positive surprise, potentially influenced by seasonal factors, ICICI Securities has adjusted its earnings per share (EPS) estimates downwards by 5% for the next two fiscal years. This revision is primarily driven by the adjusted growth forecasts. Nevertheless, the firm projects healthy returns on assets (RoA) and return on equity (RoE) for HDFC Bank in FY25 and FY26. Crucially, the target price of INR 1,850 remains firm, and the recent 10% year-to-date decline in the stock price has prompted the upgrade to a "Buy" rating. The firm suggests that the market's re-rating of the stock may be a gradual process, even with strong deposit growth, as credit growth is anticipated to be more tempered than the broader system, and borrowing substitution will likely unfold over time.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring HDFC Bank's upcoming quarterly results for further insights into its deposit and loan growth trends. The bank's ability to effectively manage its LDR and its strategy for reducing reliance on borrowings will be key determinants of its profitability and market performance in the near to medium term. The pace at which the stock price moves towards the INR 1,850 target will also be a significant indicator of market sentiment.
