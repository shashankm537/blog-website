## Indian Markets Tread Water: Nifty Stuck in a Tight Range as Investors Await a Breakout

Indian stock markets spent last week in a holding pattern, with the benchmark Nifty index oscillating within a defined range. Despite occasional attempts to push higher, the market ultimately closed with a slight dip, leaving investors to ponder the next move.

### The Consolidation Continues: Key Levels to Watch

The Nifty index experienced a relatively narrow trading band, hovering between approximately 24,025 and 24,360 throughout the week. This indicates a period of consolidation, where buyers and sellers are in a stalemate. The underlying technical structure suggests the index is facing a significant resistance zone between 24,450 and 24,750. This area is bolstered by multiple key moving averages, making it a challenging barrier to breach. However, a crucial support line, originating from April 2026 lows, is currently holding firm, preventing a sharper downturn for now.

### Selective Buying Emerges Amidst Uncertainty

While the broader market remained range-bound, there were signs of selective buying activity. Investors appear to be cautiously picking specific stocks that show promise, even as the overall sentiment remains neutral. This approach is likely to continue as traders look for opportunities within the existing trading band. A sustained move above the 24,750 mark would be a significant development, signaling a potential shift in momentum and opening the door for further upside. Conversely, a decisive break below the 23,900-24,100 support zone could signal increased selling pressure.

### What To Watch

The upcoming week is anticipated to begin with a steady to slightly positive tone, provided the Nifty successfully defends the 24,100 support level. However, the real test will be its ability to overcome the overhead resistance cluster. Traders will be closely monitoring whether the index can decisively break above 24,450 and then 24,700. Any failure to do so could see the Nifty remain confined within its current trading range.
