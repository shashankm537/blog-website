## Tejas Networks Surges on Positive Analyst Outlook, Emkay Global Sets Ambitious Target

Analysts at Emkay Global Financial are signaling strong confidence in Tejas Networks, initiating coverage with a "Buy" rating and a compelling target price. This bullish stance follows recent performance that has exceeded expectations, primarily driven by the successful execution of significant projects.

### Strong Project Pipeline Fuels Growth

Tejas Networks is positioned to capitalize on several key growth drivers, according to Emkay Global's latest research. The Indian government's focus on fostering domestic manufacturing through initiatives like the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme offers a significant tailwind. Furthermore, substantial investments in critical infrastructure projects, including BSNL's 4G network, BharatNet, and railway modernization, are expected to bolster demand for Tejas Networks' solutions. The company is also benefiting from strong references from industry giants like Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), which are opening doors to new clientele.

### Exceeding Expectations and Upgraded Forecasts

The company recently reported revenue and margin figures that surpassed Emkay Global's estimates, largely attributed to the efficient delivery of BSNL's 4G project. This positive performance has led Emkay Global to revise its financial projections upwards. For the upcoming fiscal year 2025, revenue is anticipated to quadruple compared to fiscal year 2024, a testament to the ongoing progress with BSNL and BharatNet projects. The research firm has also adjusted its earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA) margin estimates higher, reflecting the improved profitability observed.

### What to Watch

Emkay Global has updated its target price for Tejas Networks to Rs 1,100 per share, indicating a potential upside of 21% from current levels. Investors will be closely monitoring the company's continued execution on its large-scale project pipeline, particularly the BSNL 4G rollout and BharatNet expansion. Any further progress in securing new clients through its strategic partnerships will also be a key factor to observe. The global trend of diversifying telecom equipment supply chains away from Chinese manufacturers presents a sustained opportunity for Tejas Networks.
