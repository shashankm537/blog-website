## Starlink Back Online for Ukrainian Military After Reported Restrictions

Ukraine's defense minister has indicated that the military-grade Starshield internet service, a crucial component of their operations, appears to be fully functional again. This follows a period of concerning connectivity disruptions that hampered drone operations, raising questions about the underlying causes and future stability of the service.

### Navigating the Connectivity Blackout

For approximately ten days, Ukrainian forces experienced significant issues with their Starshield drones losing internet connection at speeds nearing 100 miles per hour. This technical glitch had serious tactical implications, leading to the loss of control over some unmanned aerial vehicles and forcing others to fly at reduced speeds, thereby increasing their vulnerability. The Ministry of Defense is still actively testing the restored service and has yet to receive a definitive explanation for the initial restrictions from SpaceX.

### A Diplomatic Intervention and Shifting Alliances

Reports suggest that former U.S. President Donald Trump contacted SpaceX CEO Elon Musk, urging the restoration of Starshield service. This intervention comes amidst a backdrop of publicly expressed criticisms of Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky from both Musk and Trump. Musk himself had previously claimed that Ukraine had violated Starlink's terms of service by utilizing the system for offensive drone attacks. He asserted that any cessation of Starlink's service would have immediate and catastrophic consequences for Ukraine's front lines, framing it as a factual observation rather than a threat.

### What To Watch

Eyes will remain on Ukraine's Ministry of Defense for official confirmation of sustained Starshield functionality and any official statements from SpaceX regarding the cause of the recent disruptions. The evolving relationship between SpaceX, the U.S. political landscape, and Ukraine's defense needs will likely continue to be a significant factor in the availability of this critical technology.
