## HDFC Bank: A Buy Signal from ICICI Securities Amidst Post-Merger Shifts

In a significant development for the Indian banking sector, ICICI Securities has issued a strong "Buy" recommendation for HDFC Bank, setting a target price of ₹1,850 per share. This optimistic outlook comes as the banking giant navigates the complexities and opportunities following its recent merger. Investors are closely watching how these strategic adjustments will translate into future performance.

### Navigating the New Landscape: Balance Sheet Evolution

The merger has undoubtedly reshaped HDFC Bank's balance sheet, presenting both challenges and avenues for growth. Key changes include a noticeable increase in the loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) and a shift in the composition of its funding, with a reduced share of low-cost CASA deposits and a greater reliance on borrowings. Despite these structural shifts, the bank's loan market share has seen a substantial jump, indicating its expanded reach in the lending space. The firm anticipates robust deposit growth, projecting a significant annual increase in absolute terms, even as they adjust loan growth expectations downwards to a more balanced pace, aiming to optimize the LDR.

### The Road Ahead: Growth, Profitability, and Valuation

While the recent Net Interest Margin (NIM) performance for Q4FY24 showed some positive signs, ICICI Securities acknowledges the influence of seasonality. Consequently, they have slightly revised their Earnings Per Share (EPS) estimates for the coming fiscal years downwards, primarily due to the recalibrated growth projections. However, the outlook for profitability remains strong, with healthy Return on Assets (RoA) and Return on Equity (RoE) figures anticipated for FY25 and FY26. The analyst firm views the recent 10% year-to-date correction in HDFC Bank's stock price as an attractive entry point, prompting the upgrade to a "Buy" rating, while also noting that a full re-rating might be a gradual process.

### What To Watch

Investors will be keen to observe how effectively HDFC Bank manages its deposit accretion strategy in the evolving competitive environment. The pace at which it can substitute borrowings with deposits and balance its credit growth against system-wide trends will be critical factors influencing its Net Interest Margins and overall profitability in the coming quarters.
