## Erin Brockovich Targets Data Center Secrecy, Empowers Communities

Famed environmental activist Erin Brockovich is turning her attention to a new, increasingly prevalent issue: the opaque world of data center construction. Her latest initiative aims to shed light on how these massive facilities impact local communities, often without adequate public knowledge or input.

### Unmasking the Data Deluge: A Community-Driven Map

Brockovich, whose relentless pursuit of justice against corporate polluters was immortalized in film, has launched a new website featuring a crowdsourced map of data centers across the United States. This ambitious project is described as a "work in progress," actively gathering information reported by residents living near these facilities. The response has been overwhelming, with Brockovich revealing that in the first month of her call for submissions, she received close to 4,000 reports detailing data center-related concerns.

### The Cry for Transparency: Beyond Noise and Water

In a recent Substack post, Brockovich highlighted a single, recurring theme emerging from the flood of community submissions. "The single most common concern — more than noise, more than water usage, more than rising utility bills — is the one word that keeps appearing in submission after submission: transparency," she stated. Brockovich clarifies that her mission is not an outright condemnation of data centers or artificial intelligence itself. Instead, she is raising alarms about a pervasive pattern: projects being announced only after permits are already secured, developers who are unresponsive to community queries, and local officials who enter into non-disclosure agreements before residents are even aware a project is under consideration.

### What To Watch

Expect to see Brockovich and her team continue to expand the data center map, providing a crucial resource for communities seeking information. Her advocacy is likely to encourage greater public scrutiny of data center development proposals and push for more proactive communication from developers and local governments. The growing pressure for transparency may also influence regulatory bodies and lead to policy changes aimed at ensuring communities have a voice in projects that significantly alter their environments.
