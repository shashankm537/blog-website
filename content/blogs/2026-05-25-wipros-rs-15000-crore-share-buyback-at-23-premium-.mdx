## Wipro Announces Major Share Buyback: A Lucrative Opportunity for Investors?

IT giant Wipro is set to launch a significant Rs 15,000 crore share buyback program, offering shareholders an attractive price that represents a substantial premium. With the record date now set, investors are weighing the decision of whether to acquire Wipro shares to capitalize on this substantial corporate action.

### What's the Deal with the Buyback?

Wipro has officially declared June 5th as the record date for its massive Rs 15,000 crore share repurchase. The IT services behemoth is offering to buy back its shares at Rs 250 apiece. This price point is a notable 23% higher than the stock's recent closing price of Rs 203.11 on the National Stock Exchange. This marks the company's first such initiative in almost three years, generating considerable interest among its shareholder base.

The company's board greenlit the plan in April, aiming to repurchase up to 60 crore shares, which constitutes approximately 5.7% of its total paid-up share capital. The buyback will be conducted through a tender offer, meaning all shareholders holding Wipro stock in their demat accounts on the record date will be eligible to participate. This includes investors who may have acquired shares by converting their American Depository Receipts. Furthermore, Wipro's promoters have signaled their intention to participate in this buyback.

### Why Buy Back Shares?

Companies typically engage in share buybacks for several strategic reasons. Often, it's a way to return surplus cash to shareholders in a tax-efficient manner. By reducing the number of outstanding shares, a buyback can also boost earnings per share, potentially leading to an increase in the stock's valuation. For Wipro, this move could signal confidence in its future prospects and a desire to enhance shareholder returns. The premium offered is designed to incentivize investors to tender their shares back to the company.

### What to Watch

Investors should carefully note the buyback window and the specific entitlement ratio, which are expected to be announced soon. These details will provide a clearer picture of how many shares each shareholder can effectively tender. Given the significant premium, many retail investors are likely to consider participating. However, understanding the tax implications and the potential impact on their overall portfolio diversification is crucial before making any investment decisions.
