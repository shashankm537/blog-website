## AI Takes Center Stage: Rethinking Data Infrastructure for the Agent Era

The rapid advancement of artificial intelligence is forcing a critical re-evaluation of how we manage and utilize data. As AI agents become more sophisticated, the limitations and complexities of traditional data engineering practices are coming to the forefront, prompting a shift towards more dynamic and intelligent systems.

### The Rise of Ontologies and Knowledge Graphs, Accelerated

For years, concepts like ontologies and knowledge graphs have been discussed within data circles, but their true potential has only recently been unlocked by the advent of powerful AI agents. While the foundational principles of RDF, OWL2, and SHACL remain important, the real question now is how effectively these standards can be applied in the demanding, fast-paced business environments of today. The sheer volume and speed of data processing have escalated dramatically, making more agile and context-aware data solutions essential.

### The "Modern Data Stack" Era Draws to a Close

It appears the reign of the "modern data stack" may be officially over. Even tools traditionally used for data cataloging are now evolving into more sophisticated "context engines." PostHog has articulated this shift perfectly, highlighting how many organizations are finding themselves "bundled" into new approaches, whether by design or by necessity. This indicates a move away from rigid, siloed data architectures towards more integrated and intelligent data ecosystems.

### GoDaddy Pioneers AI for Data Pipeline Automation

GoDaddy offers a compelling case study in how AI can revolutionize data engineering. They've developed an AI agent designed to streamline the creation of data pipelines, a process that historically involved significant manual effort and adherence to strict naming conventions. This agent intelligently queries for missing information, analyzes source data, and automates the generation of pipeline files and even pull requests. Their experience underscores the power of leveraging existing templates and standards to provide AI agents with a solid foundation for tackling routine engineering tasks efficiently.

### What To Watch

Expect to see continued innovation in how AI agents interact with and manage data pipelines and infrastructure. The integration of open table formats like Apache Iceberg and Delta Lake with streaming ingestion and AI-driven query patterns will likely become a major focus, demanding new architectural approaches to support both raw storage and real-time analytical needs.
