# A-One Steels Sets the Stage for a Major Public Offering

Get ready, investors! A-One Steels has officially unveiled its much-anticipated Initial Public Offering (IPO), with a price band set between Rs 385 and Rs 405 per share. This significant public offering is slated to open its doors to investors on September 24, 2026, offering a limited window to secure a stake in the company.

## Unpacking the IPO Details

The Rs 405 crore public issue is structured as a book-built IPO, a common method where the market determines the final price through investor demand. It comprises a fresh issuance of approximately 87.65 lakh shares, aiming to raise Rs 355 crore, alongside an offer for sale (OFS) of 12.35 lakh shares to the tune of Rs 50 crore. This dual approach indicates a strategy to inject capital for growth while also providing an exit route for some existing shareholders.

## Navigating the Subscription Window and Beyond

Investors keen on participating will have three days to submit their bids, with the subscription period concluding on September 28, 2026. The allocation of shares is expected to be finalized by September 29, paving the way for the company's debut on both the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) on October 1, 2026, pending necessary approvals. For those looking to enter, the minimum investment for one lot of 37 shares at the upper price band would be Rs 14,985. A notable point for comparison is A-One Steels' P/E ratio, which appears significantly lower than the industry average, suggesting a potentially attractive valuation.

### What To Watch

All eyes will be on the subscription levels during the three-day window, as robust investor demand could signal strong market confidence. The company's performance post-listing will also be a key indicator of its long-term prospects, particularly how it utilizes the funds raised from this significant IPO.
