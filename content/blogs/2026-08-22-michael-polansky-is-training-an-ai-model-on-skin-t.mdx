## Biotech's Unassuming Innovator: Michael Polansky's Bold Leap into Living Tissue AI

In a field often dominated by flamboyant personalities, Michael Polansky cuts a surprisingly quiet figure. Yet, this founder of Outer Biosciences is making waves by pioneering a groundbreaking approach: training artificial intelligence on living human skin. This innovative venture, kept under wraps until now, merges the worlds of cutting-edge AI with complex biological research, all while Polansky navigates a life entwined with global superstar Lady Gaga.

### From Finance to Future Medicine

Polansky's journey to the forefront of biotech is as unconventional as his current work. After graduating from Harvard with a focus on applied mathematics and computer science, he honed his analytical skills at the renowned hedge fund Bridgewater Associates. While he found the experience valuable, Polansky admits it wasn't his ultimate passion. A chance encounter through a childhood neighbor led him to Sean Parker, sparking a move to San Francisco and an entry into the world of venture capital, where he quickly established himself at Founders Fund.

### The Outer Biosciences Revolution: Living Tissue Meets AI

Outer Biosciences, Polansky's venture, has been quietly working on a revolutionary concept: extending the viability of living human skin outside the body for extended periods, currently over a month. This is no small feat, requiring sophisticated methods to maintain tissue health and function ex vivo. The ultimate goal is to leverage this living tissue as a unique data source for training advanced AI models. This innovative approach promises to unlock new insights into skin biology, drug development, and personalized medicine in ways previously unimaginable.

### What To Watch

The success of Outer Biosciences' AI training on living tissue could signal a paradigm shift in both biotechnology and artificial intelligence. Keep an eye on potential breakthroughs in accelerated drug testing, more accurate disease modeling, and the development of highly personalized skincare solutions. The company's next steps will likely involve expanding the scope of their research and potentially seeking strategic partnerships to bring their discoveries to market.
