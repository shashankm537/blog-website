## Major Websites Compromised: Malicious Code Targets Visitors

Major news outlets and popular services like CNN, the BBC, and Zoom were among the hundreds of websites inadvertently distributing malicious code to their visitors. An exploit targeting the Optimizely Web Experimentation platform allowed attackers to inject harmful scripts, potentially stealing financial information and even attempting to exploit iPhone and iPad vulnerabilities.

### The Invisible Threat: What Happened and Who Was Affected

Optimizely, a platform used by many businesses to test website designs and features, confirmed a security breach that allowed an unauthorized actor to insert malicious JavaScript into its system. This compromised code was then distributed to visitors of at least 178 websites that utilize Optimizely's services. The attacker's goal was twofold: to replace cryptocurrency wallet addresses with their own, aiming to redirect funds, and to capture sensitive credit card details from unsuspecting users. While the exact number of compromised card details remains unknown, the potential for widespread financial data theft is significant.

### Beyond Financial Data: An iOS Exploit Emerges

Adding another layer of concern, the malicious code also contained a secondary payload designed to target iPhones and iPads. This "iOS exploit kit" was intended to leverage vulnerabilities on Apple's mobile devices. Fortunately, reports indicate that no iPhones or iPads were successfully taken over. However, the presence of this advanced exploit highlights the sophistication and broad ambitions of the attackers involved.

### What To Watch

Optimizely has stated that the vulnerability has been patched and the malicious code removed. The company is also implementing additional security measures to prevent future unauthorized code insertions and has secured affected customer accounts. However, questions remain about how the initial access was gained and whether the attacker still possesses any lingering presence within the Optimizely system. Visitors to the affected websites should remain vigilant, monitor their financial accounts for any suspicious activity, and ensure their devices are up-to-date with the latest security patches. The security industry will be closely watching Optimizely's public explanation of the breach and the effectiveness of their implemented safeguards.
