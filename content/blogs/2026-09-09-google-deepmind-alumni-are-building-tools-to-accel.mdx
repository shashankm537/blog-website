## From DeepMind to Fusion: New Startup Aims to Power the Future

Two former Google DeepMind engineers, Federico Felici and Jonas Buchli, are leveraging their expertise in complex control systems to accelerate the development of fusion energy. Their new venture, Fusionality, is poised to address a critical gap in the burgeoning fusion industry by providing specialized control hardware and software, potentially speeding up the timeline for grid-ready fusion power.

### The Underserved Fusion Control Market

For years, the pursuit of fusion power has been hampered by the lack of readily available, specialized components. Fusion companies have consistently voiced a need for control system suppliers who understand the unique challenges of managing superheated plasmas. Felici and Buchli recognized this unmet demand, realizing that their deep experience in building sophisticated control systems for experimental fusion devices directly addressed a widespread industry bottleneck. "We spent quite a lot of our time solving exactly the problems that our customers need to solve," Felici commented, highlighting the natural fit for their entrepreneurial endeavor.

### Launching Fusionality with Seed Funding

Capitalizing on this identified market opportunity, Felici and Buchli launched Fusionality in Lausanne, Switzerland, this year. The startup has already secured a significant CHF 3 million ($3.7 million) pre-seed funding round from investors including Founderful and Playfair. This initial investment signals strong confidence in their vision to standardize and streamline the development of crucial fusion reactor control systems. Fusionality aims to move away from the current model where each fusion company builds its control systems from scratch, recognizing that a substantial portion of these systems share common requirements.

### The Challenge of Plasma Control

Fusion power relies on replicating the process that powers the sun, fusing atoms to release immense energy. This process typically involves confining and heating plasma to extreme temperatures. Maintaining the precise conditions—temperature, shape, and fuel density—within a fusion reactor is an extraordinarily complex task that demands split-second adjustments and highly sophisticated control mechanisms. Developing these intricate systems from the ground up is a significant undertaking for individual fusion companies, diverting resources and potentially slowing innovation.

### What To Watch

Fusionality's success will hinge on its ability to deliver reliable, high-performance control solutions that can integrate seamlessly into diverse fusion reactor designs. The company's progress in securing early customers and demonstrating the effectiveness of its technology will be key indicators of its impact on the fusion industry's acceleration. As more fusion startups emerge, the demand for specialized suppliers like Fusionality is expected to grow, potentially shaping the future supply chain for this transformative energy source.
