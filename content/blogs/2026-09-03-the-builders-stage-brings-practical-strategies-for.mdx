## Navigating the Startup Ascent: TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 Unveils the Builders Stage

Get ready for actionable advice and real-world strategies as TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 gears up to host its dedicated "Builders Stage." This crucial platform will convene founders, operators, and investors for candid discussions on the essential elements of building and scaling successful tech ventures.

### Unpacking the Secrets to Growth

The Builders Stage is designed for the ambitious founder who has moved beyond the initial spark of an idea and is now focused on sustainable expansion. Recognizing that scaling presents a unique set of hurdles, this stage is one of six specialized tracks at Disrupt 2026, aiming to equip attendees with the knowledge to overcome challenges in fundraising, talent acquisition, market entry, and the critical transition from seed to Series A funding rounds. Expect practical takeaways and interactive Q&A sessions to ensure you leave with concrete plans you can implement immediately.

### Insights from Industry Leaders

This year's Builders Stage promises to feature a formidable lineup of influential figures from the tech landscape. Attendees will gain direct access to the minds behind groundbreaking companies and influential investment firms. Speakers will include Grant Lee, CEO and co-founder of Gamma; Leah Solivan, founder and general partner at Precedent.vc; and Robby Stein, VP of Product at Google, among others. These leaders will share their hard-won lessons through case studies and frank conversations, covering vital topics such as securing capital, strategic hiring, effective go-to-market plans, and the operational nuances that drive exponential growth.

### More Than Just Talks: A Community of Innovators

Beyond the insightful sessions, Disrupt 2026 offers an unparalleled networking opportunity, bringing together over 10,000 founders, investors, operators, and technology leaders at the Moscone Center in San Francisco from October 13-15. The Builders Stage is a cornerstone of this ecosystem, fostering connections and collaborative problem-solving. A sneak peek at the agenda reveals sessions like "How to Win When You’re Not Building AI," featuring insights from Shan Shan of Baillie Gifford and Yuri Sagalov of General Catalyst, highlighting that success in the current market isn't solely dependent on AI development.

### What To Watch

As TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 approaches, expect further announcements regarding speakers and specific session details for the Builders Stage. Keep an eye on updates for more deep dives into fundraising strategies, innovative hiring practices, and the future of go-to-market approaches in a rapidly evolving tech environment.
