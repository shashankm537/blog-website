## Tejas Networks: Emkay Global Sees Strong Upside Ahead, Recommends "Buy"

A leading financial research firm has thrown its weight behind Tejas Networks, a domestic telecom equipment manufacturer, issuing a strong "buy" recommendation and setting an ambitious price target. This optimism stems from robust recent performance and a favorable outlook driven by government initiatives and evolving global telecom trends.

### A Performance That Impressed

Emkay Global Financial's latest report highlights that Tejas Networks has exceeded their expectations in terms of both revenue and profit margins. This strong showing is largely attributed to the successful execution of the BSNL 4G project. The firm pointed to several key growth drivers that position Tejas Networks favorably within the Indian and global telecom landscape.

The Indian government's focus on boosting domestic manufacturing through initiatives like the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme is a significant tailwind for Tejas Networks. Furthermore, substantial investments being made in critical infrastructure projects such as BSNL, BharatNet, and the Railways are expected to fuel demand for the company's products. Strategic partnerships with industry giants like TCom and TCS are also opening doors to new clients, enhancing Tejas Networks' market reach.

### Riding the Wave of Global Change

Beyond domestic factors, Tejas Networks is poised to benefit from a broader global shift in the telecom sector. As nations increasingly seek to diversify their supply chains and move away from equipment manufactured in China, Indian companies like Tejas Networks are emerging as attractive alternatives. This geopolitical and strategic realignment in the global telecom equipment market presents a considerable opportunity for the company to capture market share. Emkay Global anticipates that the company's revenue could potentially quadruple in FY25 compared to FY24, driven by the ongoing BSNL and Bharatnet projects.

### What To Watch

Investors will be keenly observing the pace of execution on these large-scale projects and any further wins from new clients secured through partnerships. The company's ability to capitalize on the global trend of diversifying telecom equipment sourcing will also be a crucial factor to monitor in the coming quarters. Emkay Global's revised price target of Rs 1100 per share suggests a significant upside potential, making Tejas Networks a stock to watch closely.
