## Drowning in Data: How One Company Reinvented Its Experimentation Game

When the dream of data-driven product development crumbles under its own weight, what's a company to do? For ManyChat, a year ago, the desire to experiment more had morphed into a painful reality of clunky processes and bottlenecks. This is the story of how they navigated the choppy waters of choosing an experimentation platform and emerged with a more streamlined, effective approach.

### The Bottleneck Blues: When Innovation Grinds to a Halt

At ManyChat, the frustration was palpable. Imagine engineers having to delay launching a second promising product idea simply because the existing system made it a technical nightmare to manage two separate experiment allocations. The sheer effort required to set up and manage experiments was actively hindering the pace of innovation, leading to a "better safe than sorry" mentality that stifled promising ideas. For those fortunate enough to get an experiment running, the process often involved tedious, repetitive tasks like copying and pasting allocation logic, a far cry from the agile development teams aspire to.

### Navigating the Choppy Waters: The Decision-Making Process

The search for a solution wasn't just about finding a tool; it was about overhauling a fundamental workflow. The team delved deep into understanding their pain points, recognizing that the existing "machinery" meant to facilitate data-driven decisions was actually becoming an impediment. This introspection allowed them to define what a truly effective experimentation platform would need to deliver – not just features, but a genuine improvement in their ability to test and learn. The industry itself was in flux during their evaluation, with significant acquisitions happening, adding a layer of complexity and urgency to their decision.

### Beyond the Contract: The Unexpected Payoffs

The chosen platform, Eppo, was the outcome, but the real victory lay in the transformation of their process. By investing in a robust experimentation system, ManyChat moved beyond the daily grind of configuration issues and into a space where they could confidently and efficiently test new ideas. This freed up valuable engineering and analyst time, allowing them to focus on higher-impact work rather than wrestling with logistical hurdles. The ripple effect was a renewed ability to be truly data-driven, translating directly into a more agile and responsive product development cycle.

### What To Watch

Expect to see ManyChat leverage its new experimentation capabilities to accelerate its product roadmap. Their ability to quickly and reliably test hypotheses will likely lead to faster iteration cycles and more informed product decisions. The industry will be watching closely to see how other companies facing similar experimentation challenges adapt their strategies.
