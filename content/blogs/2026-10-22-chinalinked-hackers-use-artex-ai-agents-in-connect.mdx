## AI Supercharges Cyber Attacks: China-Linked Hackers Leverage New Tech in Major Breach

A sophisticated cyberattack, amplified by cutting-edge artificial intelligence, has compromised the systems of hundreds of organizations globally. Microsoft has revealed that a China-aligned hacking group, known as Storm-0940, is behind a significant supply chain attack that exploited vulnerabilities in ConnectWise Cloud Backup, using a powerful AI tool to accelerate their malicious activities at an unprecedented pace.

### The ARTEX Advantage: AI at "Machine Speed"

The key to Storm-0940's recent success lies in their deployment of ARTEX, a potent AI framework initially designed for penetration testing. This framework, built with the Go programming language, was repurposed by the attackers to dramatically speed up critical phases of their operations. Microsoft observed ARTEX being used for rapid scanning and reconnaissance, identifying potential targets, and then swiftly executing password spraying and abuse of OAuth applications to gain unauthorized access to downstream victim systems. This AI-driven approach allowed the attackers to operate at "machine speed," significantly outmaneuvering traditional defenses and human-led security efforts.

### A Wide Net Cast: Global Targets and Evasive Tactics

The impact of this attack is far-reaching, with hundreds of organizations across a diverse array of sectors falling victim since September 2024. The affected industries span critical areas like aerospace and defense, government, financial services, and technology, alongside sectors such as chemicals, energy, manufacturing, and even non-profits and think tanks. Storm-0940's strategy involved leveraging compromised ConnectWise Cloud Backup application access to authenticate into target environments without needing interactive user sign-ins, making their presence harder to detect. Following this initial breach, the AI-accelerated actions included further reconnaissance, manipulation of application credentials, and attempts to escalate privileges for persistent access. This group has been observed using sophisticated techniques, including evasive password spray attacks and exploiting internet-facing applications, since at least 2021.

### What To Watch

The increasing adoption of AI in offensive cyber operations by state-sponsored actors like Storm-0940 signals a significant shift in the threat landscape. Organizations should anticipate a rise in AI-powered attacks that are faster, more scalable, and more difficult to detect. The use of frameworks like ARTEX highlights the urgent need for enhanced AI-driven security solutions and a proactive approach to identifying and mitigating AI-enabled threats. The cybersecurity community will be closely monitoring for any further developments regarding Storm-0940's activities and the broader implications of AI's role in cyber warfare.
