## Building a Data Foundation: Expert Insights and Real-World Migrations

In the ever-evolving landscape of data, building robust and efficient data platforms is paramount. This week, we're diving into essential resources that demystify the process, from foundational principles to monumental migration projects, offering practical guidance for teams looking to harness their data effectively.

### Mastering the Fundamentals: A New eBook for Data Platform Architects

For those embarking on the ambitious journey of constructing a data platform from the ground up, a new eBook, "Data Platform Fundamentals," promises to be an invaluable guide. It delves into the architectural strategies that empower teams to deliver features at an accelerated pace, highlighting the crucial role of composable systems. Crucially, the guide emphasizes the non-negotiable importance of data quality, providing actionable methods to preemptively identify and rectify issues before they impact end-users. Furthermore, it sheds light on the concept of observability, explaining how its implementation can dramatically speed up problem-solving within complex data environments.

### Lessons from the Frontlines: The NEXT QUERY 2026 Data + AI Summit

Mark your calendars for November 4th, as NEXT QUERY 2026, a free virtual summit, will bring together leading data and AI professionals. Attendees will gain direct insights from engineers at tech giants like Apple, AWS, and Microsoft AI, who will share their hard-won experiences. The summit will feature practical, production-tested lessons on critical topics including the use of AI agents powered by business semantics, ensuring data accuracy, and maintaining fresh, secure data contexts with robust governance and access controls. Attendees can also expect discussions on achieving high performance at scale for analytics and navigating the complex trade-offs inherent in architectural decisions, from deployment to cost-effectiveness.

### A Monumental Migration: Booking.com's Cloud Journey

The task of migrating vast data infrastructures is often fraught with challenges, but Booking.com offers a compelling case study in its recent blog post. The company details its ambitious undertaking of moving Europe's largest data lake to the cloud, achieving this monumental feat on schedule and without disrupting daily operations. Their success was largely attributed to an incremental migration strategy, processing workloads in manageable batches. This approach, coupled with the use of continuously updated shared data copies, ensured that various teams could continue their work seamlessly without significant delays, ultimately leading to more agile systems and enhanced data governance.

### What To Watch

The forthcoming NEXT QUERY 2026 summit will likely reveal innovative approaches to AI and data governance. Keep an eye on how companies are tackling real-time data integration and access control in increasingly complex cloud environments. Booking.com's detailed account of their data lake migration also serves as a benchmark for large-scale data infrastructure transformations.
