## Venture Capital Takes Another Swing at Pro Sports Ownership

Venture capital firms are increasingly looking beyond tech startups, with Collaborative Fund becoming the latest to invest in professional sports. Following in the footsteps of Thrive Capital, this move signals a growing trend of VCs deploying capital into the lucrative world of sports franchises.

### A New Frontier for Venture Capital

Collaborative Fund, a New York-based firm known for early investments in companies like Lyft and Reddit, has announced a stake in the D.C. United soccer club and its home stadium, Audi Field. This diversification marks a significant shift for a firm managing approximately $1 billion, demonstrating a desire to tap into the stability and brand power of established sports entities. It’s a strategy that Thrive Capital pioneered just months ago, intentionally creating a dedicated vehicle, Thrive Eternal, to acquire and hold iconic sports franchises for the long term.

### The Evolution of Sports Investment

Historically, professional sports ownership has been dominated by ultra-wealthy individuals and traditional private equity firms. We've seen tech titans like Vinod Khosla making substantial personal investments in teams like the Seattle Seahawks and the San Francisco 49ers. Meanwhile, established players like Sixth Street, Ares, and RedBird have long held stakes in various leagues and clubs, financing deals ranging from outright acquisitions to stadium development. Collaborative Fund's entry, however, suggests venture capital's unique approach is now becoming a distinct category in the sports investment landscape.

### What To Watch

With another venture capital firm making a tangible play in sports, all eyes will be on how these new investment structures perform. Expect to see more VCs explore similar opportunities, potentially targeting niche sports or emerging leagues. The long-term implications for team valuations and management styles will be fascinating to observe as this trend matures.
