**Apollo Micro Systems Rockets to New Highs: What's Fueling the Defence Stock's Surge?**

Defence sector darling Apollo Micro Systems has seen its shares ignite, climbing a remarkable 19% over the past two trading sessions and hitting fresh record highs. This impressive rally is directly linked to the company's stellar financial performance and strategic growth initiatives, signaling a strong outlook for the defence equipment manufacturer.

### Explosive Quarterly Results Drive Momentum

Apollo Micro Systems has delivered a powerful punch with its latest quarterly earnings, igniting investor confidence. The company reported a staggering 163% jump in consolidated net profit for the fourth quarter, reaching Rs 36.8 crore compared to Rs 14 crore in the previous year. This surge was mirrored in its revenue from operations, which climbed by an impressive 81% year-on-year to Rs 293.3 crore. These figures underscore a significant upswing in the company's business and its ability to translate sales into robust profits.

### A Year of Breakthrough Growth and Strategic Moves

The strong quarterly performance caps off what the company's Managing Director, Baddam Karunakar Reddy, has dubbed a "breakthrough year" for Apollo Micro Systems. The full fiscal year saw consolidated profit leap by 90% to Rs 107.4 crore, with revenue from operations growing 61% to Rs 904.3 crore. Beyond the impressive financials, the company has been busy expanding its capabilities. Key milestones include the successful acquisition of IDL Explosives, obtaining a crucial licence for Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) manufacturing, and securing its inaugural export order. These strategic maneuvers, coupled with an all-time high order book of Rs 1,432 crore as of March 31, 2026, paint a picture of a company poised for sustained expansion.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely observing the company's progress on its next planned acquisition through ADIPL, which is anticipated before the end of the next financial year. Furthermore, the continued execution of its strong order book and the integration of its new manufacturing capabilities, particularly in UAVs, will be key factors to monitor for ongoing growth and potential further share price appreciation.
