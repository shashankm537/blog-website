## Meta Unlocks Your Gadgets with a Creative AI Twist

Meta's popular personal AI agent, Muse, known for handling tasks like travel booking and form filling, is stepping into the hardware arena. The tech giant has just unveiled an exciting open-source initiative, inviting developers and tech enthusiasts to infuse their own creations with Muse's intelligence.

### Building the Future, One Gadget at a Time

This new venture, dubbed Muse Gadgets, is a game-changer for anyone with a penchant for tinkering. Meta is generously providing the foundational software – open-source firmware and a Linux software development kit – to empower creators. Imagine giving everyday objects a smart upgrade, from a personal e-ink display that shows your Muse updates to a device that connects directly to your television for an enhanced viewing experience.

The possibilities appear vast, with Meta encouraging users to connect Muse to a wide array of hardware. Whether you're working with a budget-friendly Raspberry Pi or an ESP32 board, you can now link Muse to your displays, sensors, buttons, and any other components you have at your disposal. To foster this burgeoning community, Meta has also established a dedicated Discord channel for support and collaboration.

### A Glimpse of What's Possible

Meta isn't just asking others to build; they've been busy prototyping themselves. Nat Friedman, head of product at Meta's Superintelligence Labs, showcased a device called Muse Home Link. This clever USB-C powered gadget allows Muse to seamlessly integrate with a home network, enabling it to communicate with smart devices like speakers and televisions. Meta has even produced 5,000 of these Home Links, offering them as a free incentive to Muse subscribers, a promotion that saw significant initial interest.

This move signals Meta's broader ambition to embed Muse across various platforms and user bases. Beyond individual consumers, the company is actively pursuing enterprise and small business applications, recently launching Muse for Small Business as a free offering. Muse Gadgets is a key piece in this expansive strategy, demonstrating a commitment to making Muse an indispensable, versatile AI companion.

### What To Watch

The rollout of Muse Gadgets and the accompanying community support are likely to spark a wave of innovative hardware projects. Keep an eye on the developments within the Muse Gadgets Discord channel and look for early examples of custom-built devices that showcase the power of an AI-infused hardware ecosystem. We can also anticipate Meta continuing to expand Muse's capabilities for both personal and business use.
