## August Surge: Top Portfolios Outperform as Market Shakes Up

August proved to be a dynamic month for investment portfolios, with several promising strategies delivering impressive returns. Multi-cap and flexi-cap approaches, in particular, took center stage, with a notable group of managed portfolio services (PMS) capitalizing on market movements to generate significant gains.

### Leading the Pack: Double-Digit Gains in August

The month saw a strong showing from several PMS strategies, with East Green Advisors' Quant Strategy leading the charge. This multi-cap and flexi-cap approach, benchmarked against the broad BSE 500 TRI, delivered a remarkable 14.73% return, making it the top performer. Close behind was Atlas Integrated Finance's Momentum 20 PMS Fund, also a multi-cap and flexi-cap strategy, which posted a solid 14.46% gain. Notably, Hem Securities' India Rising SME Stars was the sole small-cap strategy to break into the top performers, achieving a 13.08% return by focusing on emerging businesses.

### Navigating the Downturn: Some Strategies See Red

While many portfolios thrived, August also presented challenges for others, with a portion of PMS strategies experiencing negative returns. Amaltas Asset Management's Strategic Opportunities Series 1 saw a decline of 4.23%, while Lakewater Advisors' India Growth shed 5.12% of its value. Thematic strategies also faced headwinds; Omniscience Capital Advisors' Omni Industry Inc lost 3.67%, and its Omni Energy Transition fund declined by 4.50%. Ashima Capital Management's Long Heritage Value Fund experienced the steepest fall, with its value dropping by 7.64% during the month, marking it as the weakest performer among those tracked.

### What To Watch

As September unfolds, investors will be keenly observing whether the momentum seen by top-performing multi-cap and flexi-cap strategies continues. The performance of small-cap focused funds like Hem Securities' India Rising SME Stars will also be a key indicator of broader market sentiment. The strategies that struggled in August will face scrutiny as fund managers aim to navigate the evolving economic landscape and potentially recover losses.
