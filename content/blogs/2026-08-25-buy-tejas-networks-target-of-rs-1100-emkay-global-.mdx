## Tejas Networks Soars on Strong BSNL Progress: Emkay Global Financial Issues Bullish 'Buy' Call

Tejas Networks is capturing the attention of investors following a robust performance, largely driven by its crucial role in India's BSNL 4G project. Financial analysts at Emkay Global Financial have taken notice, issuing a confident "Buy" recommendation and setting an ambitious price target for the company's stock.

### Riding the Wave of Domestic Manufacturing and Digital India

Emkay Global Financial highlights several key factors fueling their optimistic outlook on Tejas Networks. A significant tailwind is the Indian government's strong push for domestic manufacturing, actively supported by initiatives like the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme. This policy environment directly benefits companies like Tejas, encouraging local production and innovation. Furthermore, substantial government investment in major infrastructure projects such as BSNL's 4G rollout, BharatNet broadband expansion, and the Railways' digital transformation are creating a fertile ground for Tejas's growth. The company's ability to secure references from industry giants like TCom and TCS is also a notable achievement, paving the way for new client acquisitions and further market penetration.

### Strong Execution and Favorable Market Trends

The recent performance metrics from Tejas Networks have exceeded expectations, particularly in revenue and profit margins, largely attributed to the efficient execution of the BSNL 4G project. Emkay Global Financial anticipates this momentum to continue, projecting a substantial four-fold increase in FY25 revenue compared to FY24, powered by ongoing BSNL and BharatNet projects. The firm has also revised its revenue forecasts upwards for both FY25 and FY26, reflecting this strong execution. Beyond domestic opportunities, Tejas is also poised to benefit from a global trend of telecom operators diversifying away from Chinese equipment suppliers. This strategic shift presents a significant international growth avenue for the company.

### What to Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring the continued progress and timelines for the BSNL 4G deployment, as well as any new contract wins, both domestically and internationally. The company's ability to maintain its strong margin performance amidst growing demand will also be a key indicator of its sustained profitability. Emkay Global Financial's revised price target of Rs 1100 suggests a potential upside of 21%, indicating a strong belief in the company's future prospects.
