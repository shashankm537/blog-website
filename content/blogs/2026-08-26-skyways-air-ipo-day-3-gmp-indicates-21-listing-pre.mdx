## Skyways Air IPO: Strong Subscription Signals Potential Listing Gains as Bidding Enters Final Day

Investor appetite for Skyways Air Services' initial public offering is running high, with the IPO nearing its close on Day 3. The company's foray into the public market has been met with significant interest, evidenced by robust subscription numbers and a promising grey market premium. This strong performance raises a key question for potential investors: is this an opportunity worth seizing?

### Investor Demand Surges: A Detailed Look

As of the close of Day 2, the Skyways Air Services IPO has been subscribed a notable 2.46 times. This means that for every share offered, nearly two and a half have been bid upon. The demand is particularly striking from retail investors, whose allocated portion saw a subscription rate of 3.54 times. This enthusiastic participation from individual investors often signals confidence in the company's future prospects and its value proposition within its industry.

### Understanding the Grey Market Premium and IPO Details

The grey market premium (GMP) currently suggests a listing at approximately 21% above the IPO's upper price band of Rs 138. While this is an encouraging sign, it's crucial to remember that GMP is an unofficial indicator and can be volatile. The Rs 582.8 crore IPO itself is structured with a fresh issue of shares to raise capital, alongside an offer for sale (OFS) component. This mix allows the company to fund its growth while also providing an exit for some existing shareholders. Ahead of the public offering, Skyways Air Services successfully garnered Rs 174.5 crore from anchor investors, a strong vote of confidence from institutional players.

### What To Watch

With bidding set to conclude on August 27th, all eyes will be on the final subscription figures. The company is expected to finalize IPO allotment on August 28th, with shares slated to debut on the NSE and BSE on September 1st. Investors will be closely monitoring the listing day performance for any signs of sustained investor enthusiasm or profit-taking.
