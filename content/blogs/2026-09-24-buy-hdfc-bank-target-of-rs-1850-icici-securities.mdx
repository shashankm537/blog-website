## HDFC Bank Earns a "Buy" Call from ICICI Securities Amidst Post-Merger Adjustments

Following its significant merger, HDFC Bank is garnering attention from analysts, with ICICI Securities issuing a strong "Buy" recommendation. The investment firm sees potential for the bank's stock to reach ₹1,850, suggesting a belief in its future growth trajectory despite recent market movements.

### Navigating the Post-Merger Landscape

The recent merger has undeniably reshaped HDFC Bank's financial structure, leading to a notable increase in its loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) and a shift in its deposit composition, with a reduced share of low-cost CASA deposits and a rise in borrowings. While the merger has propelled the bank's loan market share significantly, its deposit market share growth has been more measured. ICICI Securities forecasts robust deposit growth, anticipating an 18% compound annual growth rate (CAGR) between FY24 and FY26, which translates to substantial absolute deposit increases annually.

### Strategic Adjustments and Growth Projections

Despite the anticipated deposit surge, HDFC Bank is expected to maintain a strategic balance in its loan growth and borrowing levels. Consequently, ICICI Securities has revised its loan growth estimates downwards to a 13% CAGR for the same period, projecting an easing LDR. This strategic recalibration is a key factor in their outlook, as deposit accretion and the management of net interest margins (NIM) remain critical drivers of the bank's performance.

### An Upgraded Outlook and Target Price

While the Q4FY24 net interest margin (NIM) performance slightly exceeded expectations, it may have been influenced by seasonal factors. Based on revised growth estimates, ICICI Securities has trimmed its earnings per share (EPS) projections for FY25 and FY26 by 5%. Nevertheless, the firm projects healthy return on assets (RoA) of 1.7% and return on equity (RoE) of 14-15% for these fiscal years. Importantly, the target price of ₹1,850 remains unchanged, and with the stock having experienced a notable correction of 10% year-to-date, ICICI Securities has upgraded its rating to "Buy," signaling confidence in the bank's long-term value proposition.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring HDFC Bank's ability to effectively manage its loan and deposit growth in the coming quarters. The pace at which the bank can achieve a more optimal LDR and substitute borrowing costs will be crucial indicators of its future profitability and the realization of the analysts' target price. The market will also be observing how the bank navigates the evolving competitive environment in the banking sector.
