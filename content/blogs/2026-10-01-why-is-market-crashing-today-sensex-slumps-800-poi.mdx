## Dalal Street Sees Red: Rs 8 Lakh Crore Wiped Out as Markets Tumble

Indian stock markets experienced a significant downturn on Thursday, with both the Sensex and Nifty indices suffering substantial losses. Investors found themselves on the back foot as a confluence of negative factors triggered a broad-based selloff, leading to a staggering Rs 8 lakh crore evaporation in market capitalization. This marks the fourth consecutive session of declines, signaling a period of heightened investor caution.

### Foreign Funds Exit, Bond Yields Surge

A primary driver behind Thursday's market crash appears to be the intensifying outflow of funds by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs). These overseas investors offloaded Indian equities worth a substantial Rs 10,148 crore on Wednesday alone, according to preliminary data. This aggressive selling pressure, coupled with a concurrent surge in global bond yields, has made riskier assets like equities less attractive. As bond yields rise, they offer a more appealing alternative for investors seeking safer returns, prompting a shift away from the stock market.

### Rupee Weakens, Auto Sector Takes a Hit

Adding to the market's woes, the Indian Rupee saw a significant depreciation, further dampening investor sentiment. A weaker rupee can negatively impact companies that rely on imports or have significant foreign currency debt. The automotive sector, in particular, bore the brunt of the selloff, with the Nifty Auto index plummeting over 4%. This sharp decline was mirrored in individual stock performances, with major automobile manufacturers experiencing significant drops in their share prices. The broader market also reflected this pessimism, with mid and small-cap indices experiencing steeper declines than their large-cap counterparts.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring the upcoming economic data releases and any further commentary from global central banks regarding interest rate policies. The continued FII selling and the trajectory of bond yields will be key indicators to watch in the coming days. The resilience of the Indian rupee will also play a crucial role in shaping market sentiment.
