## Truecaller Bets on eSIMs to Chart a New Course

In a significant strategic pivot, the company best known for its caller identification services, Truecaller, is venturing into the world of eSIMs, aiming to open up new revenue streams. This move comes as the tech giant seeks to strengthen its financial standing and diversify its business model, particularly in light of declining advertising income.

### Expanding Horizons: Travelers Welcome New Connectivity Option

Truecaller is rolling out its new eSIM product, designed to offer seamless mobile connectivity for travelers. These digital SIM cards will provide various data plans, ranging from a modest 1 GB for a week to a more substantial 20 GB for a month. Initially, the service will be accessible in 29 countries, spanning popular travel destinations across Europe, North America, Oceania, and parts of South America and Africa.

The initial rollout includes key markets like Italy, Sweden, Spain, France, Germany, the United States, and Australia. However, it's noteworthy that India, Truecaller's largest market, is conspicuously absent from the initial list. This is likely due to stringent local telecom regulations, a factor that has previously led to the blocking of similar services from competitors in the region due to concerns about potential misuse.

### Leveraging a Massive User Base for Growth

The company is partnering with Telna, a global cellular connectivity provider, and Telness Tech, a telecom software provider, to power its eSIM platform. While the eSIM market already features established players like Airalo and Holafly, Truecaller believes its existing user base of over 500 million monthly active users gives it a distinct advantage. Chief Operating Officer Fredrik Kjell highlighted that this existing trust and established relationships offer a unique distribution channel, allowing them to acquire new eSIM customers from a familiar and reliable platform. This move is also intended to enhance the app's utility for its existing users, making it a more comprehensive tool.

### What To Watch

Truecaller's foray into eSIMs marks a critical moment for the company, especially following recent job cuts and weaker-than-expected financial results. Investors will be closely monitoring the adoption rate of these new eSIM services and how effectively Truecaller can monetize its vast user network in this competitive landscape. The success of this diversification strategy could set a new trajectory for the company's future growth and profitability.
