## HDFC Bank: ICICI Securities Sees a Bright Future, Downgrades to "Buy"

**Mumbai, India** – In a significant development for the Indian banking sector, ICICI Securities has issued a strong "Buy" recommendation for HDFC Bank, setting a compelling target price of Rs 1,850. The brokerage house's latest research report, released on April 21, 2024, highlights a bullish outlook for the banking giant, even amidst recent market volatility.

### Navigating Post-Merger Dynamics

Following its monumental merger, HDFC Bank has undergone substantial structural shifts. Key among these are a notable increase in its loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) and a decrease in its share of low-cost current and savings accounts (CASA), alongside a rise in borrowing. Despite these changes, the bank has significantly expanded its loan market share by 330 basis points to 14.5%. While its deposit market share has also grown, it has done so at a more measured pace of 110 basis points year-on-year to 11.0%. ICICI Securities anticipates robust deposit growth for HDFC Bank, projecting an 18% compound annual growth rate (CAGR) for the period FY24-26, translating to an absolute rise of INR 4.0-5.0 trillion annually. This growth is expected to accommodate the evolving competitive landscape.

### Growth Projections and Revised Estimates

While the bank is poised for strong deposit accretion, ICICI Securities expects HDFC Bank to maintain a balanced approach to its LDR and borrowing. Consequently, their loan growth estimates have been adjusted downwards to a 13% CAGR for FY24-26, with the LDR projected to ease to 100% by FY25 and 96% by FY26, a decrease from the current 104%. This implies an incremental LDR of around 74-75%. The brokerage emphasizes that deposit growth remains a critical factor influencing both the bank's overall expansion and its net interest margins (NIMs).

### An Upgraded Outlook and Target Price

The Q4FY24 NIM results for HDFC Bank, while slightly exceeding expectations, appear to be influenced by seasonal factors. Based on the revised growth estimates, ICICI Securities has trimmed its Earnings Per Share (EPS) forecast by 5% for both FY25 and FY26. Nevertheless, the bank is still anticipated to achieve a healthy return on assets (RoA) of 1.7% for FY25 and FY26, with a return on equity (RoE) in the range of 14-15%. Crucially, ICICI Securities' target price of INR 1,850 remains unchanged. The upgrade to "Buy" comes in the wake of a significant correction in HDFC Bank's stock price, which has seen a 10% year-to-date decline. The brokerage suggests that a full re-rating of the stock is likely to unfold gradually, as credit growth is expected to remain slightly slower than the system average, and borrowing substitution will be a progressive process.

### What to Watch

Investors will be closely observing HDFC Bank's ability to manage its LDR and attract deposits in a competitive market. The pace of credit growth and the success of its borrowing substitution strategies will be key indicators for future performance and the realization of ICICI Securities' target price.
