## HDFC Bank: A Promising Investment Opportunity? Analysts Issue "Buy" Recommendation

Following a significant merger, HDFC Bank is drawing renewed attention from financial analysts, with one prominent firm believing the stock is poised for a substantial rebound. ICICI Securities has issued a strong "Buy" rating, setting an ambitious price target that signals confidence in the banking giant's future performance.

### Navigating Post-Merger Dynamics

The recent integration has undeniably reshaped HDFC Bank's financial structure. Key metrics like the loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) have seen a notable increase, while the share of low-cost current account savings account (CASA) deposits has decreased, accompanied by a rise in overall borrowings. Crucially, the merger has significantly boosted the bank's market share in loans by 330 basis points, reaching 14.5%, though its deposit market share growth has been more moderate. ICICI Securities anticipates robust deposit growth in the coming years, projecting an 18% compound annual growth rate (CAGR) for FY24-26, which translates to an absolute increase of ₹4-5 trillion annually. Despite this strong deposit accretion, the bank is expected to manage its LDR strategically, with loan growth estimates revised to a 13% CAGR and an anticipated easing of the LDR.

### Analyst Outlook and Revised Estimates

While the recent quarterly net interest margin (NIM) performance exceeded expectations, analysts attribute this partly to seasonal factors. Consequently, ICICI Securities has adjusted its earnings per share (EPS) estimates downwards by 5% for both FY25 and FY26, reflecting the revised growth projections. Nevertheless, the firm maintains its positive outlook, forecasting a healthy return on assets (RoA) of 1.7% and a return on equity (RoE) between 14-15% for FY25-26. The price target of ₹1,850 remains unchanged, and the upgrade to a "Buy" rating is driven by the recent significant correction in the bank's stock price, which has seen a 10% decline year-to-date. Analysts believe that while a re-rating might be gradual due to a more measured credit growth compared to the system and a phased approach to borrowing substitution, the underlying strengths of HDFC Bank are compelling.

### What To Watch

Investors will be keenly observing HDFC Bank's ability to balance its deposit growth with strategic loan expansion and manage its borrowing costs effectively. The bank's success in integrating its operations post-merger and its performance in crucial metrics like NIM and LDR in the upcoming quarters will be key indicators for sustained growth.
