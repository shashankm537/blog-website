## Tejas Networks Soars on Analyst Confidence: Emkay Global Financial Sees Rs 1100 Target

Telecom infrastructure powerhouse Tejas Networks is attracting significant analyst attention, with Emkay Global Financial initiating coverage with a strong "Buy" recommendation and a compelling price target of Rs 1100. This optimistic outlook follows a robust performance driven by key government projects and strategic industry shifts.

### Riding the Wave of Government Initiatives and Project Wins

Emkay Global Financial's bullish stance is largely underpinned by Tejas Networks' crucial role in India's expanding digital infrastructure. The company is reportedly benefiting significantly from the Indian government's focus on boosting domestic manufacturing through initiatives like the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme. Furthermore, substantial investments in projects such as BSNL's 4G network rollout and the BharatNet program are directly fueling Tejas Networks' revenue growth. The report highlights that FY25 revenue is projected to be a remarkable four times that of FY24, a testament to the execution capabilities of the company in these large-scale deployments.

### Strategic Advantages Fueling Future Growth

Beyond immediate project wins, Tejas Networks is strategically positioned for sustained success. The company is leveraging its existing relationships with major players like Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) and Tata Communications (TCom) to onboard new clients, expanding its market reach. Moreover, a global trend towards diversifying away from Chinese telecom equipment manufacturers presents a significant opportunity for Indian companies like Tejas Networks to gain market share. This combination of government backing, strong project execution, and favorable geopolitical shifts paints a promising picture for the company's future.

### What To Watch

Investors will be keenly observing Tejas Networks' progress in fulfilling its order book, particularly the ongoing BSNL 4G and BharatNet projects. The company's ability to continue securing new contracts and expand its global footprint will be key indicators of its long-term trajectory. Analysts will also be scrutinizing margin performance as the company scales its operations.
