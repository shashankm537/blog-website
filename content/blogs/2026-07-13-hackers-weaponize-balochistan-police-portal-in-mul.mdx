## Balochistan Police Portal Hacked in Sophisticated Cyber Espionage Attacks

Sensitive data from the Balochistan Police, along with other Pakistani law enforcement agencies, has been compromised in a series of prolonged cyber espionage campaigns. Researchers have uncovered evidence suggesting that both China- and India-aligned hacking groups have been exploiting vulnerabilities since February of this year, with operations continuing until at least April 2026. The breaches targeted critical systems managing vast amounts of citizen and police information.

### Unmasking the Attackers and Their Targets

The investigation by cybersecurity firm SentinelOne has revealed that the Balochistan Police's web application servers, which house crucial data like criminal records and biometric information, were among the primary targets. These systems are vital for police operations and citizen services, making their compromise a significant security concern. Beyond Balochistan, other law enforcement bodies, including the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Police, Islamabad Police, and the Punjab Safe Cities Authority, have also been impacted by these sophisticated attacks. The attackers focused on network appliances and servers handling a wide array of sensitive data, from hotel registrations to personnel files.

### A Multi-Pronged Attack Strategy

The cybercriminals employed a variety of tools and techniques to achieve their objectives, indicating a well-resourced and coordinated effort. One China-nexus threat actor, for instance, managed to inject a malicious implant disguised as a legitimate software update into a Complaint Management System (CMS). This system, used by both police officers and the public, placed both groups directly in the attackers' crosshairs. The threat landscape revealed at least four distinct threat clusters, each leveraging a different malware family: PlugX, ShadowPad, Cobalt Strike, and Remcos RAT. The use of Remcos RAT is specifically linked to an India-nexus actor, while PlugX and ShadowPad, often associated with Chinese state-sponsored groups, suggest a broader involvement.

### What To Watch

The ongoing nature of these attacks, with operations spanning over two years, underscores the persistent threat to national security infrastructure. The diversity of the malware families and the involvement of actors with suspected ties to both China and India highlight a complex geopolitical dimension to these cyber operations. Attention will now turn to how effectively these agencies can bolster their defenses and whether any specific data exfiltration or malicious intent beyond intelligence gathering will be uncovered.
