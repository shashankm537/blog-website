## Thinking Machines' Mira Murati Re-Emerges with a Glimpse of AI's Future

After a significant period operating away from the public eye, Mira Murati, the visionary behind Thinking Machines Lab, has re-entered the spotlight. Her recent interview offered a carefully curated look at the company's ambitious plans, hinting at a new paradigm for human-AI interaction.

### A New Dawn for AI Interfaces

Murati, previously known for her pivotal role as CTO at OpenAI, has been notably elusive since founding Thinking Machines. The company has been diligently working behind the scenes, securing funding, expanding its research team, and releasing its initial product, Tinker, an API designed for customizing open-source AI models. This period of focused development has now culminated in a strategic re-engagement with the broader tech landscape.

The timing of Murati's reappearance is particularly noteworthy, occurring amidst intense competition from AI giants like OpenAI and Anthropic, as well as the high-profile emergence of Elon Musk's xAI. In this crowded and fast-paced environment, a period of quiet innovation needs to be complemented by a clear statement of intent to maintain relevance and attract continued interest.

### Beyond the Prompt: Towards Real-Time Interaction

The core of Murati's revelation centers on what Thinking Machines is terming "interaction models." These are designed to move beyond the familiar, turn-based chat interface that characterizes most current AI applications. Instead, these new models are built to process continuous streams of audio, text, and video data, analyzing them in incredibly short, 200-millisecond intervals.

This approach aims to capture the nuanced, fluid nature of human conversation, including interruptions, self-corrections, and even thoughtful pauses, all in a manner that feels far more immediate and natural. While Murati emphasized that these are early-stage developments rather than fully realized products, the ambition to create AI that truly understands and responds to the flow of human communication is clear.

### What To Watch

Expect Thinking Machines to gradually reveal more details about the capabilities and potential applications of these interaction models. The tech world will be keen to see how these new interfaces perform in practice and whether they can indeed offer a more seamless and intuitive way to engage with artificial intelligence, potentially setting a new benchmark for the industry.
