### WordPress Titan Reclaims CEO Throne After Boardroom Battle

A dramatic week for Automattic, the company behind WordPress.com, has culminated in a swift reversal. After an apparent board-led attempt to oust founder Matt Mullenweg from his CEO role, the company has officially confirmed his return, signaling a turbulent period has reached a resolution.

### A Tumultuous Week Unfolds

Automattic's internal drama became public knowledge earlier this week when reports surfaced of Mullenweg being placed on a paid leave of absence, a move described as a surprise by the company's founder. In a surprising turn of events, Mullenweg reportedly pushed back against the board's decision, allegedly locking out administrators from the company's internal Slack channels and asserting his continued control. This internal conflict was initially confirmed by Automattic, with the CFO stepping in as interim CEO.

However, the situation escalated rapidly as Mullenweg seemingly refused to step aside, leading to internal upheaval. Accounts from employees suggest a rapid shift in power dynamics following the initial board vote. Mullenweg himself hinted at his regained authority through cryptic messages within the company's communication channels, even adopting the moniker of a "pirate."

### Board Reaffirms Mullenweg's Leadership

In a statement released Saturday evening, Automattic officially confirmed Matt Mullenweg's position as both Chairman and CEO, emphasizing his full support from the board. This official confirmation appears to quash any lingering doubts about his leadership, with the company spokesperson highlighting widespread support from executives and Automatticians online. The swiftness of this public confirmation suggests an effort to restore stability and confidence following the internal discord.

### What To Watch

The immediate focus will be on Automattic's internal communications and how this situation is being presented to employees. Investors and the wider tech community will also be watching for any further statements that might shed light on the reasons behind the attempted ouster and the subsequent resolution. The company's ability to move forward cohesively will be key.
