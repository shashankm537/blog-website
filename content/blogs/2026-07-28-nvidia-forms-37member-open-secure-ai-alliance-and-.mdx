## A United Front for AI Security: NVIDIA Launches Major Alliance and Open-Source Initiative

In a significant move for the future of artificial intelligence, NVIDIA has partnered with 36 other industry leaders to create a new alliance focused on securing AI technologies. This collaboration aims to foster open development and sharing of vital tools and techniques, a crucial step in building trust and safety around increasingly sophisticated AI systems.

### The Power of Collaboration: 37 Organizations Unite for AI Safety

The newly formed Open Secure AI Alliance brings together a formidable group of companies, spanning the critical sectors of cloud computing, cybersecurity, enterprise software, and artificial intelligence. Notable members include tech giants like Microsoft, Cisco, Cloudflare, and IBM, alongside prominent AI players such as Hugging Face and CrowdStrike, and open-source advocates like Red Hat and the Linux Foundation. Their collective mission is to develop and share open technologies that address the full spectrum of AI agent security, from identity and permissions to robust guardrails and secure coding practices. The core idea is to empower defenders with AI models they can fully understand, modify, and run independently, rather than relying solely on closed systems accessed through vendor-specific interfaces.

### Introducing NOOA: A Foundation for Auditable AI Agents

Accompanying the alliance's launch is NVIDIA's first major technical contribution: the NVIDIA-labs OO Agents (NOOA) framework. This open-source initiative, released under the permissive Apache 2.0 license, is designed to simplify the process of testing, tracing, auditing, and governing the behavior of AI agents. NOOA provides a structured way for developers to build the "agent harness" – the essential software layer that surrounds an AI model, managing its context, executing actions, and tracking its progress. Within NOOA, this harness is represented as a Python class, utilizing familiar programming constructs like methods and docstrings to define agent capabilities and prompts. This approach promises to make AI agent development more transparent and controllable, allowing for deterministic code alongside LLM-driven loops for more dynamic functionalities.

### What to Watch

While the alliance's charter and governance details are still being finalized, the formation of this broad coalition signals a strong industry commitment to open and secure AI. The initial release of the NOOA framework provides a concrete starting point for developers to explore and contribute to the effort. Observers will be keen to see how the alliance progresses in establishing clear workstreams, delivery schedules, and a shared repository for its collective efforts, and how NVIDIA responds to requests for further detail on its governance and member commitments.
