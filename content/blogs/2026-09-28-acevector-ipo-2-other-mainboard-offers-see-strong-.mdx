## IPO Frenzy Continues: Three Mainboard Issues See Robust Demand on Day Two

Investors are showing strong appetite for three mainboard Initial Public Offerings (IPOs) that entered their second day of bidding. AceVector (Snapdeal), Runwal Enterprises, and German Green Steel & Power are all experiencing significant interest, signaling positive sentiment in the primary market.

### A Closer Look at the Contenders

**AceVector**, the parent company behind the e-commerce platform Snapdeal, is attracting attention with a modest grey market premium (GMP) of around 3.12%. This translates to a small potential uplift of Rs 1 over its issue price on listing. The IPO is a combination of a fresh issue aiming to raise Rs 287 crore and an offer for sale (OFS) contributing another Rs 133 crore. By midday, AceVector's offering had seen 37% subscription, with retail investors actively participating, having subscribed nearly 97% of their allocated portion. Non-institutional investors also showed good interest, subscribing 68% of their quota.

Meanwhile, **Runwal Enterprises** is also on investors' radar, boasting a GMP of approximately 4%. This suggests expectations of a slightly higher return upon listing. This IPO is a purely fresh issue, raising Rs 499.83 crore without any OFS component. Similar to AceVector, Runwal's issue also achieved 37% subscription by midday. Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs) are leading the charge here, having subscribed a substantial 96% of their reserved shares, while retail investors have committed 30% of their allocation.

**German Green Steel & Power** stands out with the most significant buzz, commanding an impressive 21% GMP in the grey market. This indicates strong confidence in its potential listing performance. The company's Rs 303.90 crore IPO is predominantly a fresh issue of Rs 290 crore, with a minor OFS of Rs 13.90 crore.

### Why the Demand?

The robust demand across these IPOs can be attributed to several factors. Investors are actively seeking opportunities in the primary market, especially for companies with clear business models and growth potential. The positive sentiment surrounding Snapdeal's associated company, AceVector, and the promising GMP for German Green Steel & Power are likely drawing in both retail and institutional investors. The strong participation from retail investors in AceVector and QIBs in Runwal Enterprises highlights a diversified investor base showing faith in these offerings.

### What to Watch

As these IPOs move towards their closing dates, attention will be on the final subscription numbers and the performance of their grey market premiums. The listing day performance of AceVector, Runwal Enterprises, and particularly German Green Steel & Power will be keenly observed, offering insights into current investor sentiment and the success of these debut offerings.
