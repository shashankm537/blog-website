## HDFC Bank: A Buy Signal from ICICI Securities Amidst Post-Merger Shifts

Following a period of significant change after its recent merger, HDFC Bank is now being eyed with optimism by ICICI Securities. The financial services firm has issued a "Buy" recommendation for the banking giant, setting an ambitious price target of Rs 1,850. This move comes as analysts assess the bank's evolving financial landscape and future growth prospects.

### Navigating the New Balance Sheet

The merger has undeniably reshaped HDFC Bank's financial structure. Analysts note a marked increase in the loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) and a decrease in the share of low-cost current account savings account (CASA) deposits, alongside a rise in overall borrowing. While the merger significantly boosted the bank's loan market share, its deposit growth, though solid, has lagged behind. ICICI Securities anticipates robust deposit growth in the coming years, estimating an 18% compound annual growth rate (CAGR) for FY24-26, which translates to substantial absolute gains in deposits annually.

### Adjusted Growth Projections and Investment Thesis

Despite strong deposit accretion, HDFC Bank is expected to prioritize a balanced LDR and manage its borrowing levels. Consequently, ICICI Securities has revised its loan growth estimates downwards to a 13% CAGR for FY24-26, anticipating a gradual easing of the LDR. This strategic focus on balance sheet management means that deposit growth will remain a critical factor influencing the bank's overall expansion and net interest margins (NIMs). The firm's research suggests that the bank's return on assets (RoA) is projected to be healthy, around 1.7% for FY25-26, with return on equity (RoE) in the 14-15% range.

### What To Watch

With the stock price having seen a recent correction, ICICI Securities believes this presents an opportune moment for investors. The firm anticipates a gradual re-rating of the stock as the market digests the bank's strategic adjustments. Investors will be closely monitoring HDFC Bank's ability to sustain strong deposit growth while effectively managing its loan book and borrowing costs in the evolving competitive environment. The effectiveness of its strategies to rebalance its balance sheet post-merger will be key to its future performance.
