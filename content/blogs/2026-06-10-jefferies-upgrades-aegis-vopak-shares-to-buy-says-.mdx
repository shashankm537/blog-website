## Aegis Vopak Shares Get a Boost as Jefferies Sees Green Shoots After Correction

Investors in Aegis Vopak Terminals are breathing a sigh of relief today, as the stock saw a significant rally following a bullish upgrade from international brokerage firm Jefferies. The firm believes the recent sell-off in the company's shares has gone too far, presenting a compelling opportunity for growth.

### Analysts Bullish on Value Despite Geopolitical Headwinds

Jefferies has upgraded Aegis Vopak Terminals shares to a "Buy" rating, setting a new target price of Rs 240. This suggests an attractive 25% upside from the current trading levels, even after a slight reduction from their previous Rs 255 target. The brokerage points out that Aegis Vopak has been disproportionately hit by recent Middle East tensions, dropping 16% while the broader Nifty index saw a more modest 8% decline. While acknowledging the lingering near-term risks, Jefferies anticipates a normalization of Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG) import volumes as geopolitical tensions subside.

### Ambitious Expansion Plans Signal Strong Future Growth

Beyond the current market fluctuations, Jefferies highlights Aegis Vopak's robust expansion strategy as a key driver for future value. The company is firmly on track with its ambitious plans, with management reaffirming significant capital expenditure targets of $5 billion by FY30 and $1.2 billion by FY27. Aegis Vopak is looking to more than double its port presence from the current six to twelve by 2030, significantly increasing its liquid storage capacity by 25% and LPG capacity by 34% through additions at JNPT and Kandla ports. Furthermore, the company is strategically diversifying into ammonia storage, with a substantial facility under development, and the upcoming Kandla-Gorakhpur pipeline is poised to boost LPG terminal throughput upon its commissioning.

### Positioned for Demand-Driven Storage Growth

Jefferies forecasts a healthy 4.7% compound annual growth rate (CAGR) in LPG demand between FY26 and FY30, which is expected to translate into a 5.3% CAGR in imports. This growing demand, coupled with the Indian government's initiative to build a 30-day LPG storage reserve, positions Aegis Vopak Terminals to capitalize on storage-led growth. The brokerage's confidence in the company's strategic positioning and expansion efforts underpins their upgraded outlook.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring the ongoing capacity expansions at key ports and the progress of the Kandla-Gorakhpur pipeline. The easing of geopolitical tensions in the Middle East will also be a crucial factor influencing LPG import volumes. Furthermore, updates on the development of the ammonia storage facility and the government's strategic LPG reserve plans will be key indicators of Aegis Vopak's future trajectory.
