## Australian Founders: The Clock Is Ticking for a Shot at Global Stardom

The opportunity of a lifetime is rapidly approaching its deadline. With just 48 hours remaining, Australian entrepreneurs have a final chance to apply for a prestigious pitch competition that could catapult their startup onto the world stage. This unique collaboration between Stripe and TechCrunch's Startup Battlefield offers a golden ticket to San Francisco's renowned TechCrunch Disrupt.

### A Rare Opportunity to Shine on the Global Stage

On August 19th, eight handpicked Australian startups will have the incredible chance to present their innovations to a captivated audience of investors, international media, and the vibrant local tech scene. The ultimate prize? Automatic entry into TechCrunch Disrupt in San Francisco, a coveted spot on the world's most iconic platform for emerging companies. This isn't just about winning; it's about securing a direct path to global recognition and potential investment.

This partnership marks the first time Stripe has joined forces with TechCrunch's flagship pitch competition, bringing its storied legacy of launching game-changing companies like Dropbox and Discord right here to Sydney. The stakes are high, with significant Stripe credits for the top three winners and the grand prize of a guaranteed spot at TechCrunch Disrupt in October 2026. Even those not selected to pitch will be invited to attend the broader Stripe Tour Sydney event, offering invaluable networking and learning opportunities.

### What the Judges Are Looking For: Innovation Over Polish

Forget needing a perfectly polished presentation; the organizers are actively seeking disruptive ideas. The core question they’re asking is whether a startup’s concept genuinely changes things, not just incrementally, but profoundly. This means that early-stage companies with a strong vision and a functional minimum viable product (MVP) are encouraged to apply, even if they haven't generated revenue or secured extensive press. Past applications or some local media coverage will not hinder your chances; the focus is squarely on the potential and impact of your innovation.

### What To Watch

With applications closing imminently, the focus will soon shift to the selection process. Expect an announcement of the eight chosen startups in the coming days. Following the Sydney event, the tech community will be eager to see which Australian ventures have earned their chance to compete on the international stage at TechCrunch Disrupt San Francisco.
