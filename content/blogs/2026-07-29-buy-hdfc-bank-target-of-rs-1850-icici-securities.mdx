## HDFC Bank: Analyst Sees Upside Ahead Despite Merger Adjustments

ICICI Securities has initiated a "Buy" recommendation for HDFC Bank shares, setting an ambitious target price of Rs 1,850. This optimistic outlook comes as the financial giant navigates the complexities and opportunities arising from its recent merger.

### Navigating Post-Merger Balance Sheet Dynamics

The integration of entities has led to significant shifts in HDFC Bank's financial structure. We're observing a notable increase in the loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) and a decrease in the share of low-cost current account savings account (CASA) deposits. Concurrently, the reliance on borrowing has seen a substantial rise. Despite these adjustments, the merger has significantly boosted the bank's loan market share, adding 330 basis points to reach 14.5%. Deposit growth, while positive, has been at a slower pace, with market share increasing by 110 basis points year-on-year to 11.0%.

ICICI Securities anticipates robust deposit growth, estimating an 18% compound annual growth rate (CAGR) for FY24-26, translating to an annual absolute increase of Rs 4.0-5.0 trillion. Even with this strong deposit accretion, the bank is expected to prioritize a balanced LDR and reduce its reliance on borrowings. Consequently, loan growth projections have been revised downwards to a 13% CAGR for the same period, with the LDR expected to ease to 100% and 96% by FY25 and FY26, respectively, from the current 104%.

### Outlook and Valuation

While the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2024 saw a slightly better-than-expected net interest margin (NIM), it appears to have been influenced by seasonal factors. Due to the adjusted growth estimates, ICICI Securities has trimmed its earnings per share (EPS) forecast by 5% for both FY25 and FY26. Nevertheless, the firm projects a healthy return on assets (RoA) of 1.7% and a return on equity (RoE) of 14-15% for FY25/26. The target price of Rs 1,850 remains unchanged, supported by the recent 10% correction in the stock price year-to-date, prompting the upgrade to a "Buy" rating. The analyst community anticipates a gradual re-rating, acknowledging that while deposit growth is strong, credit expansion might trail the broader system, and the shift away from borrowings will be a measured process.

### What to Watch

Investors will be keenly observing HDFC Bank's ability to manage its LDR effectively and how quickly it can substitute borrowings with more stable deposit funding. Future NIM trends and the pace of loan growth relative to system growth will be key indicators of the bank's performance post-merger.
