## AI Shakes Up Data Engineering: From Ontologies to Automated Pipelines

The world of data engineering is undergoing a dramatic shift, driven by the rapid advancements in Artificial Intelligence. What once seemed like niche concepts like ontologies and knowledge graphs are now front and center, while the reign of the "modern data stack" appears to be drawing to a close. This evolution is being shaped by new AI-powered tools and a re-evaluation of how we manage and utilize vast amounts of data.

### The Resurgence of Ontologies in the Age of AI

Ontologies and knowledge graphs, once academic curiosities, are experiencing a renaissance thanks to the capabilities of modern AI. While these technologies have existed for some time, their true business and technical value is only now becoming crystal clear with the advent of AI agents. The sheer scale and speed at which businesses now operate, coupled with an explosion in data volume, far exceed what we saw even a decade ago. While standards like RDF, OWL2, and SHACL offer robust frameworks, the critical question is their practical applicability in today's fast-paced business environment, a question AI is helping to answer.

### The "Modern Data Stack" Era Draws to a Close

The era of the "modern data stack" as we knew it may officially be over, with even data catalogs evolving into context engines. PostHog aptly captures the current sentiment, highlighting how many users are finding themselves bundled into integrated solutions, whether by design or by default. This shift signifies a move towards more cohesive and intelligent data platforms, moving beyond the assembly of individual tools that characterized the previous era.

### GoDaddy Pioneers AI for Data Pipeline Automation

GoDaddy offers a compelling glimpse into the future by detailing their journey in building an AI agent specifically for data pipeline creation. Previously, setting up data pipelines was a time-consuming process involving repetitive manual tasks and meticulous adherence to internal naming conventions. Their AI agent now streamlines this by proactively identifying missing information, analyzing source data, and automatically generating pipeline files along with pull requests. This initiative underscores a crucial lesson: existing templates and established standards can serve as a powerful foundation for AI agents to automate routine and often tedious engineering work.

### What To Watch

The trends highlighted suggest a future where AI agents become indispensable partners in data engineering, automating complex tasks and unlocking new efficiencies. Expect to see continued innovation in AI-driven data pipeline creation and a growing integration of knowledge graphs into core data strategies. The Open Table format is also poised for significant growth, with advancements catering to a wider range of workloads and real-time data needs.
