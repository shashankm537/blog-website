## Honasa Consumer Stock Surges on Strong Q4 Performance, Brokerages Bullish

Honasa Consumer's shares have experienced a significant uplift, climbing over 10% following the company's impressive financial results for the fourth quarter of FY26. This surge reflects renewed investor confidence, with several prominent brokerages issuing strong buy ratings and setting ambitious price targets.

### A Robust Q4 and Promising Growth Trajectory

The company has demonstrated a remarkable turnaround after a period of distribution adjustments. Honasa Consumer reported a staggering 177% year-on-year increase in net profit, reaching Rs 69 crore for the quarter, a substantial jump from Rs 25 crore in the previous year. Revenue also saw healthy growth, climbing over 23% to Rs 657 crore compared to Rs 533 crore in the same quarter last year. This financial strength underscores the company's ability to drive sales and profitability effectively.

### Brokerage Houses Eyeing Significant Upside

International brokerage Jefferies has been particularly optimistic, initiating coverage with a target price that implies a potential 57% rally for Honasa shares. They highlighted the company's consistent growth across all sales channels, with key categories performing exceptionally well in e-commerce and modern trade. Furthermore, Honasa's expanding reach in modern retail, now exceeding 10,000 stores, coupled with rapid expansion in general trade, points to a well-executed distribution strategy.

CLSA and two other brokerages also expressed positive sentiment, recognizing Honasa's strategic focus on innovation and product development. The company's efforts to optimize advertising spend and improve operational efficiency are expected to lead to margin expansion in the coming quarters. Honasa has also proactively addressed raw material cost pressures through price adjustments, ensuring a stable outlook for the immediate future.

### What to Watch

Investors will be keenly observing Honasa Consumer's ability to maintain this strong growth momentum in subsequent quarters. The continued expansion of its distribution network, particularly in the general trade segment, will be a key factor to monitor. Additionally, the success of its product innovation pipeline and the impact of margin improvement initiatives will be crucial for sustaining the positive investor sentiment.
