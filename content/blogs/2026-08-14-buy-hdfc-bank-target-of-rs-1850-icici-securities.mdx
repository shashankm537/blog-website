## HDFC Bank: A Promising Buy Signal Emerges from ICICI Securities

Following recent market fluctuations, ICICI Securities has issued a strong "Buy" recommendation for HDFC Bank, setting an ambitious target price of ₹1,850. This optimistic outlook stems from a detailed analysis of the bank's post-merger landscape and its strategic adjustments.

### Navigating the Post-Merger Landscape

The integration of HDFC Bank with its parent entity has indeed reshaped its financial structure. We're seeing a noticeable shift towards a higher loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) and a reduced proportion of low-cost current account and savings account (CASA) deposits, alongside an increased reliance on borrowings. However, this merger has also significantly boosted the bank's loan market share, adding an impressive 330 basis points to reach 14.5%. While the growth in deposits has been more measured, ICICI Securities anticipates a robust 18% compound annual growth rate (CAGR) for deposits between FY24 and FY26, projecting an absolute annual increase of ₹4 to ₹5 trillion. This growth is expected to help the bank balance its LDR and manage its borrowing mix effectively.

### Growth Projections and Re-rating Potential

While the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2024 saw a slightly better-than-expected net interest margin (NIM), it appears to be influenced by seasonal factors. Consequently, ICICI Securities has adjusted its earnings per share (EPS) estimates downwards by 5% for both FY25 and FY26, primarily due to revised growth expectations. Despite these adjustments, the bank is projected to achieve a healthy return on assets (RoA) of 1.7% for FY25 and FY26, coupled with a return on equity (RoE) of 14-15%. The current dip in HDFC Bank's stock price, down approximately 10% year-to-date, has prompted the upgrade to a "Buy" rating. The path to realizing the target price of ₹1,850 is expected to be a gradual one, as the bank navigates slower credit growth compared to the broader market and manages its borrowing substitution strategy.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring HDFC Bank's ability to execute its deposit growth strategy while effectively managing its loan book and borrowing costs. The evolution of its LDR and NIM in the coming quarters will be crucial indicators of its performance. Furthermore, the market's reaction to the bank's continued integration efforts and its competitive positioning within the evolving financial sector will shape its stock's trajectory.
