## Bajaj Finance Stock: Analysts Eye Significant Upside with Rs 9,000 Target

Financial services powerhouse Bajaj Finance is back in the spotlight as Emkay Global Financial has issued a strong "Buy" recommendation, projecting a significant 23.4% jump in its stock price to Rs 9,000. This optimistic outlook comes as the firm believes current market sentiment has created an attractive entry point for investors.

### Strategic Execution and Navigating Headwinds

Emkay Global Financial highlights Bajaj Finance's exceptional ability to execute its strategic plans as a core strength, noting that its operational prowess remains as robust as ever. The research report acknowledges recent challenges, including regulatory actions concerning EMI and e-commerce cards, as well as ongoing management succession and the upcoming listing of its housing subsidiary. However, Emkay anticipates that resolutions for these concerns are likely to emerge within the next year, mitigating their long-term impact.

### A Clear Path to Growth: Invent, Innovate, Imitate

The brokerage firm's confidence is further bolstered by Bajaj Finance's consistent application of its "Invent, Innovate, and Imitate" strategy. This approach is expected to drive substantial growth, with Emkay forecasting a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of approximately 25% for Assets Under Management (AUM) and a 21.4% CAGR for consolidated profit after tax (PAT) between fiscal years 2024 and 2027. Despite a period of underperformance, the company's ongoing progress with its 'Long Range Strategy' (LRS 2028), evidenced by its expanding customer base and AUM, suggests a compelling investment case.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring how Bajaj Finance addresses the identified near-term challenges, particularly regulatory developments and the successful integration of its housing finance arm. The company's ability to maintain its robust AUM and PAT growth trajectories in the coming quarters will be crucial for validating Emkay's bullish outlook and achieving the Rs 9,000 target price.
