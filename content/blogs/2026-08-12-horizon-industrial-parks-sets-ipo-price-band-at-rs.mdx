## Horizon Industrial Parks Gears Up for Rs 2,600 Crore IPO: Price Band Set, Key Dates Revealed

Horizon Industrial Parks is set to enter the public market with a significant Rs 2,600 crore Initial Public Offering (IPO), having officially announced its price band. This move marks a crucial step for the Blackstone-backed entity as it aims to raise substantial capital through the sale of new equity shares. Investors will be able to gauge their interest and participate in the offering within a defined price range, with specific dates now in place for both anchor and general investors.

### Unpacking the IPO Details: Price, Dates, and Allocation

The company has officially set its IPO price band at Rs 57 to Rs 60 per share. This offers potential investors a clear range for valuation and investment decisions. The subscription window for the public will open on August 17 and close on August 19, allowing a focused period for participation. Ahead of this, anchor investors will have the opportunity to place their bids starting August 14. Retail investors, who can subscribe to a minimum of one lot comprising 250 equity shares, will need to invest approximately Rs 15,000 at the upper end of the price band for a single lot. The IPO structure sees a significant portion, 75%, allocated to Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs), with 15% reserved for Non-Institutional Investors (NIIs) and 10% for retail investors, reflecting a strategy to attract a broad base of institutional support.

### Strategic Capital Deployment: Debt Reduction Takes Center Stage

A substantial portion of the funds raised through this IPO, amounting to Rs 2,250 crore out of the total Rs 2,600 crore, is earmarked for repaying or prepaying existing debt. As of March 31, 2026, Horizon Industrial Parks reported total borrowings of Rs 6,884.34 crore, making debt reduction a clear strategic priority. This focus on deleveraging signals a move towards strengthening the company's financial foundation. It's worth noting that prior to this public offering, the company successfully raised Rs 1,650 crore through a pre-IPO primary fundraise, indicating strong backing from its major shareholder, Blackstone, which currently holds an 89% stake, expected to dilute post-IPO.

### Business Snapshot: A Vast Warehousing and Industrial Footprint

Horizon Industrial Parks operates as a developer and operator of a diverse range of industrial and logistics facilities, including warehouses, fulfilment centres, and in-city logistics hubs. The company boasts a considerable portfolio of 45 assets spanning across 10 cities, covering an impressive 58.58 million square feet. Their business model primarily revolves around leasing these facilities to clients, often secured by multi-year contracts, providing a stable revenue stream. Including its stake in Vision Softech Facilities Pvt Ltd, their total network expands to 46 assets.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely observing the subscription levels across different investor categories, particularly the QIB allocation, to gauge institutional demand. The company's ability to effectively utilize the IPO proceeds for debt reduction and its future expansion plans will be key factors to monitor post-listing. Market sentiment towards industrial and logistics real estate will also play a significant role in the stock's performance.
