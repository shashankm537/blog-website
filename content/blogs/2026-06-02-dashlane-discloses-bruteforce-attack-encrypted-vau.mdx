# Dashlane Breach: Encrypted Data from a Handful of Users Compromised in Brute-Force Attack

Password manager Dashlane has revealed a security incident where a brute-force attack led to the download of encrypted user vaults for fewer than 20 individuals on its personal plan. While the company emphasizes that the compromised data remains secure without the master password, the breach highlights ongoing cybersecurity threats.

## The Attack Unfolds

The incident began on May 31st when an external attacker targeted specific Dashlane accounts. The primary objective was to bypass two-factor authentication (2FA) and add new devices to existing user profiles. Dashlane's security systems detected the unusually high volume of login attempts, which automatically triggered temporary account suspensions and authentication disruptions.

These security measures, while effective in halting the broader attack, unfortunately did not prevent the attackers from succeeding in a small number of cases. In these instances, the threat actor managed to obtain copies of the encrypted data vaults belonging to less than 20 personal plan subscribers. Dashlane has directly contacted all affected users to inform them of the situation.

## What Does This Mean for Your Data?

It is crucial to understand that even though these encrypted vaults were downloaded, they are not readily accessible. Dashlane's security architecture ensures that the vault data can only be unlocked with the user's unique Master Password. If this password is not a simple or easily guessable combination, the attackers will face significant difficulty in deciphering the contents. Furthermore, Dashlane has confirmed that its own internal systems were not breached during this event.

For the vast majority of Dashlane users, if you have not received a direct communication from Dashlane about vault risk, your account remains unaffected. The company's proactive security protocols and the inherent strength of its encryption are designed to protect user information.

## What To Watch

Dashlane is advising all users to take proactive steps to bolster their account security. This includes reviewing the list of registered devices associated with their account and removing any that are unrecognized. Enabling two-factor authentication is strongly recommended, as is the use of a robust Master Password that is long, unique, and difficult for attackers to guess. Users should remain vigilant and monitor for any suspicious activity related to their online accounts.
