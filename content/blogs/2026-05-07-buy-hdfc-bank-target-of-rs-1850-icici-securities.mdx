## HDFC Bank: Analyst Sees Upside Despite Post-Merger Adjustments

Leading financial services firm ICICI Securities has issued a strong "Buy" recommendation for HDFC Bank, setting an ambitious price target of ₹1,850 per share. This optimism comes as the bank navigates significant structural shifts following its recent merger, with analysts believing the current stock correction presents a compelling entry point.

### Navigating the Merger Landscape

The integration of HDFC into HDFC Bank has naturally led to notable changes in the bank's balance sheet. Key among these are an elevated loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) and a reduced share of low-cost current account savings account (CASA) deposits, offset by a rise in borrowing. While the merger has substantially boosted the bank's loan market share, its deposit market share growth has been more measured. ICICI Securities projects robust deposit growth in the coming years, estimating an 18% compound annual growth rate (CAGR) for fiscal years 2024-2026, which translates to an absolute increase of ₹4-5 trillion annually.

### Growth Projections and Revised Estimates

Despite strong deposit accretion, the bank is expected to prioritize a balanced LDR and manage its borrowing mix. Consequently, ICICI Securities has tempered its loan growth forecast to a 13% CAGR for FY24-26, anticipating a gradual easing of the LDR to around 100% or below by FY26. This revised outlook, which factors in the competitive environment and the bank's strategic adjustments, led to a slight 5% reduction in earnings per share (EPS) estimates for FY25 and FY26. However, the firm maintains a positive long-term view, forecasting a healthy return on assets (RoA) of 1.7% and a return on equity (RoE) of 14-15% for the same period, with the target price remaining firm at ₹1,850.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring HDFC Bank's ability to manage its LDR and optimize its borrowing costs in the wake of the merger. The pace of deposit growth and the bank's success in balancing credit expansion with funding will be critical determinants of its future profitability and the realization of the analyst's price target. The market's reaction to the gradual re-rating of the stock, as predicted by ICICI Securities, will also be a key indicator.
