## ICICI Securities Sees Major Upside for HDFC Bank, Recommends 'Buy'

HDFC Bank is back in the spotlight as ICICI Securities issues a strong "Buy" recommendation, coupled with an ambitious price target of Rs 1,850. This endorsement comes after a thorough analysis of the bank's post-merger landscape and its strategic positioning for future growth. Investors are taking note as analysts identify significant potential in one of India's largest private lenders.

### Navigating the Post-Merger Landscape

Following its significant merger, HDFC Bank has undergone substantial structural adjustments to its balance sheet. Key changes include a notable increase in the loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) and a decrease in the share of low-cost current and savings accounts (CASA). The integration has dramatically expanded the bank's loan market share, giving it a significant edge. While deposit growth is also on an upward trajectory, it has not kept pace with the surge in loan market share.

ICICI Securities projects robust deposit growth for HDFC Bank over the next two fiscal years, anticipating an annual increase of INR 4.0-5.0 trillion. Despite this strong inflow, the bank is expected to maintain a balanced approach between its lending activities and borrowing. This strategy will likely lead to a moderation in loan growth estimates and an easing of the LDR, bringing it closer to a more sustainable equilibrium.

### Growth Projections and Valuation

While the recent Net Interest Margin (NIM) figures for the fourth quarter of FY24 showed some positive signs, they might be partly influenced by seasonal factors. Consequently, ICICI Securities has revised its earnings per share (EPS) estimates downwards for FY25 and FY26, reflecting the recalibrated growth expectations. Nevertheless, the outlook remains bright, with projections for a healthy Return on Assets (RoA) of 1.7% and a Return on Equity (RoE) between 14-15% for the same period. The firm's confidence is further underscored by maintaining the price target of Rs 1,850, even as the stock has experienced a recent correction. This upgrade to "Buy" reflects the attractive valuation following the stock's decline.

### What To Watch

Investors will be keenly observing HDFC Bank's ability to balance its credit growth with deposit accretion, a crucial factor for sustained NIM expansion. The pace of borrowing substitution and how effectively the bank navigates the evolving competitive environment will also be key performance indicators to monitor. The market will also be looking for signs of gradual re-rating as the bank executes its strategy and delivers on its growth promises.
