## Data Engineering's Next Frontier: Intent, AI, and Evolving Skills

The world of data engineering is undergoing a dramatic transformation, moving beyond rigid workflows to a more intelligent, intent-driven future. This shift is reshaping how we build and manage data systems, demanding new skills and presenting exciting new challenges for professionals in the field.

### The Rise of Intent-Based Systems

Gone are the days when data engineering was solely about meticulously defining every step of a process. As Katelyn Lesse points out, the focus is shifting from *how* to execute a task to *what* needs to be achieved. This "intent-based" approach simplifies complex operations by allowing systems to understand the desired outcome and figure out the best way to get there. This paradigm change brings a wave of new considerations, including robust infrastructure management, secure identity protocols, comprehensive observability, and ensuring strict security and isolation for data. Traditional coding exercises and bootcamps may soon be insufficient to prepare aspiring data engineers for this evolving landscape.

### Navigating the AI Integration

The integration of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning into production environments is no longer a distant dream but a present reality. InfoQ's 2026 trends report highlights the critical need for reliable AI agents and the infrastructure to support them. This includes not only evaluating AI models but also implementing them alongside established data management practices like data contracts and open table formats. The report emphasizes the growing importance of dedicated infrastructure, effective context management, and rigorous testing to ensure AI systems function dependably in real-world applications.

### Insights from Industry Leaders

To gain a clearer picture of these shifts, an upcoming virtual summit, NEXT QUERY 2026, will feature engineers from tech giants like Apple, NVIDIA, Coinbase, and Microsoft AI. They will share invaluable lessons learned from running complex systems today and their strategies for anticipating future needs. Discussions will delve into production best practices from companies like Coinbase and AppLovin, alongside a deep dive into how Intuit Mailchimp successfully re-architected its real-time customer data platform.

### What To Watch

The coming months will likely see a greater emphasis on tools and methodologies that support intent-based architectures and the reliable deployment of AI in production. Expect to see continued evolution in areas like data observability, AI agent development frameworks, and the standardization of data management practices for AI workloads. The NEXT QUERY 2026 summit should offer a preview of these emerging trends and the practical approaches being adopted by leading organizations.
