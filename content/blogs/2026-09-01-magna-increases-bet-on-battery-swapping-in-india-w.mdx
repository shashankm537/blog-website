## Magna Doubles Down on India's Electric Future with Major Yuma Investment

Canadian auto parts giant Magna International is significantly upping its commitment to India's burgeoning electric vehicle landscape, injecting another $35 million into battery-swapping startup Yuma Energy. This substantial investment underscores Magna's strong belief in the potential of battery swapping to revolutionize transportation in India, particularly for its vast two- and three-wheeler market and a rapidly expanding delivery sector.

### Fueling the Gig Economy, One Swap at a Time

Magna's increased stake in Yuma is a clear bet on the backbone of India's modern economy: the gig worker. For delivery riders, every minute spent waiting for an electric vehicle to charge translates directly into lost earnings. Yuma's battery-swapping model offers a compelling solution, allowing riders to exchange a depleted battery for a fully charged one in under two minutes. This drastically improves vehicle uptime, making electric mobility a far more practical and cost-effective choice for those who rely on their vehicles for their livelihood. While electric vehicles are still a minority in the gig economy, Yuma, with Magna's backing, is poised to accelerate their adoption by making the transition seamless and efficient.

### Expanding Yuma's Reach and Impact

This latest funding round builds upon Magna's previous significant investments in Yuma and its parent company, Yulu, a prominent Indian electric mobility startup. Since its inception, Yuma has already facilitated over 60 million battery swaps and deployed roughly 100,000 batteries across its network. The infusion of $35 million will undoubtedly fuel further expansion, allowing Yuma to scale its operations, enhance its technology, and reach more riders in key urban centers. As Yuma's network grows, it becomes an increasingly attractive ecosystem for both riders and businesses looking to electrify their fleets.

### What to Watch

Magna's continued and increased commitment signals a strong long-term vision for battery swapping in India. Investors and industry observers will be keen to see how this capital will translate into tangible growth for Yuma, including the expansion of its swapping station infrastructure and the potential for new partnerships. The success of this model could pave the way for wider adoption of electric two- and three-wheelers across India, reshaping urban mobility.
