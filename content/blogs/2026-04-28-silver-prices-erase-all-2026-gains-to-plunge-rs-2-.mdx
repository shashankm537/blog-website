## Silver's Wild Ride: Rally Reverses, Wiping Out All 2026 Gains

Silver, once the shining star of 2025, has experienced a dramatic U-turn, erasing all its impressive gains for the current year. What began as a spectacular rally, soaring by 170% in 2025 and adding another 74% in January 2026, has now seen prices plummet, leaving investors scrambling. The white metal is now trading below its 2025 closing price, a stark reminder of its volatility.

### The Sudden Plunge: What's Driving the Selloff?

The speed and magnitude of silver's decline have surprised many. May silver futures have shed a staggering 46% from their peak, a loss of over Rs 2 lakh per kilogram in just three months. This sharp correction can be attributed to a confluence of factors, signaling a shift in investor sentiment and global economic pressures.

One significant driver appears to be the unexpected reaction to geopolitical tensions. While silver is typically considered a safe haven, it has fallen even as tensions in West Asia have escalated. The initial surge in crude oil prices and heightened geopolitical risks had sparked a broad risk-off sentiment, prompting investors to seek liquidity and reduce leveraged positions. In such scenarios, even traditionally safe assets can face selling pressure as investors liquidate holdings to manage margin calls or rebalance their portfolios.

### A Stronger Dollar and Hawkish Fed Bite

Adding to silver's woes is a stronger U.S. dollar and a more assertive stance from the Federal Reserve. As silver is priced in dollars, a strengthening dollar naturally makes it more expensive for buyers using other currencies, thereby dampening demand. Furthermore, the prospect of higher interest rates from a hawkish Fed diminishes the attractiveness of non-yielding assets like silver, as investors can find better returns in interest-bearing investments. This combination of currency strength and monetary policy tightening is a double blow to silver's appeal.

### What To Watch

With silver prices now significantly down, the question on many investors' minds is whether this presents a buying opportunity. Market watchers will be closely observing upcoming economic data from the U.S., particularly inflation figures and the Federal Reserve's commentary, for clues on future interest rate policy. Geopolitical developments in West Asia will also continue to play a crucial role in shaping investor sentiment towards safe-haven assets. The interplay of these factors will likely dictate silver's next move.
