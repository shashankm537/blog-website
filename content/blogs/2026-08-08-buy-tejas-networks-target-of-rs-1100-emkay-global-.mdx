## Tejas Networks: Emkay Global Bullish on Telecom Player, Sets Ambitious Price Target

A prominent financial services firm is signaling strong confidence in Tejas Networks, recommending a "buy" and forecasting significant growth potential for the Indian telecom equipment maker. The optimism stems from recent performance and a favorable outlook for the company's strategic positioning in the domestic and global markets.

### Driving Factors: BSNL's 4G and Domestic Manufacturing Push

Emkay Global Financial's recent research report highlights the robust execution of BSNL's 4G project as a key catalyst for Tejas Networks' positive momentum. This successful project delivery not only boosts current revenues but also underscores the company's capabilities. Furthermore, the Indian government's strong emphasis on domestic manufacturing, supported by initiatives like the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme, provides a significant tailwind. This policy direction is expected to fuel substantial investments in critical infrastructure projects such as BSNL, BharatNet, and railway network upgrades, all areas where Tejas Networks is well-positioned to contribute.

### Expanding Horizons: New Clients and Global Trends

Beyond domestic projects, Tejas Networks is also benefiting from strategic partnerships and evolving global dynamics. Strong references from major players like Tata Communications and TCS are opening doors to new clients and expanding the company's footprint. Simultaneously, a global trend towards diversifying telecom equipment sources away from Chinese vendors is creating a favorable environment for Indian manufacturers like Tejas Networks. This international shift, coupled with its proven track record, positions the company for expanded market share and increased revenue streams in the coming years, with Emkay projecting a substantial revenue increase for FY25.

### Analyst's Outlook and Price Target

Based on its analysis, Emkay Global Financial has revised its price target for Tejas Networks upwards to Rs 1100 per share, representing a potential upside of over 21% from its previous target. This revised valuation, derived using a discounted cash flow (DCF) methodology, reflects the firm's increased confidence in the company's future earnings potential and operational efficiency. The "buy" recommendation remains in place, signaling a positive outlook for investors.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring Tejas Networks' continued execution on major government telecom projects and its success in securing new contracts from both domestic and international clients. The company's ability to navigate the evolving global supply chain landscape and capitalize on the "China Plus One" strategy will be crucial indicators of its future growth trajectory.
