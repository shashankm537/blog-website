## From VLC's Orange Cone to Robot Control: Jean-Baptiste Kempf's Next Big Move

You've likely encountered his work every time you've played a video online. Now, the mastermind behind the ubiquitous VLC Media Player is shifting his focus from smooth playback to the real-time control of robots and drones. Jean-Baptiste Kempf, a celebrated figure in the open-source community, is launching Kyber, a new venture aiming to power the coming wave of physical AI and remote operations.

### Building the Foundation for a Robotic Future

Kempf envisions a world where millions of robots and drones will soon navigate our streets and workplaces. To facilitate this, Kyber is developing a crucial infrastructure layer designed for ultra-low latency control of these devices. Their core offering is an SDK (Software Development Kit) that synchronizes video, audio, sensor data, and control commands with remarkable speed. This technology is seen as fundamental to the advancement of "physical AI," where intelligent systems interact directly with the real world.

### Securing Investment for Ambitious Goals

The potential of Kyber has already attracted significant attention, leading to a $5 million funding round spearheaded by Lightspeed Venture Partners. This investment underscores the growing belief in the necessity of robust underlying systems for physical AI to reach its full capabilities. Kempf, a serial entrepreneur with a proven track record, aims to address scenarios where operators, computing power, and physical actions are geographically separated, a common challenge in fields ranging from industrial automation to remote healthcare.

### Speed and Synchronization: The Kyber Advantage

The very name "Kyber" hints at the critical need for speed, drawing inspiration from the rapid lightsaber crystals of Star Wars lore. Kempf emphasizes that in controlling real-world devices, every millisecond counts. The company's innovative approach leverages deep expertise in video streaming technology, a domain where Kempf has extensive experience from his time at cloud gaming startup Shadow. This foundation allows Kyber to effectively minimize lag, a vital component for seamless and responsive remote operation of complex machinery.

### What To Watch

With substantial funding secured and a clear vision for the future, Kyber is poised to become a key player in the burgeoning field of physical AI and remote operations. Keep an eye on their progress as they refine their SDK and partner with early adopters across various industries. The successful integration of their technology could dramatically accelerate the deployment and effectiveness of robots and other remotely controlled systems.
