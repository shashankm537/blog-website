A significant breakthrough in the fight against cybercrime has emerged with reports of a key suspect linked to the notorious ShinyHunters digital extortion group being detained in Jordan. Known by the online alias "Rey," this individual is reportedly cooperating with U.S. federal authorities, potentially providing crucial insights into the group's inner workings and its members.

### The Key Suspect and His Past

Sources familiar with the investigation indicate that Saif ‌al-Din Khader, the individual behind the "Rey" and "ReyXBF" handles, was taken into custody on September 29, 2026. His alleged cooperation with the FBI and other law enforcement agencies is being hailed as a critical step in ongoing efforts to apprehend other members of the elusive ShinyHunters collective. This isn't Rey's first brush with the digital underworld, nor law enforcement. Independent security journalist Brian Krebs, in a November 2025 report, identified Rey as one of the administrators of Scattered LAPSUS$ Hunters (SLH), a formidable group believed to be a fusion of cybercrime entities like Scattered Spider, LAPSUS$, and ShinyHunters themselves. Prior to this, Rey was also reportedly involved as an administrator for the data leak website of the Hellcat ransomware group, which became active in late 2024. The same year, he allegedly took the reins as administrator of BreachForums, a prominent platform for stolen data. Interestingly, Khader had previously asserted to Krebs that he had been assisting law enforcement as early as June 2025, adding another layer to his complex history.

### Broader Crackdown: Recent Arrests and FBI Efforts

This latest development concerning Rey follows closely on the heels of another significant arrest tied to ShinyHunters operations. Just last week, a 24-year-old man from Amsterdam was apprehended for his alleged involvement in the threat actor's malicious cyber activities. While initially unnamed by authorities, independent reports soon identified him as Pepijn van der Stap, an individual with a past as a "reformed hacker" who had since transitioned into an offensive security lead role at the Dutch firm Neo Security. However, a spokesperson claiming to represent ShinyHunters swiftly denied any connection to van der Stap, muddying the waters around the group's true composition. Following the Amsterdam arrest, FBI Director Kash Patel emphasized the agency's proactive stance, stating that "FBI teams are actively working with partners to obtain and execute more leads in the ongoing investigation based on this arrest," a sentiment he reiterated on social media.

### What To Watch

The detention of "Rey" and his reported cooperation could significantly accelerate the global crackdown on ShinyHunters and its affiliates. Expect to see potential further arrests as law enforcement leverages the intelligence gathered from Khader. The investigation may also shed more light on the intricate relationships between different cybercrime groups, particularly the "amalgamation" model seen with Scattered LAPSUS$ Hunters. Details regarding the extent of the group's operations and the fate of those implicated will be key areas to monitor in the coming weeks and months.
