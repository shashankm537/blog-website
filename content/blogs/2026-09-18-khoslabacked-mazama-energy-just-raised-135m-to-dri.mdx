## Geothermal Startup Mazama Energy Secures Major Funding for Super-Hot Rock Power

Mazama Energy, a startup backed by tech investor Vinod Khosla, has just announced a significant funding boost of $135 million to revolutionize geothermal energy production. This substantial investment, which was oversubscribed, will fuel their ambitious plans to tap into ultra-hot subterranean rocks for clean, reliable electricity generation.

### Drilling Deeper for Unprecedented Power

The company's innovative approach focuses on enhanced geothermal systems, specifically targeting super-hot rock formations far beneath the Earth's surface. Mazama has demonstrated impressive drilling speeds, reaching depths of approximately 15,000 feet in just 15 days. At these extreme levels, they can access heat exceeding 750°F (400°C), a temperature that, under immense pressure, transforms water into a "supercritical" fluid. This unique state allows for significantly more efficient energy absorption compared to traditional steam, potentially enabling each well to generate up to ten times the power of conventional geothermal wells.

### Ambitious Growth and Future Potential

This new capital injection is earmarked for developing horizontal wells designed to produce a remarkable 15 megawatts of electricity per well. Mazama anticipates beginning electricity generation as early as next year. Their initial project in Oregon is now estimated to have the capacity to produce a staggering 10 gigawatts of electricity, a doubling of their previous projections. This vast potential aligns with the growing demand for clean energy to power energy-intensive industries like AI data centers, positioning geothermal as a compelling alternative to fossil fuels.

### What To Watch

With this significant funding secured, all eyes will be on Mazama Energy as they progress with their Oregon site development, aiming for full completion by 2030 with a projected output of 200 megawatts. The company has also already acquired land for a second development, signaling a clear trajectory for expansion. The success of their super-hot rock technology could unlock a new era of geothermal power, potentially tapping into the immense global potential of these deep, heat-rich environments.
