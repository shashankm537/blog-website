## Tejas Networks Sees Bullish Outlook as Emkay Global Financial Sets Rs 1100 Target

Investors are taking notice of Tejas Networks, with a prominent financial firm issuing a strong "buy" recommendation and a significant price target. Emkay Global Financial's latest report highlights promising growth drivers for the company, signaling optimism for its future performance.

### Strong Fundamentals Driving Growth

Emkay Global Financial's bullish stance is rooted in Tejas Networks' impressive financial results, which notably surpassed revenue and margin expectations. A key contributor to this success has been the effective execution of BSNL's 4G project. The report further emphasizes several strategic advantages for Tejas Networks, including the government's focus on domestic manufacturing through the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme. Furthermore, substantial investments in critical infrastructure projects like BSNL, BharatNet, and the Railways are expected to provide a steady stream of opportunities. The company is also benefiting from established relationships with industry giants like TCom and TCS, which are helping to secure new clients and expand its market reach.

### Positive Outlook Fueled by Government Initiatives and Global Trends

Looking ahead, the outlook for Tejas Networks appears robust, driven by both domestic and international factors. Emkay Global Financial projects a substantial fourfold increase in FY25 revenue compared to FY24, largely attributed to the ongoing execution of projects for BSNL and BharatNet. This optimistic revenue forecast is supported by upward revisions to revenue estimates for both FY25 and FY26. Additionally, the firm has increased its EBITDA margin projections, reflecting the company's improved profitability. The global shift away from Chinese telecom equipment presents another significant tailwind, positioning Tejas Networks favorably to capture a larger share of the market.

### What To Watch

Investors will be keen to observe Tejas Networks' continued progress in executing its large-scale projects, particularly those with BSNL and BharatNet. The company's ability to secure new clients through its existing partnerships and capitalize on the global trend of diversifying telecom equipment suppliers will also be crucial indicators of its future success. The market will be closely watching whether the company can sustain its revenue growth and margin expansion as projected by Emkay Global Financial.
