## Tejas Networks Sees Bullish Forecast from Emkay Global, Rs 1100 Target Set

Analysts at Emkay Global Financial are sounding an optimistic note for Tejas Networks, issuing a strong "Buy" recommendation with a significantly raised price target. This positive outlook stems from the company's recent performance and its strategic positioning in key growth areas.

### Strong Quarter Driven by BSNL Project

Tejas Networks has delivered a robust financial quarter, exceeding expectations on both revenue and profit margins. This impressive performance was largely fueled by the successful execution of projects for state-owned telecom giant BSNL, particularly its ongoing 4G network rollout. The company's ability to deliver on these large-scale projects highlights its operational efficiency and growing capabilities in the domestic telecom infrastructure space.

### Key Growth Drivers in Play

Several factors are contributing to Emkay Global's bullish stance on Tejas Networks. The Indian government's focus on boosting domestic manufacturing through initiatives like the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme provides a significant tailwind. Furthermore, substantial investments planned for critical infrastructure projects such as BSNL's network expansion, the BharatNet rural broadband initiative, and railway telecommunications offer a large addressable market. The company also stands to benefit from its growing client base, bolstered by references from major players like TCom and TCS, and the global trend of telecom operators diversifying away from Chinese equipment suppliers. Emkay anticipates that these factors will lead to a remarkable four-fold increase in Tejas Networks' revenue for FY25 compared to FY24, driven by the ongoing execution of BSNL and BharatNet contracts.

### Revised Outlook and Price Target

In light of Tejas Networks' strong performance and favorable market dynamics, Emkay Global has revised its price target upward to Rs 1100 per share, representing a potential upside of 21% from its previous Rs 975 target. This revised valuation, based on a discounted cash flow methodology, underscores the firm's confidence in the company's future growth trajectory. The "Buy" rating has been maintained, signaling a conviction in the stock's ability to outperform.

### What To Watch

Investors will be keenly observing the continued progress on the BSNL 4G and BharatNet projects, as well as any new contract wins that further validate Tejas Networks' market position. The company's ability to sustain its margin improvements and navigate the evolving global telecom landscape will also be critical to watch.
