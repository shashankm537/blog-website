## HAL Soars on Strong Q1 Earnings, Analysts Boost Price Targets

Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL) is flying high in investor sentiment following a robust first-quarter earnings report that surpassed expectations. The defence behemoth announced a significant jump in profits and revenue, prompting a wave of optimism and upward revisions to target prices from leading financial institutions.

### Stellar Financial Performance Fuels Investor Confidence

HAL has kicked off fiscal year 2027 with impressive financial results for the April-June quarter. The company reported a healthy 15% year-on-year increase in consolidated net profit, reaching Rs 1,590 crore, a welcome leap from Rs 1,384 crore in the same period last year. This growth was supported by a comparable 14% rise in revenue from operations, which climbed to Rs 5,515 crore. The company's earnings per share also saw a notable uptick, improving from Rs 20.69 to Rs 23.77. These figures painted a bright picture for the public sector undertaking, leading to an approximate 2% surge in its share price on Wednesday.

### Brokerages Rally Behind HAL with Upgraded Price Targets

The positive earnings report has clearly resonated with financial analysts, who have responded by hiking their price targets for HAL shares. Nomura, a prominent international brokerage, highlighted that HAL's Q1 performance exceeded estimates across the board, with a strong order book providing sustained visibility. Impressed by the company's trajectory, Nomura has raised its target price to Rs 6,314 per share, up from Rs 6,040, while reiterating its "Buy" recommendation. This new target suggests a potential upside of over 26% from the stock's recent closing price. Closer to home, Motilal Oswal Financial Services also joined the bullish chorus, increasing its target price for HAL to Rs 5,800 from Rs 5,500, maintaining its "Buy" rating and signaling an anticipated upside of more than 16%.

### What To Watch

Investors will be keenly watching for further commentary from HAL management regarding order book execution and future contract wins. The continued positive sentiment from brokerages suggests that HAL's upward momentum may persist, contingent on the company's ability to capitalize on its strong financial footing and defence sector opportunities.
