### Learning from Mistakes: The Heart of Backpropagation

Now, we turn to the crucial part: how does a neural network actually *learn* when its initial predictions are wrong? This is where backpropagation shines. Imagine the neural network makes a guess, and it's off the mark. Backpropagation is the mechanism that figures out *how* wrong it was and then systematically adjusts the internal "weights" of the network to improve its accuracy for next time. It’s like a student reviewing their test answers to understand where they went wrong and how to do better on the next exam.

This adjustment process is the core of how AI systems get smarter. The network calculates the error in its prediction and then "propagates" that error backward through its layers. Each neuron receives feedback on its contribution to the overall mistake and tweaks its internal settings accordingly. This iterative process of predicting, calculating error, and adjusting is what allows neural networks to master complex tasks with remarkable precision.
