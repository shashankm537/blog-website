## Transformers and Rectifiers Powers Ahead with Major MEIL Order

Transformers and Rectifiers (India) Ltd. saw its stock surge by almost 8% on Monday following the announcement of a significant new contract. The company secured a substantial project order from Megha Engineering and Infrastructures Limited (MEIL), valued between Rs 100 crore and Rs 500 crore, signaling a boost for its manufacturing and infrastructure business.

### A Boost from a Powerhouse

The newly acquired order from MEIL is for the manufacturing of transformers and associated services. This collaboration is set to be executed over the next 35 months, demonstrating a significant commitment from both companies. MEIL, a prominent name in the engineering and infrastructure sector, has recognized Transformers and Rectifiers' capabilities, placing a large-scale order that underlines the company's growing reputation in the industry. This project is classified as "large" according to the company's policies, indicating its substantial impact on their order book.

### Expanding Horizons: Entry into the Nuclear Sector

Adding another layer of positive development, Transformers and Rectifiers has also announced a landmark achievement: its first foray into India's burgeoning nuclear power sector. The company has received a crucial order from the Nuclear Power Corporation of India Limited (NPCIL) for Generator Transformers. These vital components will be supplied for NPCIL's Kaiga Units 5 & 6 nuclear power project located in Karnataka. This strategic move diversifies the company's portfolio, marking a significant entry into a high-stakes and technically demanding segment of critical power infrastructure.

### What to Watch

Investors will be keen to observe the progress of the MEIL project and the successful integration of Transformers and Rectifiers into the nuclear power supply chain. The successful execution of these large orders could pave the way for further opportunities in both conventional and advanced energy sectors. The company's continued ability to secure such significant contracts will be a key indicator of its future growth trajectory.
