## Pivotal's Captain Departs: A New Era for Larry Page's Flying Car Venture

The innovative team behind Pivotal, the flying car company backed by Google co-founder Larry Page, is facing a significant leadership change. Ken Karklin, who helmed the electric vertical takeoff and landing (eVTOL) firm for over four years, has stepped down as CEO. This departure signals a potential shift for the company as it navigates the rapidly evolving advanced air mobility landscape.

### A Chapter Closes for a Visionary Leader

For more than four years, Ken Karklin has been the driving force behind Pivotal, a company dedicated to making personal electric flight a reality. In a recent press release, Karklin announced he is "pursuing new endeavors," marking the end of his tenure. He expressed immense pride in Pivotal's progress, stating that the company has moved beyond theoretical promises and is now operating in the real world, facing actual challenges and contributing to the future of air travel. This sentiment highlights the transition from a nascent idea to a tangible, operational enterprise.

### Navigating the Transition: An Interim Pilot Takes the Controls

Stepping into the CEO role on an interim basis is Mike Ross, a seasoned aviation executive who joined Pivotal’s board in November 2025. Ross acknowledged Karklin's crucial role in bringing Pivotal to its current standing and assured stakeholders that the company's core mission, priorities, and development roadmap will remain unchanged. He emphasized Pivotal's commitment to safety, engineering excellence, and accessibility as they continue to advance towards bringing their fourth-generation Helix aircraft to market.

Pivotal's journey includes the launch of its lightweight, single-seat Helix aircraft, which notably does not require a pilot's license and retails for around $200,000. Beyond personal aviation, the company has also been developing the BlackFly aircraft, specifically designed for defense and first-responder applications, demonstrating a dual focus on both consumer and critical-use markets.

### What To Watch

All eyes will now be on Mike Ross and the Pivotal team as they manage this leadership transition. Investors and industry observers will be keen to see how the company continues to execute its ambitious product development and sales strategies, particularly with the ongoing rollout of the Helix and further advancements in the BlackFly program. The success of these initiatives will be key indicators of Pivotal's future trajectory in the competitive advanced air mobility sector.
