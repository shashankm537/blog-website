**Rethinking Data Engineering Interviews and Streamlining Big Data Operations**

The landscape of data engineering is evolving, with new tools and methodologies emerging to tackle complex challenges. This week, we're seeing significant shifts, from how we assess data engineers' skills to how major companies are optimizing their data infrastructure for efficiency and cost savings.

### The Future of Coding Interviews: Beyond Just Writing Code

The traditional coding interview, focused solely on generating new code, is becoming a relic of the past. Many organizations are now embracing a more practical approach: the "fix-and-explain" coding interview. This method aims to test a data engineer's true understanding of code, their debugging prowess, and their ability to articulate solutions. To help aspiring data engineers hone these crucial skills, LeetCode.ai has launched a new "code review" feature. This innovative tool allows users to practice identifying and correcting bugs, mirroring the demands of these modern interview formats.

### Driving Efficiency with Lakehouse Migrations and Unified Control Planes

Two prominent companies are showcasing how they've dramatically improved their data operations. Flock has successfully migrated its extensive lakehouse from AWS Glue to DuckDB, a move that has slashed core build times by an impressive 74% and compute expenses by 91%. This was achieved by leveraging Open Iceberg storage for compute modularity, replacing heavy distributed systems with efficient single-node engines tailored to specific workloads. Meanwhile, Meesho has engineered MeeUPP, a sophisticated control plane designed to manage its vast Spark operations. This unified system orchestrates over 125,000 daily Spark runs, intelligently routing them to optimize resource utilization and significantly reduce cluster costs by 90%, while simultaneously cutting compute expenses by 40%.

### What To Watch

Expect to see more discussions around the effectiveness of "fix-and-explain" interviews as companies look for candidates with strong analytical and problem-solving skills. On the infrastructure front, the success of Flock and Meesho highlights a growing trend towards optimizing data pipelines for both performance and cost, likely spurring further adoption of similar modular architectures and centralized control plane solutions within the data engineering community.
