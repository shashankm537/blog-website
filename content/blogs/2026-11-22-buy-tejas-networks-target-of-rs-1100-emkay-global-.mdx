## Tejas Networks Eyes $1100 Target as Emkay Global Issues Strong Buy Call

Investors in Indian telecom equipment maker Tejas Networks are buzzing with optimism following a significant upgrade from Emkay Global Financial. The brokerage firm has not only reiterated its "Buy" recommendation but also boosted its price target for the stock, citing strong performance and promising future growth drivers. This positive outlook is rooted in the company's impressive execution and its strategic positioning within India's rapidly expanding digital infrastructure landscape.

### BSNL Project Fuels Revenue Surge and Margin Expansion

Tejas Networks has recently demonstrated robust financial performance, exceeding expectations on both revenue and profitability. A key catalyst for this success has been the company's active involvement in the rollout of Bharat Sanchar Nigam Limited's (BSNL) 4G network. This significant project has not only driven substantial revenue growth but also contributed to an improvement in the company's earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA) margins. Emkay Global's analysts were particularly impressed by the company's ability to deliver effectively on large-scale government projects, a trend expected to continue.

### Multiple Growth Pillars Underpin Bullish Outlook

Emkay Global's positive stance on Tejas Networks is underpinned by several compelling growth factors. The Indian government's strong emphasis on domestic manufacturing and the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme is a significant tailwind, encouraging local production of critical telecom equipment. Furthermore, substantial government investments in BSNL, BharatNet, and railway communication projects provide a steady stream of demand. The company also benefits from strong endorsements and a growing client base, thanks to its partnerships with industry giants like Tata Communications and TCS, which are opening doors to new opportunities. Additionally, the global trend of moving away from Chinese telecom equipment suppliers creates a favorable environment for companies like Tejas Networks to expand their international footprint.

### What To Watch

With Emkay Global projecting a considerable upside potential of 21% and setting a target price of Rs 1,100, all eyes will be on Tejas Networks' continued execution of major government projects. Investors will also be keenly observing the company's ability to leverage its growing partnerships and capitalize on the global shift in telecom supply chains. The successful expansion of its customer base and the ongoing impact of government initiatives will be crucial indicators for future performance.
