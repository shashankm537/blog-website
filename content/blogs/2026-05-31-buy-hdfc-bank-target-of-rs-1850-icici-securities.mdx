## ICICI Securities Sees Major Upside in HDFC Bank, Recommends "Buy"

Financial analysts at ICICI Securities have thrown their weight behind HDFC Bank, issuing a strong "Buy" recommendation with an ambitious price target. This bullish outlook comes as the banking giant navigates significant changes following its recent merger.

### Navigating Post-Merger Dynamics

The integration of HDFC into HDFC Bank has undeniably reshaped its financial landscape. Analysts note a noticeable shift, including a higher loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) and a reduced share of low-cost current account savings account (CASA) deposits, alongside an increased reliance on borrowings. Despite these adjustments, the merger has significantly boosted HDFC Bank's market share in loans, adding an impressive 330 basis points to reach 14.5%.

While loan market share has surged, the growth in deposits has been more measured. ICICI Securities projects a robust annual growth rate of 18% for deposits over the next two fiscal years, translating to an absolute increase of INR 4 to 5 trillion each year. This growth is anticipated to be achieved even within the evolving competitive banking environment. The bank is expected to strategically manage its LDR and borrowing mix, leading to a projected moderation in loan growth to 13% CAGR for FY24-26, with LDR easing to around 100% by FY25 and 96% by FY26.

### Earnings Outlook and Valuation

The recent quarterly earnings for Q4FY24 showed a net interest margin (NIM) that slightly surpassed expectations, though this may have been partly influenced by seasonal factors. Consequently, ICICI Securities has revised its earnings per share (EPS) estimates downwards by 5% for both FY25 and FY26, primarily due to the adjusted growth projections. Nevertheless, the firm anticipates HDFC Bank to deliver a healthy return on assets (RoA) of 1.7% and a return on equity (RoE) of 14-15% for these periods. The target price for the stock remains firm at INR 1,850.

Following a recent dip of 10% year-to-date, ICICI Securities has upgraded its rating on HDFC Bank to "Buy." The analysts suggest that the stock may see a gradual re-rating, as investors weigh the strong deposit growth against a potentially more measured credit expansion compared to the broader system, alongside a phased approach to substituting borrowings.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring HDFC Bank's ability to manage its LDR and deposit growth effectively in the coming quarters. Key performance indicators to watch will include deposit accretion trends, NIM stability, and the pace of loan growth in relation to system-wide expansion. The success of the bank's borrowing substitution strategy will also be a critical factor influencing its profitability and overall valuation.
