## HDFC Bank Gets a Vote of Confidence: ICICI Securities Eyes 18.5% Upside

In a move signaling renewed optimism for India's largest private lender, ICICI Securities has issued a strong "Buy" recommendation for HDFC Bank. Analysts are projecting a significant jump in the bank's stock value, setting a price target of ₹1,850, a substantial increase from its current trading levels. This endorsement comes after a period of notable shifts within the bank following its recent merger.

### Navigating Post-Merger Dynamics

The integration of HDFC Ltd. into HDFC Bank has led to a significant reshaping of the bank's financial structure. Key changes include a higher loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) and a reduced share of low-cost current account savings account (CASA) deposits, alongside an increased reliance on borrowings. While the merger has boosted the bank's loan market share considerably, its deposit market share growth has been more moderate. ICICI Securities anticipates strong deposit growth for the bank in the coming years, projecting an 18% compound annual growth rate (CAGR). Despite this robust deposit accretion, the bank is expected to manage its LDR and borrowing mix strategically, leading to a revised loan growth estimate of 13% CAGR.

### Earnings Outlook and Stock Re-rating

While the bank's recent Net Interest Margin (NIM) performance in Q4FY24 showed a slight improvement, analysts attribute this partly to seasonal factors. Consequently, earnings per share (EPS) estimates for FY25 and FY26 have been adjusted downwards by 5% to account for the moderated growth projections. Nevertheless, ICICI Securities forecasts healthy return on assets (RoA) of 1.7% and a return on equity (RoE) of 14-15% for the same periods. The "Buy" rating has been upgraded due to a notable correction in the stock price, which has seen a 10% dip year-to-date. However, the re-rating is anticipated to be gradual, as the bank balances strong deposit growth with a potentially slower credit expansion compared to the broader market and a phased approach to borrowing substitution.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely observing HDFC Bank's ability to effectively manage its deposit and loan growth strategies in the evolving competitive landscape. The pace at which the bank can optimize its balance sheet post-merger and improve its CASA ratio will be crucial. Furthermore, the market will be keen to see how the bank's NIMs perform beyond seasonal fluctuations and how its credit growth evolves relative to the system.
