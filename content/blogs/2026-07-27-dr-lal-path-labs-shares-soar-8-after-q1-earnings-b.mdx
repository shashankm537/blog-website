## Dr Lal Path Labs Soars on Stellar Q1 Performance; Analysts Bullish

Dr Lal Path Labs experienced a significant surge in its stock price, climbing nearly 8% on Monday to reach a new 52-week high. This impressive rally follows the company's announcement of first-quarter earnings that surpassed analyst expectations, prompting several financial institutions to revise their price targets upwards.

### A Quarter of Strong Growth

The diagnostics giant reported a robust 28% year-on-year increase in its consolidated net profit for the April-June quarter of FY27, reaching Rs 169.5 crore. This marks a substantial jump from Rs 132.4 crore in the same period last year. Accompanying this profit growth, revenue from operations also saw a healthy rise of over 19% year-on-year, totaling Rs 797.7 crore for the quarter. In addition to these strong financial results, the company declared an interim dividend of Rs 5 per equity share, with July 30 set as the record date for shareholder eligibility.

### Brokerage Houses Cheer the Results

The positive quarterly performance has garnered significant attention from financial analysts, with many reiterating their optimistic outlook on Dr Lal Path Labs. For instance, Nomura maintained its 'Buy' rating and significantly boosted its target price to Rs 2,085 per share from Rs 1,860. This upward revision suggests an impressive potential upside of 18.5% based on the stock's prior closing price. Nomura specifically highlighted that the company's earnings exceeded their projections, largely due to better-than-anticipated realisations. This improvement is attributed to strategic price increases implemented under the CGHS and ECHS schemes, with management anticipating these benefits to persist for the next two to three quarters. Other prominent brokerage firms like Nuvama and JM Financial are also reportedly reviewing their targets, indicating a general positive sentiment across the analyst community.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring the company's ability to sustain these price increases and the continued positive impact of the CGHS and ECHS schemes in upcoming quarters. Further updates from brokerage firms and the company's commentary on future growth strategies will be key indicators for the stock's trajectory.
