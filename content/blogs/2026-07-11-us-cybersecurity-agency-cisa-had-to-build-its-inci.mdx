## Cybersecurity Agency Scrambled to Write the Rules Mid-Crisis

In a stark admission, the U.S. cybersecurity agency tasked with protecting critical national infrastructure revealed it was forced to create its incident response plan *after* a major security breach was discovered. This revelation comes from the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) itself, detailing how its team had to improvise response protocols in real-time when sensitive government access keys were found exposed online.

### A Playbook Built Under Pressure

CISA, a vital branch of the Department of Homeland Security, disclosed in a post-incident report that its staff "had to spend time building [a playbook] during the early stages of the incident." This admission highlights a significant vulnerability: rather than having pre-established procedures for various security scenarios, CISA had to construct its emergency guide on the fly. While the agency has not specified how much this delay impacted their overall response time, the situation underscores the critical need for comprehensive preparedness in the face of escalating cyber threats.

The incident, first reported by independent journalist Brian Krebs, stemmed from a CISA contractor's employee mistakenly uploading sensitive credentials to a public GitHub repository. A security researcher from the firm GitGuardian discovered the exposed data and attempted to alert the contractor without success. It was only after Krebs contacted CISA directly that the agency took action, promptly removing the repository and resetting the compromised access keys to prevent potential misuse.

### Lessons Learned and New Channels

CISA emphasized that no sensitive customer or mission data was compromised during this episode, and they expressed gratitude to both the researcher and the reporter for their crucial role in identifying and reporting the breach. A key takeaway for the agency was the inadequacy of its existing channels for receiving such critical alerts from the cybersecurity community. To address this, CISA has since revamped its notification pathways, aiming to establish clearer, more efficient processes for security researchers to report potential vulnerabilities and incidents. This proactive step is crucial for building trust and improving rapid response capabilities within the broader cybersecurity ecosystem.

### What To Watch

Moving forward, attention will be on CISA's implementation of these new reporting channels and whether the agency will prioritize the development of comprehensive incident response playbooks for a wider range of potential threats. The ongoing leadership transition at CISA also remains a factor to monitor as the agency navigates these evolving cybersecurity challenges.
