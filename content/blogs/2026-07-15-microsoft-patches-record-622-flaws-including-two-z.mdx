## Microsoft Unleashes Patch Storm: Record Fixes Include Actively Exploited Threats

In a significant cybersecurity alert, Microsoft has released its largest-ever Patch Tuesday update, addressing a staggering 622 vulnerabilities. What's particularly alarming is that this massive rollout includes fixes for two "zero-day" flaws – security holes that were already being actively exploited by malicious actors before Microsoft could patch them.

### The Urgent Threats: Two Zero-Days Under Fire

The most pressing issues in this update are the two zero-day vulnerabilities. One, identified as CVE-2026-56164, targets on-premises SharePoint Server. This flaw allows an unauthenticated attacker to gain elevated privileges remotely, a dangerous gateway into sensitive company documents. Microsoft has credited incident responders from Mandiant and Google's FLARE team for uncovering this exploit, suggesting it was found within active attack campaigns.

The second actively exploited vulnerability, CVE-2026-56155, affects Active Directory Federation Services (AD FS). This particular flaw permits an attacker who has already gained some level of access to escalate their privileges locally through weak access controls. Microsoft's own incident response team, DART, is credited with discovering this issue, highlighting the immediate danger posed to systems managing user authentication.

### A Record-Breaking Patching Effort

Beyond the two critical zero-days, the sheer scale of this Patch Tuesday is unprecedented. With over 600 vulnerabilities addressed, this update dwarfs previous records, more than tripling the roughly 200 fixes issued in June. This massive undertaking suggests a concentrated effort by Microsoft to shore up its defenses against a wide array of security weaknesses, impacting a broad spectrum of its products and services. Organizations are strongly advised to prioritize these updates, especially those running SharePoint Server 2016 and 2019, which are also reaching the end of their extended support and lack a paid support program.

### What To Watch

The sheer volume of this patch suggests a significant increase in discovered vulnerabilities or a proactive move to address a broad threat landscape. Security teams will be diligently assessing the impact of these patches across their environments. For those running affected versions of SharePoint Server, the convergence of end-of-support and critical patching presents a particularly complex challenge. Expect ongoing analysis from cybersecurity experts regarding the specific techniques used in the zero-day exploits and the broader implications for enterprise security.
