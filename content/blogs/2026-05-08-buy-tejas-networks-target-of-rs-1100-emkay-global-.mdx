## Tejas Networks Stock Soars on Positive Analyst Outlook, Analysts Eye Rs 1100 Target

A leading financial analysis firm is singing the praises of Tejas Networks, issuing a strong "buy" recommendation and setting an ambitious price target. This positive sentiment stems from recent performance that has exceeded expectations, particularly driven by a significant government project.

### Strong Performance Fuels Optimism

Emkay Global Financial, in a recent research report, has thrown its weight behind Tejas Networks, signaling a notable upside potential. The company's recent financial results showcased impressive revenue and margin performance, largely attributed to its work on the BSNL 4G project. This successful execution has caught the attention of analysts, who believe Tejas Networks is strategically positioned for future growth.

The firm highlights several key factors supporting their bullish stance. A significant driver is the Indian government's push for domestic manufacturing, bolstered by initiatives like the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme. Coupled with substantial investments in crucial infrastructure projects such as BSNL, BharatNet, and the Indian Railways, Tejas Networks stands to benefit immensely. Furthermore, strong collaborations with industry giants like Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) are opening doors to new clients and reinforcing the company's credibility in the global telecom equipment market, especially as countries look to diversify away from Chinese suppliers.

### Future Growth Prospects Look Bright

Looking ahead, analysts anticipate a substantial revenue surge for Tejas Networks, projecting a potential fourfold increase in FY25 compared to FY24, driven by continued progress on the BSNL and BharatNet projects. This optimistic outlook has led to upward revisions in revenue estimates for both FY25 and FY26. Moreover, the company's improved margin performance, beating previous forecasts, has also prompted an increase in its EBITDA margin projections. Emkay Global Financial has consequently revised its target price for Tejas Networks to Rs 1100 per share, representing a significant potential gain from its current trading levels.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring the progress of the BSNL and BharatNet projects in the coming quarters, as their successful execution will be critical to realizing the projected revenue growth. Additionally, any new client acquisitions or further partnerships, particularly those stemming from global efforts to diversify telecom supply chains, will be key indicators of Tejas Networks' expanding market reach. The company's ability to maintain its current margin performance amidst increasing project execution will also be a crucial factor to observe.
