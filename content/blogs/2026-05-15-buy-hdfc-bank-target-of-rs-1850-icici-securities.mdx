## HDFC Bank Gets a Boost: Analysts See Strong Upside Ahead

In a significant development for the banking sector, ICICI Securities has issued a strong "Buy" recommendation for HDFC Bank, setting an ambitious price target of Rs 1,850. This optimistic outlook comes in the wake of recent structural changes within the bank following its merger, which analysts believe are laying the groundwork for substantial future growth.

### Navigating Post-Merger Realities

The merger has undeniably reshaped HDFC Bank's financial profile, leading to a notable increase in its loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) and a shift in its deposit mix. While the share of low-cost current account savings account (CASA) deposits has decreased, the bank has seen a significant rise in its overall borrowings. This strategic realignment has boosted HDFC Bank's loan market share considerably, though deposit growth, while improving, is catching up at a slightly more measured pace.

ICICI Securities anticipates robust deposit growth for HDFC Bank over the next couple of years, projecting an impressive compounded annual growth rate (CAGR) of 18%. This growth is expected to add trillions to the bank's deposit base annually, even in a competitive environment. Despite this strong inflow, the bank is expected to maintain a balanced approach to its LDR and actively manage its borrowing costs, leading to a projected easing of the LDR to more sustainable levels.

### A Promising Outlook Despite Adjustments

While the recent quarterly net interest margin (NIM) performance showed some seasonal strength, analysts are tempering their earnings per share (EPS) estimates slightly for the upcoming fiscal years. This adjustment is primarily due to revised projections for loan growth, which is now expected to grow at a CAGR of 13%. However, the overall outlook remains very positive, with the bank projected to achieve a healthy return on assets (RoA) of 1.7% and a return on equity (RoE) between 14-15%. The target price of Rs 1,850 remains firm, reflecting confidence in the bank's long-term prospects. The recent dip in HDFC Bank's stock price has presented a compelling entry point, prompting the upgrade to a "Buy" rating.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring HDFC Bank's ability to effectively manage its deposit accretion and loan growth in parallel. The gradual substitution of borrowings and the bank's performance in a dynamic credit landscape will be key factors influencing its re-rating. The sustained growth in deposits and its impact on net interest margins will be critical indicators to track in the coming quarters.
