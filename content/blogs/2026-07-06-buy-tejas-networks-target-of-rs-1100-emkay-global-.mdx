## Tejas Networks Sees Strong Upside Potential, Emkay Global Financial Initiates "Buy"

Telecom infrastructure provider Tejas Networks is catching the eye of financial analysts, with Emkay Global Financial recently issuing a strong "Buy" recommendation. The firm is particularly optimistic about the company's future, setting an ambitious price target that signals significant growth ahead.

### Riding the Wave of Domestic Manufacturing and Network Expansion

Emkay Global Financial points to several key factors fueling their positive outlook for Tejas Networks. A major driver is the Indian government's strong push for domestic manufacturing and incentives like the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme, which directly benefits companies like Tejas. Furthermore, significant investments being channeled into critical national projects such as BSNL's 4G network rollout, BharatNet for rural broadband, and the Railways' communication infrastructure are creating substantial demand for Tejas's solutions. The company is also leveraging its established relationships with major players like Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) to secure new business, and the global trend of diversifying away from Chinese telecom equipment suppliers further bolsters its competitive position.

### Robust Growth Projections Driven by Key Projects

The research report highlights impressive revenue growth projections for Tejas Networks, largely on the back of its involvement in major government-backed projects. Emkay forecasts a quadrupling of FY25 revenue compared to FY24, a testament to the anticipated execution of the BSNL and BharatNet projects. This optimism is supported by upward revisions to revenue estimates for both FY25 and FY26, reflecting the company's recent performance that exceeded expectations. Analysts also anticipate an improvement in EBITDA margins, indicating enhanced operational efficiency and profitability.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring Tejas Networks' progress on the BSNL 4G and BharatNet projects, as their successful execution is crucial for achieving the projected revenue growth. The company's ability to secure new international clients and capitalize on the global shift in telecom equipment sourcing will also be a key indicator of its long-term potential.
