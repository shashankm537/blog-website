## Tejas Networks Sees Strong Upside Potential as Emkay Global Issues "Buy" Rating

**Bengaluru, India – April 24, 2024** – Investors in the Indian telecommunications sector have a reason to be optimistic, as Emkay Global Financial has issued a bullish "Buy" recommendation for Tejas Networks. The financial services firm has set an ambitious target price of ₹1,100 per share, signaling a significant upside potential based on recent performance and future growth prospects. This development comes after Tejas Networks reportedly surpassed revenue and margin expectations, largely driven by its involvement in the crucial BSNL 4G project.

### Riding the Wave of Domestic Manufacturing and Infrastructure Growth

Emkay Global's positive outlook on Tejas Networks is underpinned by several key factors that align with India's economic and technological ambitions. The company is poised to benefit significantly from the Indian government's strong emphasis on promoting domestic manufacturing, bolstered by initiatives like the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme. This focus creates a favorable environment for local players like Tejas Networks to expand their market share. Furthermore, substantial investments being channeled into critical infrastructure projects, including the BSNL 4G network, BharatNet, and railway modernization, are expected to fuel demand for Tejas Networks' cutting-edge telecommunications solutions.

The report also highlights Tejas Networks' strategic advantage through its associations with major players like TCom and TCS. These collaborations are instrumental in building new client relationships and strengthening its market position. Additionally, the global trend of diversifying away from Chinese telecom equipment manufacturers presents a significant opportunity for Indian companies like Tejas Networks to step in and fill the void. Emkay Global projects a remarkable four-fold increase in Tejas Networks' revenue by FY25, primarily driven by the execution of the BSNL and BharatNet projects.

### Financial Forecasts Signal Robust Growth

The optimism surrounding Tejas Networks is further reflected in Emkay Global's revised financial projections. The firm has increased its revenue estimates for FY25 and FY26 by 6% and 7% respectively, acknowledging the strong performance and the anticipated surge in demand. Alongside the revenue uplift, Emkay Global has also raised its EBITDA margin forecast by an impressive 10 to 150 basis points, indicating improved profitability. This upward revision in financial targets is a testament to the company's operational efficiency and its ability to capitalize on market opportunities. The new target price of ₹1,100 per share, up from the previous ₹975, represents a compelling 21% upside potential, making Tejas Networks an attractive proposition for investors.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring the continued progress and execution of the BSNL 4G and BharatNet projects, which are expected to be major revenue drivers. The company's ability to secure new client wins and expand its global footprint, particularly in markets looking to diversify their telecom equipment suppliers, will also be crucial indicators of its future success. Furthermore, any updates on further government initiatives supporting domestic telecom manufacturing will be keenly observed.
