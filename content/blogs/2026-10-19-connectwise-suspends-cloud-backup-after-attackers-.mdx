## ConnectWise Halts Cloud Backup Amidst Security Breach

A cybersecurity incident has forced IT management giant ConnectWise to take drastic action, temporarily disabling key services and suspending its cloud backup offering. The company confirmed that attackers gained unauthorized access to application identities, impacting numerous Microsoft 365 tenants.

### Services Taken Offline

In response to the breach, ConnectWise swiftly moved to neutralize the threat by disabling seven of its own application identities. The most significantly impacted service is ConnectWise Cloud Backup, which has been suspended by revoking the necessary certificates for affected application connections. This measure effectively blocks any new authentication attempts through these compromised channels, preventing further unauthorized activity. While this action halts new backups and access to existing archives for restoration, ConnectWise assures that all previously stored backup data remains securely encrypted and has not been accessed by the attackers.

### The Nature of the Attack

The attackers targeted specific application identities within ConnectWise's system, gaining unauthorized access that allowed them to compromise customer Microsoft 365 environments. ConnectWise clarified that while the disabled connections prevent new logins, they do not undo any malicious changes already made within a compromised tenant. This means that any unauthorized permissions or credentials established by the threat actors will require separate investigation and remediation by affected customers. The company is working diligently to identify the full scope of the intrusion and ensure the integrity of its platform.

### What To Watch

ConnectWise is expected to provide further updates as its investigation progresses. Customers will likely be advised on specific steps they need to take to review their Microsoft 365 environments for any unauthorized modifications. The company's priority will be restoring full functionality to its cloud backup and management services while ensuring that robust security measures are in place to prevent future incidents.
