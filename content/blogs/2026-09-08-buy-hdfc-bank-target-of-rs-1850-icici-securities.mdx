## HDFC Bank: A Buy Signal from ICICI Securities?

Investors are keeping a close eye on HDFC Bank following a new report from ICICI Securities that has upgraded the stock to "Buy" with an ambitious price target. This optimistic outlook comes after the significant merger that has reshaped the banking giant's financial landscape.

### Navigating Post-Merger Adjustments

The integration of HDFC into HDFC Bank has brought about notable shifts in the bank's balance sheet. We're seeing a significant increase in the loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) and a decrease in the share of low-cost current account savings account (CASA) deposits, alongside a rise in borrowing. While the merger boosted the bank's loan market share substantially, its deposit market share growth has been more measured. ICICI Securities forecasts robust deposit growth, anticipating an annual increase of INR 4 to 5 trillion for the next two fiscal years. However, the bank is expected to prioritize a balanced LDR and manage its borrowing mix, leading to a revised loan growth estimate.

### Revised Estimates and a Positive Outlook

Despite the expected strong deposit accretion, ICICI Securities has slightly reduced its earnings per share (EPS) estimates for fiscal years 2025 and 2026. This adjustment is primarily due to the revised loan growth projections. Nonetheless, the report anticipates healthy return on assets (RoA) and return on equity (RoE) for the bank in the coming years. The target price of Rs 1,850 remains in place, and the recent dip in the stock price, down 10% year-to-date, has prompted the "Buy" recommendation. The brokerage suggests that a significant re-rating might take time as the bank navigates its growth strategy, balancing deposits with credit expansion and borrowing adjustments.

### What to Watch

Investors will be keen to observe how HDFC Bank manages its LDR and borrowing costs in the coming quarters. Future deposit growth figures and the pace of loan disbursements will be key indicators of the bank's progress in integrating its new structure and achieving its growth targets. The market will also be watching for any further clarity on the bank's strategic priorities as it moves beyond the initial merger phase.
