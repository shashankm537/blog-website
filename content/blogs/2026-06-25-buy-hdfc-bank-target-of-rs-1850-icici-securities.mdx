## ICICI Securities Flips the Switch: HDFC Bank Earns a "Buy" Rating

After a period of market scrutiny, a leading financial analyst is signaling renewed confidence in HDFC Bank. ICICI Securities has released a bullish report, recommending a "Buy" on the banking giant's stock with a significant price target. This shift comes as the bank navigates the complexities of its recent merger and aims to strengthen its financial footing.

### Navigating the Post-Merger Landscape

The integration following the HDFC-HDFC Bank merger has undoubtedly reshaped the bank's financial structure. Analysts highlight a notable increase in its loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) and a reduction in the share of low-cost current account savings account (CASA) deposits, alongside a rise in borrowings. While the merger has boosted the bank's loan market share considerably, deposit growth has lagged slightly behind, presenting a key area of focus for the institution.

ICICI Securities anticipates robust deposit growth in the coming years, projecting an annual increase of trillions of rupees. Despite this positive outlook, the bank is expected to prioritize a balanced approach to its LDR and the management of its borrowings. Consequently, loan growth estimates have been revised downwards, with an expectation for the LDR to normalize to more sustainable levels in the near future, indicating a strategic move towards better asset-liability management.

### A Closer Look at Earnings and Outlook

While the most recent quarterly earnings for Net Interest Margin (NIM) showed a slight improvement, analysts suggest this may have been influenced by seasonal factors. Following adjustments to growth estimates, ICICI Securities has marginally reduced its earnings per share (EPS) projections for the upcoming fiscal years. Nevertheless, the outlook remains positive, with expectations of healthy returns on assets (RoA) and return on equity (RoE) for HDFC Bank.

The analysts are maintaining their price target of Rs 1,850 for the stock, underscoring their belief in its underlying value. The recent dip in HDFC Bank's share price, down 10% year-to-date, has been a key factor in the upgrade to a "Buy" rating. The firm acknowledges that a full re-rating of the stock might take time, as the bank balances its strong deposit accretion with a potentially more measured pace of credit expansion compared to the broader market.

### What to Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring HDFC Bank's progress in managing its loan-to-deposit ratio and its success in attracting and retaining low-cost deposits. The bank's ability to navigate the competitive landscape and execute its borrowing substitution strategy will be crucial in driving future growth and profitability. The market will also be keen to see how the bank's earnings perform against the revised estimates in the coming quarters.
