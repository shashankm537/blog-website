## Lalithaa Jewellery Set for Stock Market Debut Amidst Strong Investor Demand

Jewellery lovers and market watchers are buzzing as Lalithaa Jewellery Mart prepares to list on the BSE and NSE today. Early indicators from the grey market suggest a confident start, with a significant premium hinting at robust investor interest following a massively oversubscribed initial public offering (IPO).

### A Grand Entrance: IPO Performance and Investor Enthusiasm

The Rs 1,700 crore IPO from Lalithaa Jewellery Mart has certainly captured the market's attention. When the subscription books closed on August 19th, the issue had been subscribed a remarkable 62.97 times, demonstrating a powerful appetite for the company's shares. Retail investors showed strong backing, with their portion seeing over 11 times subscription. However, it was the institutional investors who truly drove the demand. Non-Institutional Investors (NIIs) subscribed over 73 times, while Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs) placed bids for an astounding 145 times the shares allocated to them, signaling immense confidence in Lalithaa's future prospects.

### Strategic Vision: How IPO Funds Will Fuel Growth

Lalithaa Jewellery Mart has laid out a clear plan for the capital raised through its IPO. The company intends to utilize the Rs 1,200 crore from the fresh issue to strengthen its financial position and expand its operational capabilities. A significant portion of these funds is earmarked for working capital requirements, ensuring smooth day-to-day operations and inventory management. Additionally, capital expenditure for new store openings and upgrading existing facilities is a key priority, indicating a strategic focus on expanding its retail footprint and enhancing customer experience. The remaining Rs 500 crore will come from an offer for sale by promoter Kiran Kumar Jain.

### Value Proposition: Attractive Valuation in a Competitive Market

A crucial factor contributing to the strong investor interest appears to be Lalithaa Jewellery Mart's valuation. At the upper end of its Rs 190–201 price band, the company's price-to-earnings (P/E) ratio based on projected FY26 earnings is around 9.95 times. This valuation stands out when compared to its peers in the jewellery sector, whose average P/E ratio for FY26 hovers around 29.69 times. This suggests that Lalithaa Jewellery Mart is presenting an attractive entry point for investors seeking exposure to the burgeoning jewellery market, potentially offering significant upside.

#### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring the trading debut for any immediate price discovery and observing how the stock performs in the initial days of listing. The company's ability to execute its expansion plans and manage its working capital efficiently will be key to sustained growth and investor confidence. The market will also be watching the broader performance of the jewellery sector for any sector-specific tailwinds or headwinds.
