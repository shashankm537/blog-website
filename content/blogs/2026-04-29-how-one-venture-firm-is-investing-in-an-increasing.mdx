## Navigating a Divided World: Kompas VC Launches New Fund with a Regional Bet

In an era marked by increasing global fragmentation, one venture capital firm is charting a unique course. Kompas VC, with offices strategically placed across Europe and Tel Aviv, has secured a new €160 million fund to back startups navigating a world divided into distinct economic and political spheres. Their approach hinges on understanding and capitalizing on these regional differences rather than trying to find global unicorns.

### A World Divided: The Three Spheres Strategy

Kompas VC sees the global landscape as largely falling into three dominant spheres: the United States, Europe, and China. According to partner Sebastian Peck, these regions are increasingly charting "very, very different trajectories." This outlook directly informs their investment strategy, focusing on companies that can thrive within these distinct ecosystems, rather than those requiring immediate global scale.

The firm is doubling down on its commitment to what it calls "core industrial competitiveness challenges." This encompasses critical areas like advanced manufacturing, robust supply chains, essential infrastructure, and the burgeoning field of sustainability. While these themes remain universally important, their emphasis and the specific problems startups are solving vary significantly from one region to another, a nuance Kompas aims to exploit.

### Investing in the Physical World

Kompas VC is deliberately steering clear of the current hype cycle dominated by AI and explosive, purely digital growth. Instead, their focus is firmly planted in the "physical world," supporting startups that are actively involved in producing tangible goods. Their investment thesis centers on companies driving decarbonization efforts, boosting productivity in industrial sectors, and enhancing risk management within critical supply chains.

This deliberate niche allows Kompas to tap into a trend that resonates across their target regions: reshoring. As nations and blocs look to strengthen domestic production capabilities, startups addressing these needs, regardless of their specific market, often find ample scale to achieve significant growth. Kompas believes this focus on tangible innovation offers a more resilient and sustainable path to venture-scale returns in today's complex environment.

### What To Watch

With this new capital injection, expect Kompas VC to actively seek out and invest in European and Israeli startups at the forefront of industrial innovation. Their success will likely be measured by their ability to identify and nurture companies that can effectively cater to the specific industrial needs and geopolitical realities of their respective spheres. The firm's distinct regional focus could prove to be a prescient strategy as global divisions continue to shape economic landscapes.
