## Tonbo Imaging IPO Soars: Overwhelming Demand Signals Strong Market Confidence

Tonbo Imaging's Rs 852 crore initial public offering (IPO) has closed with an astonishing subscription rate, attracting bids for nearly 140 times the shares on offer. This overwhelming demand, particularly from institutional investors, suggests significant investor confidence in the defence technology firm's future prospects.

### A Phenomenal Response from Investors

The IPO, which opened for subscription on October 15th and closed on October 19th, saw bids for a staggering 1,158,691,464 shares against the 8,526,033 shares made available. Leading the charge were Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs), who subscribed their portion a remarkable 293.56 times, demonstrating a strong appetite for the company's offerings. Non-institutional investors also showed considerable interest, with their segment being subscribed 170.94 times, while the retail investor portion saw a healthy 31.06 times subscription.

### Glimmers of Success in the Grey Market

Adding to the positive sentiment, Tonbo Imaging's shares were trading at a significant premium in the grey market on the morning of October 20th. Reports indicate a premium of around 71.20%, potentially pointing to a listing price of Rs 1,201 against the IPO's upper price band of Rs 700. While these grey market premiums are unofficial indicators and not guaranteed, they often reflect early investor sentiment and can offer a glimpse into potential listing day performance.

### What to Watch

Investors will be keenly awaiting the finalisation of the basis of allotment, expected on October 21st. The company's shares are tentatively scheduled to debut on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) and the National Stock Exchange (NSE) on October 23rd. The focus will be on whether the strong subscription levels translate into a robust market debut for Tonbo Imaging.
