## HDFC Bank: A Buy Signal from ICICI Securities Amidst Post-Merger Shifts

Leading financial services firm ICICI Securities has issued a strong "Buy" recommendation for HDFC Bank, setting an ambitious target price of ₹1,850. This optimistic outlook comes as the banking giant navigates significant structural changes following its recent merger. Investors are keenly watching how these adjustments will impact the bank's future performance.

### Navigating the New Balance Sheet Landscape

The merger has undeniably reshaped HDFC Bank's financial foundation. Analysts are observing a notable increase in its loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR), a dip in the proportion of low-cost current and savings accounts (CASA), and a rise in overall borrowing. While the merger has propelled the bank's loan market share significantly, its deposit market share growth has been more measured. ICICI Securities projects robust deposit growth, estimating an 18% compound annual growth rate (CAGR) for the period between FY24 and FY26, which translates to an absolute increase of ₹4-5 trillion annually.

### Growth Projections and Revised Estimates

Despite strong deposit accretion, ICICI Securities anticipates HDFC Bank will maintain a balanced approach to its LDR and explore borrowing substitution strategies. Consequently, their loan growth forecast has been adjusted downwards to a 13% CAGR for FY24-26, with expectations for the LDR to ease to around 100% by FY25 and 96% by FY26. The firm's analysis suggests that the incremental LDR will likely fall within the 74-75% range. Deposit growth remains a critical factor influencing both overall expansion and the net interest margin (NIM).

### Upgraded to "Buy" After Stock Correction

While the fourth quarter of FY24 saw a slightly better-than-expected NIM, this was potentially influenced by seasonal factors. Taking into account the revised growth estimates, ICICI Securities has trimmed its Earnings Per Share (EPS) projections for FY25 and FY26 by 5%. Nevertheless, the firm expects HDFC Bank to achieve a healthy Return on Assets (RoA) of 1.7% and a Return on Equity (RoE) of 14-15% for the same periods. Importantly, the target price of ₹1,850 remains unchanged. The upgrade to a "Buy" rating is directly attributed to the recent sharp correction in HDFC Bank's stock price, which has seen a 10% year-to-date decline. The re-rating is expected to be gradual, as credit growth may trail the system, and borrowing substitution efforts will unfold over time.

### What To Watch

Investors will be monitoring HDFC Bank's ability to effectively manage its LDR and CASA ratios post-merger. The pace of deposit and credit growth, alongside the bank's success in optimizing its borrowing mix, will be key indicators to watch for sustained profitability and the realization of ICICI Securities' ambitious target price.
