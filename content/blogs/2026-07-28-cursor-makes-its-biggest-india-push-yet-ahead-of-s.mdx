## AI Coding Assistant Cursor Rolls Out India-Specific Pricing

AI-powered coding startup Cursor is making a significant play in the Indian market, launching a localized subscription plan just weeks before its anticipated acquisition by SpaceX. This move signals a strategic bet on India’s burgeoning developer community to fuel the company's future expansion.

### Tailored for the Indian Market

Cursor has introduced "Cursor Start," a new subscription tier priced at ₹649 (approximately $7) per month, specifically designed for Indian developers. This offering comes at a considerably lower price point than Cursor's standard $20-a-month Pro subscription, demonstrating a clear understanding of local market dynamics and affordability. The decision to localize pricing reflects the immense growth Cursor has seen in India, which is already its third-largest global market. The company reports a more than threefold increase in its Indian user base over the past year, highlighting the significant concentration of "power users" in the region.

### Tapping into India's Engineering Prowess

Simon Green, Cursor's Head of Asia-Pacific and Japan, emphasized that India's deep pool of software engineering talent made it the ideal first market for this pricing localization. The move is aimed at adapting their commercial model to better suit the market and foster further growth. With an estimated 27 million developers on platforms like GitHub, India stands as a global powerhouse in software development, second only to the United States. This vast and growing talent pool makes India a natural and vital market for tools that enhance developer productivity.

The Cursor Start plan provides enhanced access to AI coding capabilities, including the Composer 2.5 and Grok 4.5 models, with increased usage limits compared to the free tier. It also offers features like cloud agents, an iOS app, plugins, and support for advanced protocols. This plan is positioned to serve developers who require more AI assistance than the free version offers but may not need the full suite of features in the premium Pro subscription.

### What To Watch

With the SpaceX acquisition on the horizon, this strategic pricing move in India could be an early indicator of how Cursor plans to leverage its new resources and global reach. Investors and developers will be keen to see how this localized approach impacts user acquisition and engagement in one of the world's most dynamic tech landscapes.
