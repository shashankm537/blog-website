## HDFC Bank: ICICI Securities Sees a Buying Opportunity with a Bold Price Target

HDFC Bank's stock has been under pressure, but one leading financial services firm believes it's time for investors to take another look. ICICI Securities has issued a strong "Buy" recommendation, setting an ambitious price target of Rs 1,850, signaling confidence in the banking giant's future performance following its recent significant merger.

### Navigating the Post-Merger Landscape

The integration of HDFC into HDFC Bank has undoubtedly reshaped the bank's financial structure. We're seeing a notable shift with a higher loan-to-deposit ratio and a reduced proportion of low-cost current account savings account (CASA) deposits, offset by an increased reliance on borrowings. This strategic move has significantly boosted the bank's market share in loans, adding a substantial 330 basis points to reach 14.5%. While deposit growth has also seen an increase, it hasn't matched the pace of loan expansion, with a 110 basis point rise year-on-year to 11.0%.

### Growth Projections and Key Variables

Despite the structural changes, ICICI Securities forecasts robust deposit growth for HDFC Bank, estimating an 18% compound annual growth rate from FY24 to FY26, translating to an absolute rise of Rs 4 to 5 trillion annually. Even with this strong deposit accretion, the bank is expected to prioritize a balanced loan-to-deposit ratio and manage its borrowing mix. Consequently, loan growth projections have been adjusted downwards to a 13% CAGR for the same period, with the loan-to-deposit ratio anticipated to ease to 100% in FY25 and 96% in FY26, down from the current 104%. The incremental loan-to-deposit ratio is estimated to be around 74-75%, indicating a more measured approach to lending. Ultimately, the pace of deposit growth and its impact on net interest margins (NIMs) will be critical factors to monitor.

### Upgrade to Buy Amidst Stock Correction

While the fourth quarter of FY24 saw a slightly better-than-expected NIM, this may have been influenced by seasonal factors. ICICI Securities has revised its earnings per share (EPS) estimates downwards by 5% for both FY25 and FY26, primarily due to the reduced growth forecasts. However, the firm anticipates healthy return on assets (RoA) of 1.7% and return on equity (RoE) of 14-15% for FY25-26. The recent 10% year-to-date correction in HDFC Bank's stock price has prompted ICICI Securities to upgrade its rating to "Buy." The research report suggests that while a re-rating of the stock is likely to be gradual, the bank's strong deposit growth and its strategic adjustments in the face of system-wide credit growth and borrowing substitution present a compelling investment case.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely observing HDFC Bank's ability to manage its deposit growth effectively and its strategy for balancing credit expansion with borrowing costs. The progress of its post-merger integration and its impact on net interest margins will also be key indicators of its future performance.
