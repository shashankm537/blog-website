## Beyond the Usual Suspects: Identifying India's Next Growth Engines

As India's stock market navigates a more nuanced landscape, a shift is underway, moving beyond established giants like IT and banking. Jitendra Gohil, CIO of Listed Equities at Bajaj Alts, is pinpointing emerging sectors poised for significant expansion, suggesting a new breed of growth leaders is on the horizon.

### Sunrise Sectors Lighting Up the Investment Horizon

Gohil's radar is locked onto industries that are still in their nascent stages of a powerful growth trajectory. He highlights electronics manufacturing, renewable energy sources like solar, and the crucial areas of power equipment and battery technology as prime investment candidates. These sectors are not just trending; they represent fundamental shifts in India's economic development and energy independence. Furthermore, he sees potential in waste management, a sector critical for sustainable urban growth and resource optimization.

### Navigating the Opportunities: Defence, Infrastructure, and Beyond

Beyond the immediate sunrise industries, Gohil also identifies compelling opportunities within defence, shipbuilding, infrastructure development, and the aerospace sector. These areas are indicative of India's growing strategic importance and its ambition to become a global manufacturing and innovation hub. While acknowledging the promise, Gohil offers a note of caution regarding valuations, urging investors to be discerning about the price they are willing to pay for these future growth stories.

### A Resilient Economy Navigating Global Tides

India's economic resilience is proving to be a significant, and perhaps underappreciated, strength in the current global climate. Despite geopolitical tensions, such as the conflict between Iran and the USA, India has managed to keep inflation in check and its bond yields have remained relatively stable. This stability stands in stark contrast to the significant surges seen in major global economies, underscoring India's effective economic management. Strong domestic indicators across credit growth, tax collections, and retail sales paint a positive picture for the latter half of the year, suggesting a potentially stronger performance for Indian equities compared to the first half.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely watching the actual earnings performance of these identified growth sectors and how efficiently companies can translate their potential into profitability. The influence of global capital flows, driven by geopolitical events, will also remain a key factor to monitor, potentially impacting the pace of domestic market development.
