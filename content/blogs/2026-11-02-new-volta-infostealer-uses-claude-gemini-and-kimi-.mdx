## New "VOLTA" Malware Smarts Up Cybercrime with AI-Powered Victim Valuation

Cybercriminals are taking their tactics to the next level with a new strain of Windows malware dubbed VOLTA. This sophisticated threat doesn't just steal data; it uses advanced artificial intelligence to assess the financial worth of its victims, making targeted attacks even more lucrative for attackers.

### The "Evidence Bundle" of Your Digital Life

Researchers have uncovered that VOLTA goes beyond simply pilfering passwords and payment card details. The malware meticulously collects a comprehensive "evidence bundle" from infected machines. This bundle paints a detailed picture of the victim, including their geographic location, employer, estimated birth year, and even indicators of financial activity through things like iPhone backup data and live Roblox account valuations. It's designed to build a structured profile, making it easier for attackers to understand who they're dealing with and how much they might be worth.

### AI as the Cybercriminal's Accomplice

What truly sets VOLTA apart is its innovative use of large language models (LLMs). After gathering a wealth of personal and financial information, the malware sends this data to an AI system for analysis. It leverages a service that can access multiple AI models, including Anthropic's Claude, Google's Gemini, and Moonshot AI's Kimi. This allows VOLTA to prioritize its victims, identifying those with the highest potential for financial gain by cross-referencing stolen data with information gleaned from sources like Gmail receipts from major online retailers. The AI is even programmed to navigate potential refusals from one model by seamlessly switching to another, ensuring a valuable assessment is always delivered.

### What To Watch

Expect to see attackers refining this AI-driven approach to data valuation. As cybersecurity firms develop defenses against VOLTA, threat actors will likely experiment with other LLMs and potentially adapt their data collection methods to bypass emerging security measures. The integration of AI into malware is a significant evolution in the cybercrime landscape, and VOLTA serves as a stark warning of what's to come.
