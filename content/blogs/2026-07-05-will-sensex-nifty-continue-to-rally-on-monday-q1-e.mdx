## Market Momentum: Will the Rally Continue This Week?

Indian stock markets closed last week on a strong footing, with both the Sensex and Nifty posting gains of nearly 0.9%. A powerful surge in the final three trading days not only erased earlier losses but also pushed the benchmark indices to new highs, adding significant wealth to investors. This week, a combination of corporate earnings and geopolitical stability could dictate whether this upward trend persists.

### The Earnings Season Kicks Off

The much-anticipated first-quarter earnings season is about to commence, with tech giant Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) set to declare its April-June quarter results on July 9th. This announcement will officially launch the reporting cycle for the IT sector and other major companies. Analysts are closely watching these corporate results as a key determinant of market direction. Positive earnings reports could further fuel investor confidence and support the ongoing rally.

### Geopolitical Calm Calms Markets

A significant factor contributing to the recent market optimism appears to be a period of relative calm on the geopolitical front. Reports indicate that peace efforts in the Middle East are holding steady, with no new escalations reported. This stability, following diplomatic engagements, has provided a much-needed breather for global markets. While oil prices saw a minor uptick, they remain close to pre-conflict levels, suggesting that immediate supply concerns have eased.

### What To Watch

Investors will be keenly observing the Q1 earnings of major companies, particularly from the IT sector, for signs of sustained growth and profitability. The ongoing diplomatic developments and their impact on global stability will also be a crucial watch point. Any shifts in geopolitical tensions or unexpected earnings surprises could influence market sentiment throughout the week.
