## Tejas Networks Soars on Strong Performance: Emkay Global Financial Sees Major Upside

Tejas Networks has caught the eye of financial analysts, with Emkay Global Financial issuing a strong "Buy" recommendation and projecting a significant price target. This optimism stems from the company's recent performance and its strategic positioning within India's booming telecommunications sector.

### Powering Progress: BSNL's 4G Project Fuels Growth

A key driver behind Emkay's bullish outlook is Tejas Networks' impressive execution of BSNL's 4G project. The company has not only met but exceeded revenue and margin expectations, demonstrating its capability to deliver on large-scale national infrastructure initiatives. This success underscores Tejas Networks' role in bolstering India's digital connectivity.

Beyond the BSNL project, Emkay highlights several other factors propelling Tejas Networks forward. The Indian government's strong push for domestic manufacturing and the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme provide a fertile ground for local players. Furthermore, substantial investments in projects like BharatNet and the Railways' network upgrades, coupled with strategic partnerships and client references from major players like TCom and TCS, are opening new avenues for business. The global trend of diversifying away from Chinese telecom equipment also presents a significant opportunity for Indian manufacturers like Tejas Networks to capture market share.

### Analyst Forecasts Signal a Bright Future

Building on the strong performance and favorable market dynamics, Emkay Global Financial has revised its price target for Tejas Networks to ₹1,100 per share. This represents a substantial 21% potential upside from current levels. The firm has also increased its revenue forecasts for FY25 and FY26, anticipating that the successful execution of the BSNL and BharatNet projects will more than quadruple FY24 revenues. Alongside revenue growth, Emkay expects an improvement in EBITDA margins, further enhancing profitability.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring Tejas Networks' continued progress on its ongoing projects and the company's ability to secure new contracts. The expansion of 5G infrastructure across India and the potential for increased exports are also key areas to observe. The competitive landscape and evolving technological advancements within the telecom sector will also shape the company's trajectory.
