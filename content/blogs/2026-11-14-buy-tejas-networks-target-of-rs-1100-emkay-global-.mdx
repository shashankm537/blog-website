## Tejas Networks Soars on Strong Growth Prospects: Emkay Global Financial Says "Buy"

Tejas Networks is catching the eye of financial analysts, with Emkay Global Financial issuing a bullish report forecasting significant growth and a strong upside potential. The firm has initiated a "Buy" rating on the stock, setting an ambitious target price of Rs 1,100. This optimistic outlook stems from the company's recent performance and its strategic positioning within key Indian telecommunications initiatives.

### Riding the Wave of Domestic Manufacturing and BSNL's 4G Push

A major driver behind Emkay Global's confidence is Tejas Networks' impressive execution on the BSNL 4G project, which has surpassed revenue and margin expectations. The Indian government's strong emphasis on domestic manufacturing, coupled with incentives like the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme, creates a fertile ground for companies like Tejas. Furthermore, substantial investments in national broadband initiatives such as BharatNet and railway communication projects provide a consistent demand pipeline for the company's solutions.

The company is also benefiting from strong endorsements and references from major players like Tata Communications (TCom) and Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), which are opening doors to new clients and expanding its market reach. This is particularly crucial as the global trend shifts towards diversifying away from Chinese telecom equipment, creating a significant opportunity for Indian manufacturers to step in and fill the void.

### Doubling Down on Growth: Revised Targets and Enhanced Margins

Looking ahead, Emkay Global anticipates a substantial revenue surge for Tejas Networks in fiscal year 2025, potentially quadrupling its fiscal year 2024 revenue. This aggressive forecast is primarily attributed to the continued execution on the BSNL and BharatNet projects. To reflect this positive trajectory, Emkay has revised its revenue estimates upwards for both FY25 and FY26.

Moreover, the firm has also raised its EBITDA margin projections, indicating improved operational efficiency and profitability. The revised target price of Rs 1,100 per share, up from the previous Rs 975, represents an attractive 21% upside potential based on their discounted cash flow (DCF) valuation methodology. Emkay Global maintains its conviction in the company's long-term prospects, reinforcing its "Buy" recommendation.

### What to Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring Tejas Networks' continued progress on large-scale government projects and its ability to secure new contracts, particularly as the global telecom landscape continues to evolve. The company's success in leveraging domestic manufacturing policies and expanding its client base will be key indicators of its sustained growth trajectory.
