## Tech Mahindra Stock Sees Profit-Taking After Impressive Surge: What's Next for Investors?

Tech Mahindra's shares experienced a dip on Monday, as investors cashed in on significant gains following a robust Q2 earnings report. The IT major had previously seen a remarkable 22% climb in just four days, but a market-wide sentiment shift and profit-booking have led to a minor correction. This begs the question: what does this mean for shareholders and potential buyers?

### A Q2 Performance That Sparked Excitement

The recent rally was fueled by Tech Mahindra's strong financial performance in the second quarter of FY27. The company announced a significant jump in consolidated net profit, soaring by 32.7% year-on-year to Rs 1,585.6 crore. This was complemented by a healthy 15.6% rise in revenue from operations, reaching Rs 16,180.2 crore. Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization (EBITDA) also saw a substantial increase of 32.8%, indicating improved operational efficiency and profitability.

### Technical Indicators and Analyst Views

Technical analysis suggests that while the recent surge has led to some profit-taking, the underlying bullish momentum might still be intact. According to Sudeep Shah, Vice President and Head of Technical & Derivatives Research at SBI Securities, Tech Mahindra has successfully broken past a key resistance level. He points to the Relative Strength Index (RSI) and MACD indicators showing an upward trend, signaling strengthening buying interest. Shah suggests that the Rs 1,690-1,700 range could now serve as crucial support, and the stock may continue its upward trajectory if it holds above this zone.

However, not all analysts are equally optimistic in the short term. Nomura, for instance, maintains a "Neutral" stance on Tech Mahindra, forecasting no immediate upside. Despite this, Nomura acknowledges the company's potential for growth, citing the impact of recent large telecom deal wins and expansion in other business segments. The firm also notes that Tech Mahindra's target of achieving an average EBIT margin of 15% in FY27 remains on track, driven by AI-powered productivity gains and ongoing operational improvements.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring Tech Mahindra's ability to maintain its footing above the Rs 1,700 support level in the coming trading sessions. Any sustained upward movement beyond the recent highs, coupled with continued positive sentiment around the IT sector, could reignite the buying momentum. Conversely, a breach of this support could signal further consolidation or a deeper correction. The market will also be looking for more concrete evidence of margin expansion initiatives translating into tangible results, as highlighted by the company's management.
