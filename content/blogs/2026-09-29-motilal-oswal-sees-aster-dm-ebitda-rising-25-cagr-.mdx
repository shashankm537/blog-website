# Aster DM Healthcare Poised for Strong Growth Following Landmark Merger

**Motilal Oswal initiates coverage with a 'Buy' rating, forecasting significant earnings uplift and strategic expansion.**

The healthcare sector is buzzing with optimism for Aster DM Healthcare, following a recent brokerage report highlighting substantial growth potential. Motilal Oswal, a prominent financial services firm, has launched its coverage of the company with a 'Buy' recommendation and a price target of Rs 910, signaling a potential upside of up to 22%. This positive outlook is largely driven by the recent strategic merger with Quality Care India, a move expected to unlock significant synergies and drive robust earnings growth.

### Synergy Unlocked: The Power of Consolidation

The integration of Aster DM Healthcare with Quality Care India, effective July 1, 2026, is a game-changer for the combined entity. This amalgamation brings together Aster, CARE Hospitals, KIMSHEALTH, and Evercare, creating a formidable network of 39 hospitals spread across 28 cities. With approximately 10,900 beds currently available and 10,559 operational, the expanded scale provides a strong foundation for future success. Beyond the sheer size, the merger is anticipated to generate considerable efficiencies through centralized procurement, optimized supply chains, shared clinical expertise, and rationalization of corporate costs. Management estimates these initiatives alone could boost EBITDA by a remarkable 10-15%.

### Early Indicators Point to Enhanced Performance

The initial financial results following the merger are already painting a promising picture. For the first quarter of fiscal year 2027, the combined entity reported a substantial 20% year-on-year increase in revenue, reaching Rs 26 billion. Equally impressive, EBITDA saw a robust 30% surge to Rs 5.8 billion, with EBITDA margins expanding by 170 basis points to a healthy 22.2%. This improvement is underpinned by a 13% rise in patient volumes and a notable 510 basis point increase in occupancy rates, which now stand at 64%. These early metrics clearly demonstrate the positive impact of the consolidation and the growing operational efficiency.

### Ambitious Expansion Fuels Future Growth

Adding to the optimism, the merged entity is embarking on an aggressive capacity expansion program. Plans are in motion to add over 4,150 new beds, pushing the total capacity beyond the 15,000-bed mark. A significant portion of these planned additions, more than half, will come from brownfield projects, indicating a strategic approach to leveraging existing infrastructure and minimizing new development risks. Motilal Oswal forecasts that this strategic expansion, coupled with ongoing operational improvements, will drive revenue, EBITDA, and PAT to grow at impressive CAGRs of 19.5%, 25%, and 33% respectively, over the fiscal years 2026 to 2028.

### What To Watch

Investors will be keenly observing the continued integration of the merged entities and the successful execution of the ambitious expansion plans. The market will also be focused on how effectively the company capitalizes on the projected synergies to further enhance its EBITDA margins and overall profitability. Tracking patient volumes and occupancy rates in the coming quarters will be crucial in assessing the sustained demand for Aster DM Healthcare's services.
