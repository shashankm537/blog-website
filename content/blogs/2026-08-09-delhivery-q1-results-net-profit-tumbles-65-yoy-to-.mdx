## Delhivery Navigates Challenging Q1: Profit Dips Amidst Revenue Surge

Logistics giant Delhivery has reported a significant drop in net profit for the first quarter of the fiscal year, even as its revenue continues to climb. The company's latest financial results paint a picture of a business navigating a turbulent operational landscape while still demonstrating robust top-line growth.

### Profit Takes a Hit, but Revenue Soars

Delhivery's net profit for the first quarter of FY27 saw a substantial decline of 65% year-on-year, settling at Rs 32 crore. This comes despite a healthy 28% increase in revenue from operations, which reached Rs 2,930.7 crore. While the profit dip is notable, the consistent revenue growth signals sustained demand for the company's logistics services.

### External Headwinds Impacting Margins

The company attributed the profit squeeze to a confluence of challenging external factors. Volatile labour availability, exacerbated by election periods and climate-related disruptions, alongside geopolitical uncertainties and shifts in labor codes, created significant operational hurdles. These issues necessitated additional investments in buffer staff and network capacity to maintain service standards, ultimately impacting the bottom line. Furthermore, rising global crude oil prices translated into higher fuel costs, a factor that, while partially offset by customer contract pass-through mechanisms, contributed to the profit decline.

### Looking Ahead: Stability and Growth on the Horizon

Despite the first-quarter pressures, Delhivery anticipates that the increased costs will be absorbed by its ongoing revenue expansion throughout the rest of the fiscal year. The company is also seeing the full effect of contractual revisions related to fuel costs beginning to materialize in the second quarter. Additionally, adjustments to statutory minimum wages in key states are expected to be integrated into the company's operational model.

### What To Watch

Investors and industry observers will be keen to see how Delhivery manages its cost efficiencies in the coming quarters, particularly as it continues to scale its operations. The company's ability to translate its strong revenue growth into improved profitability will be a key indicator of its resilience and strategic execution. The impact of recently implemented wage adjustments and the full realization of fuel cost pass-throughs will also be closely monitored in the next earnings report.
