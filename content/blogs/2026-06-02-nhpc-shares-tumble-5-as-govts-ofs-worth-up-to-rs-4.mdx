# NHPC Shares Dip as Government Launches Significant Stake Sale

Investor confidence took a hit Tuesday as shares of the state-owned hydropower giant NHPC tumbled by nearly 5%. This decline follows the government's initiation of an Offer for Sale (OFS), looking to offload a substantial portion of its holdings in the company at a discounted price.

## Government's Divestment Strategy Unfolds

The Indian government has launched an ambitious plan to reduce its stake in NHPC, marking a significant move in its divestment strategy. The OFS, which opened for institutional investors on Tuesday, offers up to a 6% equity stake. This sale, priced at Rs 71 per share, represents an approximately 8% discount to the previous day's closing price, a factor that has clearly unsettled market participants. The government initially proposed selling 3% of its stake, with an option to divest an additional 3% if demand is strong, potentially raising over Rs 4,279 crore.

## Market Reaction and Investor Participation

The market responded swiftly to the news, with NHPC's stock price reacting negatively to the discount offered. The OFS will be accessible to retail investors, eligible employees, and those carrying forward previous bids on Wednesday. This phased approach allows for broader participation while the initial discount aims to attract significant interest. The government currently holds a substantial majority stake in NHPC, exceeding 67% as of the end of March 2026, and this sale is being conducted through a separate window mechanism on major stock exchanges, adhering to SEBI's guidelines.

## What to Watch

Attention will now turn to the participation levels during the retail investor window. The government's decision to exercise its oversubscription option will be a key indicator of investor appetite for NHPC stock at the current price points. Investors will also be closely monitoring the stock's performance in the coming days as the OFS concludes and the market digests the implications of this large-scale divestment.
