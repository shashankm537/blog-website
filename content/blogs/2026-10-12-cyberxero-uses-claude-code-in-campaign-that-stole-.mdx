## AI-Powered Cyberattack Leverages Claude Code to Steal Hundreds of Thousands of Ukrainian Records

A sophisticated cyberattack, dubbed "CyberXero," is making headlines after researchers uncovered its dual-pronged approach to breaching systems and stealing vast amounts of data. This malicious operation cleverly combines traditional hacking tools with advanced artificial intelligence, specifically leveraging Anthropic's Claude Code, to target organizations across Ukraine and beyond. The breach has already resulted in the compromise of over 628,000 Ukrainian personal records, raising serious concerns about data security.

### A Double Threat: Global Reach and Targeted Assault

CyberXero is not a one-trick pony. Researchers from SOCRadar have detailed a two-part strategy employed by this threat actor, identified as a Russian-speaking, financially motivated entity. The first, broader campaign automates attacks against widely used platforms like WordPress and e-commerce sites. This global effort focuses on exploiting known vulnerabilities within these systems, aiming to gain initial access and pilfer sensitive information, including payment data. Simultaneously, a more precise and manual campaign is underway, specifically targeting Ukraine's energy sector and critical infrastructure. This second pipeline is geared towards deep reconnaissance and opportunistic exploitation, suggesting a more strategic objective beyond simple data theft.

### The AI Advantage: Claude Code Fuels the Attack

What sets CyberXero apart is its integration of artificial intelligence. The operation utilizes Anthropic's Claude Code as an orchestration layer, augmenting its offensive capabilities. This AI integration likely allows the attackers to automate tasks, analyze potential targets more effectively, and adapt their attack methods with greater speed and precision. The discovery of leaked AI session logs, scripts with sensitive tokens, and exfiltrated victim data from an exposed server provided crucial insights into the technical underpinnings of this advanced operation. This sophisticated use of AI underscores a growing trend in cybercrime, where sophisticated tools are increasingly employed for malicious purposes.

### What To Watch

The ongoing nature of the CyberXero campaign means vigilance is paramount. Organizations, particularly those running WordPress or e-commerce platforms, should prioritize patching known vulnerabilities and strengthening their security posture. For Ukraine, the focused targeting of critical infrastructure warrants heightened defensive measures and intelligence sharing among relevant agencies. The continued evolution of AI in cyber warfare suggests that future attacks may become even more complex and difficult to detect.
