## Tejas Networks Sees Bullish Outlook: Emkay Global Financial Raises Target Price to Rs 1100

A leading financial services firm has signaled strong confidence in Tejas Networks, recommending a "buy" on the company's stock. This positive assessment comes on the heels of robust performance and a promising outlook driven by significant government initiatives and global telecom trends.

### Driving Growth: BSNL's 4G and Domestic Manufacturing Push

Tejas Networks has impressed analysts, particularly with its execution on BSNL's 4G project, which exceeded revenue and margin expectations. The company is well-positioned to capitalize on the Indian government's focus on boosting domestic manufacturing through schemes like the Production Linked Incentive (PLI). Furthermore, substantial investments planned for crucial projects like BSNL, BharatNet, and the Railways are expected to fuel considerable demand for Tejas Networks' solutions.

The company is also benefiting from strong endorsements, with references from major players like TCom and TCS paving the way for securing new clients. In the international arena, the global shift away from Chinese telecom equipment provides a significant tailwind for Indian manufacturers like Tejas Networks, opening up new market opportunities. Analysts project a substantial revenue surge for FY25, potentially quadrupling from FY24 levels, driven by the ongoing execution of BSNL and BharatNet projects.

### Revised Price Target Reflects Strong Growth Potential

In light of these positive developments and the company's strong performance, Emkay Global Financial has revised its target price for Tejas Networks upward. The new target stands at Rs 1100 per share, a notable increase from the previous Rs 975, representing a potential upside of 21%. This optimistic valuation is based on a discounted cash flow (DCF) methodology, reflecting confidence in the company's long-term growth trajectory and profitability. The research report, dated April 23, 2024, reiterates the "BUY" recommendation, underscoring the firm's conviction in Tejas Networks' future prospects.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring Tejas Networks' continued execution on large-scale government projects and its ability to leverage the growing global demand for non-Chinese telecom infrastructure. The company's success in securing new client references and expanding its market share will be key indicators of its sustained growth.
