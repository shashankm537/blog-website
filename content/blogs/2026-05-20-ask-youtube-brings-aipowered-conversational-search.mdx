## YouTube Gets an AI Brain Boost: Conversational Search and Smarter Shorts Incoming

Get ready for a smarter YouTube. Google is injecting powerful AI into its video giant, transforming how we find content with conversational search and supercharging the creation of short-form videos with its cutting-edge Gemini AI.

### "Ask YouTube": Your AI-Powered Video Navigator

Tired of sifting through endless search results? YouTube is rolling out "Ask YouTube," an AI-driven feature designed to answer your more complex questions directly. Imagine asking for detailed tips on teaching a child to ride a bike or seeking creator reviews of cozy games before bed – and getting a concise, AI-generated answer compiled from both Shorts and longer videos. This intelligent system will even allow for follow-up questions, helping you zero in on exactly what you're looking for without endless scrolling. Currently, U.S. Premium subscribers on desktop can get an early look at this enhanced search experience.

### Gemini Omni Arrives: Revolutionizing Shorts Creation

The AI revolution is also hitting YouTube Shorts. Google's advanced AI video model, Gemini Omni, is being integrated into YouTube Shorts Remix and the YouTube Create app. This powerful tool promises to elevate video creation by better understanding user intent, leading to more coherent and engaging storytelling. Behind the scenes, Gemini Omni will handle intricate video and audio adjustments, empowering creators to build on each other's ideas in entirely new ways. While other platforms have seen varied success with AI-generated content, YouTube appears to be integrating this technology with a more measured approach.

### Protecting Creators in the Age of AI

As AI-generated content becomes more prevalent, YouTube is also enhancing its tools to protect creators. The platform is expanding its likeness-detection tool to creators aged 18 and older. This feature aims to combat deepfakes by allowing creators to identify and request the removal of AI-generated content that misrepresents them. The effectiveness of this expanded tool will be closely watched as it rolls out more broadly.

#### What To Watch

Keep an eye on how quickly "Ask YouTube" rolls out to more users and platforms. We'll also be looking for early examples of how Gemini Omni is being used to create innovative and compelling Shorts content, and how effective YouTube's likeness-detection tool proves to be in safeguarding creators.
