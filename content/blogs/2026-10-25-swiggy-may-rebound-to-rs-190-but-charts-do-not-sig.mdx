## Swiggy Stock: Eyeing a Rebound, But Trend Reversal Still Uncertain

Investors in Swiggy are hoping for a brighter future for the food delivery giant's stock, with some technical indicators suggesting a potential bounce back. However, seasoned analysts caution that while a short-term recovery might be on the cards, the underlying trend remains uncertain and a definitive shift in market sentiment hasn't materialized yet.

### Technical Signals and Potential Upside

Following its listing, Swiggy's stock has experienced a noticeable decline, consistently making lower highs and lower lows on its chart. This pattern suggests a bearish momentum has been in play. Yet, current conditions indicate the stock may be oversold, which could trigger a temporary rebound. Analysts are watching for a potential move towards the Rs 190 mark, a level that could offer some respite to investors.

Despite this potential for a short-term uptick, the broader chart structure does not yet confirm a significant change in the stock's trajectory. The absence of a clear trend reversal means that any gains might be temporary, with the risk of further downside still present if the market sentiment doesn't fundamentally shift.

### What the Charts Aren't Showing

While a rebound to Rs 190 is within the realm of possibility, the technical analyst at LKP Securities, Vatsal Bhuva, emphasizes that the current chart setup doesn't signal a true trend reversal. This means that while the stock might temporarily move upwards, the underlying downward pressure could persist. The market is looking for more substantial evidence of renewed buying interest and a change in the overall market sentiment to confirm a lasting recovery.

The continued formation of lower highs and lower lows is a key indicator that the bearish trend remains intact. Until this pattern is broken and replaced with a series of higher highs and higher lows, investors should exercise caution.

### What to Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring Swiggy's stock performance in the coming days, paying particular attention to whether it can sustain any upward momentum and break its current bearish pattern. Key resistance levels will need to be overcome for any significant trend reversal to be confirmed, while a failure to hold support could signal further declines. The overall market sentiment towards tech and growth stocks will also play a crucial role.
