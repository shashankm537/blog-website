## Tejas Networks Sees Bullish Outlook from Emkay Global Financial, Rs 1100 Target Set

Leading financial services firm Emkay Global Financial has initiated coverage on Tejas Networks with a strong "Buy" recommendation and an ambitious price target of Rs 1100. This optimistic outlook comes on the heels of Tejas Networks' robust financial performance, particularly its successful execution of the BSNL 4G project, which has exceeded expectations.

### Strong Fundamentals Driving Growth

Emkay Global Financial's report highlights several key factors contributing to their bullish stance on Tejas Networks. The Indian government's strong push for domestic manufacturing, coupled with incentives like the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme, significantly benefits companies like Tejas Networks. Furthermore, substantial investments by government entities in crucial infrastructure projects such as BSNL, BharatNet, and the Railways are expected to fuel sustained demand for Tejas Networks' telecommunications solutions. The company also stands to gain from strategic partnerships and references from major players like Tata Communications and TCS, which are instrumental in acquiring new clients and expanding its market reach.

### Global Shifts and Future Projections

Beyond domestic drivers, the global trend of diversifying away from Chinese telecom equipment suppliers presents a significant opportunity for Tejas Networks. This geopolitical shift is creating fertile ground for Indian companies to step in and capture market share. Emkay Global Financial anticipates an impressive revenue surge for Tejas Networks in FY25, potentially quadrupling its FY24 revenue, primarily driven by the ongoing execution of the BSNL and BharatNet projects. The firm has also revised its revenue estimates upward for FY25 and FY26, reflecting the strong performance beat and improved margins. Consequently, the earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA) margin is also projected to see a healthy increase.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring the continued progress of the BSNL 4G rollout and the execution pace of the BharatNet project. The company's ability to secure new client wins, especially through its existing partnerships, will be another critical indicator of its growth trajectory. Any further positive developments regarding the global shift in telecom equipment sourcing could also provide a significant boost to Tejas Networks' market position and valuation.
