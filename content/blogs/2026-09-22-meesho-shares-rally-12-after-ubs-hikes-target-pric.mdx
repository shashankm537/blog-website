## Meesho Soars on UBS Upgrade: Analysts Bullish on Growth and Profitability

Indian e-commerce platform Meesho is experiencing a significant surge in its stock, climbing as much as 12% after a major international brokerage firm, UBS, substantially increased its price target. This optimistic outlook from analysts suggests strong future performance, driven by key operational improvements and expanding user base.

### A Boost from UBS: Target Price Hike Signals Confidence

The market reacted enthusiastically to UBS's upgraded target price for Meesho, raising it by a considerable 24% to Rs 260. This move signals a strong belief in the company's growth trajectory and profitability potential. UBS has also revised its Net Merchandise Value (NMV) estimates upwards for the coming years, alongside a healthy increase in contribution profit and EBITDA projections. This enhanced financial outlook is directly linked to Meesho's impressive expansion in both seller and buyer numbers.

### Driving Forces Behind the Optimism: User Growth and Margin Improvement

The sustained growth in sellers, which saw an 81% year-over-year increase to over a million in the first quarter of FY27, and a 29% jump in buyers to 274 million, are central to analysts' bullish stance. This expansion is supported by a rapidly growing catalog of products and a more robust logistics network. Furthermore, UBS foresees a strengthening medium-term margin profile for Meesho, attributing this to advancements in advertising revenue generation and a more efficient logistics operation.

A key area of focus for analysts is the improvement in Meesho's contribution margin (CM). While the company faced challenges in maintaining logistics margins due to increased costs, these expenses are now reportedly declining. Management anticipates a continued decrease in logistics costs, paving the way for Meesho to rebuild its targeted logistics margin over the next few quarters. Simultaneously, advertising revenue has crossed a significant threshold, and while Meesho is carefully balancing revenue growth with user experience, this is expected to contribute positively to overall margins.

### What to Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring Meesho's upcoming quarterly results to see if these positive trends in user growth, logistics cost reduction, and advertising monetization continue to materialize. Any further improvements in contribution margins will be a key indicator of the company's ability to translate its expanding user base into sustainable profitability. The continued expansion of its seller network and buyer engagement will also be critical to watch.
