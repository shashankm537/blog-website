## HDFC Bank Gets a Boost: ICICI Securities Recommends a "Buy" with Price Target

HDFC Bank is back in the spotlight as ICICI Securities has issued a strong "Buy" recommendation, setting an ambitious target price of Rs 1,850. This comes after the bank navigated significant structural shifts following its recent merger, prompting analysts to re-evaluate its growth prospects.

### Navigating Post-Merger Adjustments

The integration of the merged entities has led to notable changes in HDFC Bank's financial structure. Key shifts include a sharp increase in the loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) and a reduced share of low-cost current account savings account (CASA) deposits, alongside a greater reliance on borrowings. While the merger significantly boosted the bank's loan market share, deposit growth has trailed slightly behind, creating a new competitive dynamic that analysts are closely watching.

Despite these adjustments, ICICI Securities projects robust deposit growth for HDFC Bank in the coming years, anticipating an 18% annual increase between FY24 and FY26. This substantial inflow is expected to enable the bank to manage its LDR more effectively and gradually substitute its borrowings. As a result, loan growth estimates have been revised slightly downwards to a 13% CAGR, with an expectation of the LDR easing to more sustainable levels.

### Outlook and Upgraded Rating

While the recent quarterly net interest margin (NIM) figures showed some positive movement, analysts attribute this partly to seasonal factors. Consequently, earnings per share (EPS) estimates for FY25 and FY26 have been adjusted downwards by 5% to reflect the revised growth projections. However, the bank is still expected to deliver healthy return on assets (RoA) of 1.7% and return on equity (RoE) of 14-15% over the same period.

Crucially, ICICI Securities has upgraded HDFC Bank's stock rating to "Buy," citing a significant correction in its share price year-to-date. While the potential for a rapid re-rating is acknowledged to be gradual, the analyst firm believes the bank is well-positioned for steady progress, with deposit accretion remaining a key driver for future performance and profitability.

### What To Watch

Investors will be keenly observing HDFC Bank's ability to sustain its deposit growth momentum and effectively manage its loan book in the evolving competitive landscape. The pace at which the bank can balance its lending activities with deposit inflows and reduce its reliance on borrowings will be critical factors influencing its stock performance in the upcoming quarters. Furthermore, the bank's net interest margin performance in the next reporting periods will be a key indicator of its profitability.
