## HDFC Bank: A Buy Signal from ICICI Securities Amidst Post-Merger Shifts

HDFC Bank is attracting significant attention from analysts, with ICICI Securities issuing a strong "Buy" recommendation and setting an ambitious target price. This comes as the banking giant navigates crucial structural changes following its recent merger, presenting both challenges and opportunities for investors.

### Navigating the Post-Merger Landscape

The integration of HDFC into HDFC Bank has brought about notable transformations in the bank's financial structure. Key among these are a considerable increase in its loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) and a reduction in its share of low-cost current account savings account (CASA) deposits, alongside a rise in overall borrowings. While the merger has boosted the bank's loan market share significantly, its deposit market share growth has been more measured. Analysts anticipate substantial annual deposit growth in the coming years, even as the bank aims for a more balanced approach to its LDR and borrowing strategies.

### Growth Projections and Investment Outlook

Despite the adjustments, ICICI Securities forecasts robust growth for HDFC Bank, projecting an 18% compound annual growth rate (CAGR) for deposits between FY24 and FY26. This growth is expected to be accompanied by a more controlled loan expansion of 13% CAGR, leading to an easing of the LDR. While the bank's earnings per share (EPS) estimates for FY25 and FY26 have been slightly revised downwards due to these growth adjustments, the outlook remains positive, with anticipated healthy returns on assets (RoA) and equity (RoE). The recent dip in the bank's stock price has prompted ICICI Securities to upgrade its rating to "Buy," believing the market may be underestimating its long-term potential.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring HDFC Bank's ability to sustain its deposit growth momentum and manage its borrowing costs effectively. The pace at which the bank can rebalance its loan portfolio and integrate its operations will be critical. Furthermore, market sentiment towards the banking sector and the broader economic environment will play a significant role in the stock's re-rating.
