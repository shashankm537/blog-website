## Indian Textile Stocks Poised for Growth as Global Sourcing Shifts

The global textile industry is undergoing a significant transformation, and Indian manufacturers are set to be major beneficiaries. Leading brokerage firm Nuvama Institutional Equities has thrown its weight behind three prominent Indian textile companies, forecasting substantial growth as international brands increasingly look beyond China for their sourcing needs. With "Buy" ratings and attractive upside potential, these stocks are capturing market attention.

### The Great Textile Realignment: India Steps Up

The global textile market, a colossal $1.6 trillion industry, is experiencing its most substantial sourcing shift in two decades. For years, China has dominated apparel exports to key markets like the United States. However, that dominance is waning, with China's share of US apparel imports nearly halving over the last ten years. This presents a golden opportunity for countries like India, Vietnam, and Bangladesh to capture a larger slice of the global pie. Nuvama's analysis suggests that India is well-positioned to capitalize on this trend, with its robust manufacturing capabilities and competitive pricing.

### Top Picks and Their Growth Drivers

Nuvama has identified KPR Mill, Indo Count Industries, and Sanathan Textiles as prime investment opportunities, each with a distinct advantage. KPR Mill, India's largest listed garment manufacturer, boasts a fully integrated operation from cotton yarn to finished garments and is consistently running at high utilization levels. Nuvama sees it as a direct play on the burgeoning global demand for garments, setting a target price of Rs 1,276, implying a 21.5% upside. Indo Count Industries, already the world's largest bed-linen manufacturer by capacity, is strategically moving towards higher-margin, value-added products and benefits from its manufacturing presence in the US. Nuvama has placed a target price of Rs 541 on Indo Count, suggesting a potential 35% increase. Lastly, Sanathan Textiles offers investors a pure-play exposure to the value-added polyester yarn segment, a crucial component in modern textile production.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring how these companies execute their expansion plans and adapt to evolving market demands. The continued shift in global sourcing away from China will be a key indicator of future performance. Furthermore, any updates on order books, capacity utilization, and new product development from KPR Mill, Indo Count, and Sanathan Textiles will be crucial for gauging their trajectory.
