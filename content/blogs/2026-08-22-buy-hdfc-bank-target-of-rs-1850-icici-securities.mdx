## HDFC Bank: A Buy Signal Emerges from ICICI Securities Amidst Post-Merger Adjustments

Following the significant merger, HDFC Bank is capturing the attention of analysts, with ICICI Securities issuing a "Buy" recommendation and setting an ambitious target price. This optimistic outlook comes as the bank navigates key balance sheet shifts and aims for substantial growth in the coming years.

### Navigating a New Financial Landscape

The recent merger has ushered in notable structural changes for HDFC Bank, including a significant increase in its loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) and a temporary dip in the share of low-cost current account savings account (CASA) deposits. Alongside these shifts, the bank has seen a considerable rise in its borrowing component. This strategic consolidation has propelled HDFC Bank's loan market share to an impressive 14.5%, though its deposit market share growth has been more measured.

ICICI Securities anticipates robust deposit growth for HDFC Bank, projecting an 18% compound annual growth rate (CAGR) between FY24 and FY26, which translates to an absolute increase of INR 4 to 5 trillion annually. Despite this strong deposit accretion, the bank is expected to prioritize a balanced LDR and a gradual substitution of borrowings. Consequently, loan growth estimates have been adjusted to a 13% CAGR for the same period, with the LDR projected to ease to 100% by FY25 and 96% by FY26, down from the current 104%.

### Unveiling the Growth Outlook and Target Price

While the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2024 saw a slightly better-than-anticipated net interest margin (NIM), analysts believe this was partly influenced by seasonal factors. Adjusting for the revised growth estimates, ICICI Securities has trimmed its earnings per share (EPS) forecast by 5% for both FY25 and FY26. Nevertheless, the bank is expected to maintain healthy profitability, with a projected return on assets (RoA) of 1.7% and a return on equity (RoE) between 14-15% for FY25 and FY26.

The research report reiterates the target price of INR 1,850 for HDFC Bank. This optimism is further bolstered by a recent 10% correction in the stock's year-to-date performance, prompting an upgrade to a "Buy" rating. The analysts suggest that a significant re-rating of the stock may occur gradually, acknowledging that while deposit growth is strong, credit growth is likely to remain somewhat slower than the overall market, and the process of borrowing substitution will be a measured one.

### What to Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring HDFC Bank's ability to effectively manage its LDR and CASA ratios in the coming quarters. The pace of credit growth relative to system growth and the success of its borrowing substitution strategy will be key indicators of its future performance and potential to achieve the ambitious target price.
