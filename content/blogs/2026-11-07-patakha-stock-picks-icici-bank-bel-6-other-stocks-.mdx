## Festive Spark: Top Stock Picks for a Booming Samvat 2083

As the festive spirit of Samvat 2083 dawns, investors are on the lookout for promising opportunities to grow their portfolios. Analysts are highlighting several key companies that could deliver significant returns, with some predictions suggesting potential gains of up to 62%. This is a season where traditional optimism meets strategic investment, and certain sectors are showing particular strength.

### Unlocking Growth in Logistics and Infrastructure

PL Capital is bullish on **Delhivery**, setting a target price that implies a healthy upside of 26%. They believe that as inflationary pressures ease and the second half of fiscal year 2027 approaches, the company's business-to-consumer (B2C) and Part Truck Load (PTL) segments are set for a seasonal boost. This, combined with expected healthy volume growth leading to an 18% sales compound annual growth rate over the next three years, points to significant operating leverage benefits.

Another industrial player showing promise is **Ingersoll Rand India**. PL Capital has assigned a target price suggesting a 28% increase from current levels. The company is strategically positioned to capitalize on robust domestic demand for compressors, leveraging its strong market position. An anticipated 50% capacity expansion at its Sanand facility is poised to be a major growth driver, translating into higher volumes and improved operational efficiency.
