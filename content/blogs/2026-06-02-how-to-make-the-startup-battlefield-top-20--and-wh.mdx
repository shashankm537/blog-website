## Your Shot at Startup Glory: How to Conquer Startup Battlefield

Dreaming of a six-minute spotlight on the Disrupt Main Stage, a chance to impress Silicon Valley's elite investors, and a shot at a $100,000 prize? This year's Startup Battlefield is your opportunity, and the application deadline has just been extended to June 8th, giving you a precious window to throw your hat in the ring. But what does it truly take to stand out from the crowd and secure your spot amongst the Top 20?

### Beyond the Hype: What Makes a Battlefield Contender?

The journey to the Startup Battlefield Top 20 begins with an application that screams innovation. We're not just looking for incremental improvements; we're seeking category-defining ideas with the potential to disrupt entire industries and leave a lasting global impact. Your application needs to clearly articulate what makes your company uniquely compelling and ready to command attention on the world stage.

### Making Your First Impression Count

Your product and founder videos are paramount in this selection process. These are your first, and arguably most crucial, introductions to the judges. Don't just talk about your vision; **show** it. Demonstrate your product in action, vividly illustrating its unique value proposition and what sets you apart from the competition. Let your passion and conviction shine through; compelling storytelling can be just as powerful as impressive metrics.

### The Perks of Participation: More Than Just the Prize

Even if you don't make it to the final five, every company that reaches the Startup Battlefield Top 20 gains invaluable experience. You'll work closely with the TechCrunch team to hone your pitch, refine your presentation, and prepare for a six-minute live demo and Q&A session. You’ll have the chance to present in front of a formidable lineup of investors, including heavy hitters like Aileen Lee of Cowboy Ventures and Navin Chaddha of Mayfield, offering unparalleled networking and feedback opportunities.

### What To Watch

With the application deadline looming, expect a flurry of submissions. We'll be closely watching to see which innovative startups seize this chance to showcase their groundbreaking ideas. The selection process will undoubtedly be competitive, but for those who make the cut, the path to potential glory at Disrupt is wide open.
