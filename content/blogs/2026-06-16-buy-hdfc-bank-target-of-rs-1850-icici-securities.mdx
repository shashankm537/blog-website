## HDFC Bank: ICICI Securities Sees a Bright Future, Upgrades to "Buy"

Following a period of significant change after its merger, HDFC Bank is catching the eye of analysts. ICICI Securities has released a new report recommending investors to "Buy" the stock, setting an optimistic target price of Rs 1,850. This upgrade comes after the bank's stock has seen a notable correction, presenting what analysts believe is a prime opportunity.

### Navigating Post-Merger Balance Sheet Adjustments

The integration of the merged entities has led to some notable shifts in HDFC Bank's financial structure. We're seeing a substantial increase in the loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) and a decrease in the proportion of low-cost current and savings accounts (CASA). Simultaneously, the bank's reliance on borrowing has grown. While the merger has boosted its loan market share significantly, deposit growth is progressing at a more measured pace.

ICICI Securities projects robust deposit growth for HDFC Bank, anticipating an annual increase of INR 4.0-5.0 trillion between FY24 and FY26. Despite this strong deposit accretion, the bank is expected to maintain a balanced approach to its LDR and explore ways to reduce its reliance on borrowings. This strategic focus has led to a revision in loan growth estimates, now projected at a 13% compound annual growth rate (CAGR) for the same period.

### A Positive Outlook Despite Growth Revisions

While the latest quarterly net interest margin (NIM) performance for Q4FY24 exceeded expectations, it appears to be partly influenced by seasonal factors. Consequently, ICICI Securities has adjusted its earnings per share (EPS) estimates downwards by 5% for both FY25 and FY26, primarily due to the recalibrated growth projections. The brokerage firm, however, still anticipates HDFC Bank to deliver a healthy return on assets (RoA) of 1.7% for FY25/26 and a return on equity (RoE) of 14-15%. Crucially, their target price of INR 1,850 remains unchanged, reinforcing their bullish stance. The upgrade to "Buy" is driven by the recent dip in the stock price, offering an attractive entry point for investors.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring HDFC Bank's ability to manage its loan-to-deposit ratio effectively while continuing to grow its deposit base. The pace at which the bank substitutes its borrowings and how its credit growth compares to the broader market will also be key indicators. The gradual re-rating of the stock is expected as these factors play out.
