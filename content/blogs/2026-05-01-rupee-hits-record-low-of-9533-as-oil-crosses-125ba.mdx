## Rupee Sinks to Record Low as Oil Prices Surge

The Indian rupee has hit a new historic low against the US dollar, closing Thursday at 94.90/$ after an intraday nosedive to 95.33/$. This dramatic depreciation comes amid a backdrop of soaring global crude oil prices and ongoing foreign investment outflows, painting a challenging picture for India's economy.

### Intervention and Volatility Mark Trading Session

Thursday's trading session for the rupee was anything but ordinary. The currency experienced significant volatility, swinging nearly 50 paise between 95.35/$ and 94.86/$. This is a stark contrast to the more typical daily fluctuations of 20-25 paise, indicating intense pressure. Traders believe the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) stepped in during the latter half of the trading day, selling dollars to pare some of the rupee's losses and prevent it from closing above the crucial 95/$ psychological barrier.

### Global Headwinds Weigh Heavily on the Rupee

The primary driver behind the rupee's slide appears to be the relentless climb in crude oil prices, which have now crossed the $125 per barrel mark. As India is a major importer of oil, this surge directly translates into higher demand for dollars to pay for these imports, weakening the rupee. Compounding this issue are sustained outflows of foreign portfolio investment, as global investors shift their capital in search of safer havens or higher returns elsewhere. Further adding to dollar demand, there are indications that state-run oil companies may not be fully utilizing a special line of credit provided by the RBI, leading them to continue seeking dollars in the spot market for their purchases.

### What To Watch

All eyes will remain on the price of crude oil and the RBI's continued intervention efforts in the currency markets. Investors will also be closely monitoring foreign fund flows, as any significant reversal could provide further support or pressure on the rupee. The effectiveness of the RBI's measures to manage currency volatility will be a key focus in the coming days.
