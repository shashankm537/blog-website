## Benchmark Bets on Patience in a Hectic Tech Landscape

In a venture capital world often characterized by breakneck speed and aggressive deal-making, the renowned firm Benchmark is charting a decidedly different course. Despite shifts in its partnership and a recent increase in fund size, the firm’s leaders made it clear at TechCrunch Disrupt that their core philosophy of patient, deliberate investment remains unwavering, even as others race to deploy capital.

### The Benchmark Blueprint: Consistency in Change

While Benchmark has seen some personnel and fund size adjustments, notably raising $2 billion across two funds this summer, a significant departure from their historical practice, the fundamental approach to investing remains intact. The firm's five general partners presented a united front, emphasizing a commitment to their established pace of making only two to three new investments per partner annually. This deliberate strategy contrasts sharply with the frenetic activity seen elsewhere in the market, suggesting a deep-seated belief in the power of focused, long-term partnerships.

### Navigating the Noise: An Unhurried Approach

The partners articulated a unique perspective on the current economic climate, dismissing many widely reported warning signs as mere "X-fueled hysteria." Their Monday partner meetings, notably, continue to operate without a formal agenda, a testament to their trust in organic discussion and deep dives into potential investments. This lack of a rigid structure, coupled with their commitment to deeply understanding founders over a decade or more, highlights a culture that prioritizes substance over speed, even when faced with the allure of quicker returns.

### Evolving Without Compromise

Benchmark’s Peter Fenton acknowledged the firm's evolution, including the expansion to a larger core fund and the introduction of a new growth fund. He stressed that the core principle of working closely with founders from the earliest stages remains unchanged, a commitment that inherently doesn't scale with rapid growth. The decision to increase the core fund size from $500 million, a figure held for two decades, was framed as a necessary adjustment to align with the current economic reality, a move Fenton admitted might have been a tactical oversight in the past. The new growth fund, he added, represents an expansion of their mandate, allowing them to participate more broadly within the ecosystem without deviating from their foundational values.

### What to Watch

Expect Benchmark to continue its deliberate investment strategy, focusing on a select few high-conviction opportunities. Their ability to adapt their fund structure while maintaining their core philosophy will be closely observed as a potential counterpoint to the prevailing trends in venture capital. The firm's long-term outlook suggests a resilience that may prove valuable in an unpredictable market.
