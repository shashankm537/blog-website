## Tejas Networks Soars on Strong Performance: Emkay Global Financial Initiates Buy Call with Ambitious Target

Tejas Networks is capturing the attention of market watchers, with a prominent financial institution forecasting significant growth for the telecom equipment maker. Following a robust performance driven by key government projects, Emkay Global Financial has issued a strong "Buy" recommendation, setting an ambitious price target that suggests substantial upside potential for investors.

### A Surge Fueled by Government Initiatives and Project Execution

Emkay Global Financial's latest research report highlights Tejas Networks' impressive recent results, which exceeded revenue and margin expectations. A primary driver behind this positive performance is the successful execution of the BSNL 4G project. This success is underpinned by several critical factors, including the Indian government's strong push for domestic manufacturing, amplified by the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme. Furthermore, substantial investments in critical infrastructure projects like BSNL, BharatNet, and the Railways are creating a fertile ground for Tejas Networks' expansion. The company also benefits from strong endorsements and references from industry giants like TCom and TCS, which are opening doors to new clients and markets.

### Strategic Advantages Position Tejas for Future Gains

Beyond current project wins, Tejas Networks is strategically positioned to capitalize on broader market trends. The global shift away from Chinese telecom equipment presents a significant opportunity for homegrown players. Analysts at Emkay foresee Tejas Networks' revenue potentially quadrupling in fiscal year 2025 compared to fiscal year 2024, largely due to continued progress on the BSNL and BharatNet initiatives. The firm has also revised its revenue forecasts upwards for both FY25 and FY26, reflecting the strong momentum. Moreover, improvements in the company's EBITDA margins are also noted, suggesting enhanced profitability as operations scale.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring the pace of execution on ongoing BSNL and BharatNet projects. The company's ability to secure new orders and expand its client base, particularly through its partnerships, will be crucial indicators. Furthermore, the ongoing global re-evaluation of telecom supply chains could present further tailwinds for Tejas Networks. Emkay Global Financial's revised target price of Rs 1,100 per share signals strong confidence in the company's growth trajectory.
