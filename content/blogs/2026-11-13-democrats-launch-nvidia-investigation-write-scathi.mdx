## Nvidia Under Fire: Democrats Launch Probe into Trump-Era Chip Deals

A whirlwind of controversy is swirling around tech giant Nvidia, as Democratic lawmakers have launched a formal investigation into the Trump administration's handling of the company's chip sales to China. This move comes amidst seismic shifts within Nvidia itself, including the ousting of its long-time CEO, Jensen Huang, who is now facing multiple accusations and agreeing to testify before Congress.

### Questionable Dealings and Suspicious Connections

At the heart of the Democratic inquiry lies a deeply concerning alleged arrangement between the Trump administration and Nvidia. Top Democrats on the House Ways and Means and Senate Finance Committees have penned a strong letter to Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick, demanding the preservation of records related to a deal they deem questionable, and potentially illegal. The letter outlines a "scheme" where Nvidia's advanced H200 chips, manufactured in Taiwan, were reportedly routed through the United States before being shipped to China. The Trump administration then allegedly imposed a tariff on these sales, effectively taking a percentage of Nvidia's revenue, a move Democrats argue is an unconstitutional export tax. This transaction seemingly paved the way for the Commerce Department to issue export licenses.

### CEO's Troubles Compound Company's Woes

Adding fuel to the fire, Nvidia's CEO, Jensen Huang, has been removed from his position following admissions of an extramarital affair with a woman identified as Alice Huang, who is under suspicion by U.S. officials for allegedly aiding in the transshipment of advanced Nvidia chips to China via her employer, Megaspeed. Further allegations have surfaced accusing Huang of additional affairs with Nvidia employees. While the specifics of Huang's departure are complex, it undeniably casts a long shadow over the company's recent dealings and its leadership. The public agreement for Nvidia and AMD to share 15% of their China chip sales revenue with the U.S. government in August 2025, as previously reported, now faces intensified scrutiny within this broader context.

### What To Watch

With the Democratic investigation officially underway and Nvidia grappling with internal upheaval and serious allegations, the coming weeks promise significant developments. Congressional committees are likely to demand further explanations regarding the specifics of the alleged "scheme" and the Commerce Department's role. Investors and industry observers will be closely monitoring Nvidia's public statements and any further revelations that emerge from the ongoing probes. The integrity of export control processes and the potential for political influence in major technology deals are now firmly in the spotlight.
