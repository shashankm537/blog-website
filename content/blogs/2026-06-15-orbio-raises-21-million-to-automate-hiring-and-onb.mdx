## AI Steps In: Orbio Secures $21 Million to Revolutionize Frontline Worker Management

A decade of experience navigating the complexities of managing large workforces, first at e-commerce giant Amazon and later at a thriving floral startup, revealed a persistent gap for Sergi Bastardas: the lack of efficient systems to support the very people keeping businesses running. This insight fueled the creation of Orbio, a tech startup poised to transform how companies hire and manage their essential frontline employees, as evidenced by their recent substantial funding boost.

### Addressing the "Human Infrastructure" Gap with AI

Orbio, co-founded by Bastardas, Nacho Travesí, and Antonio Melé in 2025, tackles a critical challenge many businesses face – effectively managing their dispersed and often high-turnover frontline workforce. The company's innovative approach leverages AI agents, designed to streamline the entire employee lifecycle, from initial recruitment to ongoing engagement. This isn't just about basic automation; Orbio's AI is designed to handle sophisticated tasks like conducting initial interviews, assessing candidate suitability, monitoring daily performance, and even facilitating regular employee check-ins.

The company recently announced a significant milestone, securing $21 million in Series A funding led by Dawn Capital. This investment underscores the growing recognition of Orbio's potential to solve real-world business problems. Early adopters, including major players like Poke and YUM! Brands – the conglomerate behind familiar names like Pizza Hut, Taco Bell, and KFC – are already seeing tangible benefits, moving from pilot programs to full-scale deployment of Orbio's software to manage their vast frontline teams.

### Empowering Businesses and Employees Through Intelligent Automation

Orbio's suite of AI agents, including names like Maria, Daniel, and Claire, are engineered to work collaboratively, creating a dynamic and responsive workforce management system. Bastardas emphasizes the goal is to empower businesses to run their operations more autonomously while simultaneously enhancing the employee experience. By delegating certain operational tasks to AI, managers can free up their time to focus on more strategic initiatives and providing crucial human support where it's most needed.

The intelligence of Orbio's system lies in its ability to create a feedback loop between its AI agents. Data gathered from onboarding processes informs the quality of recruitment efforts, while insights from exit interviews can be used to refine hiring criteria and reduce employee turnover. This interconnected approach allows businesses to proactively identify potential retention risks and make data-driven decisions to foster a more engaged and stable workforce. This integrated data approach promises a more strategic and less reactive way of managing the essential workers who drive business success.

### What To Watch

With this substantial funding injection, Orbio is expected to accelerate its product development and expand its reach into new markets. Investors and industry observers will be keen to see how quickly other large enterprises adopt Orbio's AI-powered solutions, particularly in sectors traditionally reliant on manual HR processes. The company's ability to demonstrate measurable improvements in hiring efficiency and employee retention will be key indicators of its future success in reshaping frontline workforce management.
