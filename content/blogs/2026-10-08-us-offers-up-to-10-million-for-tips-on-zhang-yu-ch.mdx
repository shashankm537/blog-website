### Nation Offers Millions for Information on Suspected Cyber Attacker

The United States is escalating its pursuit of justice for a series of sophisticated cyberattacks, announcing a significant reward for any information leading to the capture of a Chinese national accused in the 2021 HAFNIUM breaches. This move underscores the seriousness with which Washington views threats to its critical infrastructure and its commitment to holding those responsible accountable.

### The Hunt for Zhang Yu and the HAFNIUM Breach

The U.S. State Department's "Rewards for Justice" program has posted a bounty of up to $10 million for credible tips that identify or locate Zhang Yu. He is a Chinese citizen facing charges in the United States for his alleged involvement in the HAFNIUM attacks, a widespread campaign that exploited vulnerabilities in Microsoft Exchange Server software. These attacks, which occurred primarily between February 2020 and June 2021, compromised numerous organizations and government agencies, raising significant national security concerns. Zhang remains at large, with U.S. authorities actively seeking his whereabouts.

### A Pattern of State-Sponsored Cyber Activity

Authorities allege that Zhang was a director at Shanghai Firetech Information Science and Technology, a company believed to be part of a broader network used by the Chinese government for cyber operations. This case is not isolated; Zhang is charged alongside another individual, Xu Zewei, who was arrested in Italy and subsequently extradited to the United States. The charges against them, unsealed in July 2025, detail accusations of malicious cyber activities targeting U.S. critical infrastructure. The FBI has previously stated that the Chinese government often employs contractors to mask its involvement in such operations, suggesting a pattern of state-sponsored cyber intrusions.

### What To Watch

Expect continued efforts by U.S. law enforcement and intelligence agencies to gather intelligence on Zhang's location and his alleged collaborators. The substantial reward offered signals a high priority placed on resolving the HAFNIUM case and potentially deterring future state-backed cyber threats. International cooperation will likely remain crucial in tracking down individuals accused of such sophisticated cybercrimes.
