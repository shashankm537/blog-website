## Figma Faces Lawsuit Over Alleged Harassment and Forced Confrontation

A former Figma engineer has filed a lawsuit against the popular design software company, alleging a disturbing pattern of sexual harassment and subsequent retaliation. The suit claims that instead of investigating her complaints, management and HR repeatedly instructed her to confront the individuals she accused of misconduct, a directive that allegedly damaged her career and well-being.

### A Culture of "Direct Feedback" Under Scrutiny

The core of the lawsuit, filed in U.S. District Court for the Southern District of New York, centers on Figma's purported "direct feedback" culture. According to the complaint, this philosophy was twisted into a mechanism where employees reporting harassment were instead pushed to handle the situation themselves. Serry Park, who joined Figma in 2022, asserts that this approach by managers and HR undermined her efforts to address the alleged harassment, leading to significant negative impacts on her professional advancement, financial compensation, and mental health. Park, who is of Asian descent, also claims to have experienced racial and gender discrimination, including performance critiques rooted in harmful stereotypes about Asian women.

### Allegations of Discrimination and Lack of Action

Park expressed her initial hope that Figma would be a place where she could grow her career, describing it as a "dream company." However, her experience reportedly took a sharp turn as she navigated what she believed to be a formal complaint process. She states that despite meticulously documenting the alleged incidents, she felt her concerns were not taken seriously by the company, particularly as an engineer in a male-dominated tech environment. This alleged lack of decisive action and the requirement to confront her alleged harassers has led to the current legal action, seeking damages and a court order to change Figma's internal policies regarding harassment investigations.

Figma, in response to the lawsuit, has vehemently denied the allegations. A company spokesperson stated, "Figma does not tolerate discrimination or harassment. Figma also does not tolerate extortion. These claims have no merit, and we will present the facts in court."

### What To Watch

The legal proceedings are likely to delve deeply into Figma's internal HR practices and the company's interpretation and application of its "direct feedback" policy. The outcome could have significant implications for how tech companies handle harassment complaints and the responsibilities they have in protecting their employees from hostile work environments. Investors and industry observers will be closely watching how this case unfolds and what precedent it might set for workplace harassment litigation in the tech sector.
