## HDFC Bank: ICICI Securities Sees a Buy Opportunity Amidst Post-Merger Shifts

Financial markets are buzzing as ICICI Securities has issued a "Buy" recommendation for HDFC Bank, setting an ambitious target price of ₹1,850. This assessment comes following significant structural changes within the bank after its recent merger, prompting a closer look at its financial trajectory.

### Navigating the New Landscape Post-Merger

The merger has undeniably reshaped HDFC Bank's balance sheet, leading to a notable increase in its loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) and a decrease in the share of low-cost CASA deposits, while borrowings have risen significantly. While the bank's loan market share has jumped by a substantial 330 basis points to 14.5%, its deposit market share has seen a more modest increase of 110 basis points year-on-year to 11.0%. ICICI Securities projects robust deposit growth for the bank, estimating an 18% compound annual growth rate (CAGR) between FY24 and FY26, which translates to an absolute rise of ₹4-5 trillion annually. This growth is expected to help the bank manage its LDR and borrowing mix, with a projected easing of LDR to 100% in FY25 and 96% in FY26, down from its current 104%.

### A Positive Outlook Despite Growth Adjustments

Despite the positive outlook on deposit accretion, ICICI Securities has slightly tempered its loan growth estimates to a 13% CAGR for FY24-26, factoring in the bank's strategic focus on a balanced LDR and borrowing strategy. While the Net Interest Margin (NIM) performance in the fourth quarter of FY24 was slightly better than anticipated, it's believed to have been influenced by seasonal factors. Consequently, earnings per share (EPS) estimates for FY25 and FY26 have been revised downwards by 5%. Nevertheless, the bank is still expected to achieve healthy return on assets (RoA) of 1.7% and a return on equity (RoE) of 14-15% for the same period. The "Buy" upgrade to the stock comes in light of its recent 10% year-to-date correction, presenting what analysts deem an attractive entry point.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring HDFC Bank's deposit growth trajectory and its effectiveness in managing its loan-to-deposit ratio. The pace at which the bank can substitute its borrowings and the system's overall credit growth will be key factors influencing its re-rating. The market will also be watching for how the bank's NIMs perform in the coming quarters, especially as seasonal effects subside.
