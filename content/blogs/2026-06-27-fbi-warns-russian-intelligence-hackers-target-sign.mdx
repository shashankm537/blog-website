## Russian Hackers' New Signal Scam: Your Backups Are Not Safe

Cybersecurity alerts are flashing again as Russian intelligence operatives have escalated their phishing attacks targeting Signal users. What began as a campaign to steal account access has evolved into a sophisticated plot to compromise your encrypted message history, using your own backup recovery key against you. This alarming development means even seemingly secure conversations could be laid bare.

### The Evolution of the Signal Phishing Scheme

Initial warnings from the FBI and CISA highlighted how hackers were tricking Signal users into revealing SMS verification codes or account PINs. They also employed deceptive links disguised as group invitations, which secretly linked an attacker's device to the target's account. The latest evolution, however, is far more insidious. Now, attackers are expertly guiding victims through the process of enabling Signal backups, then subtly persuading them to share their crucial backup recovery key.

This recovery key is the ultimate backdoor. Once compromised, it allows hackers to not only restore your past conversations but also to access ongoing private and group messages, effectively hijacking your account. Disturbingly, this key remains valid even if you create a new account using the same phone number. The FBI and CISA are clear: this tactic specifically targets Signal's backup functionality, leaving users vulnerable to widespread data breaches.

### Who's Being Targeted and How

The individuals caught in the crosshairs of this persistent campaign are those deemed of high intelligence value. This includes current and former government officials from the U.S. and internationally, military personnel, political figures, journalists, and individuals associated with Ukraine. These sophisticated phishing messages are carefully crafted to appear legitimate, often masquerading as official communications from Signal support. They might claim a mandatory two-factor authentication rollout or an urgent need for data recovery to prevent message loss. The attackers are meticulously guiding victims step-by-step, making the request for the recovery key seem like a standard, albeit urgent, procedure.

### What To Watch

The immediate course of action for all Signal users is to proactively generate a new backup recovery key within the app's settings. This action will invalidate any previously compromised keys, preventing future unauthorized access to your backups. While this action secures future data, it's important to understand that any information already exfiltrated by attackers will be lost. Authorities are working to identify and track the specific Russian intelligence service groups, including those linked to the FSB and Russian military services, responsible for these attacks. Expect continued advisories and an increased focus on educating users about these evolving threats.
