## India's Housing Market Tilts Upwards: Premium Homes Steal the Spotlight

India's property landscape is experiencing a significant transformation, with developers shifting their focus away from affordable housing and towards the burgeoning premium and luxury segments. This strategic pivot is occurring even as overall sales show a mixed picture across major cities, grappling with affordability challenges and cautious buyer sentiment.

### Developers Embrace the High-End Market

Recent analyses reveal a clear trend: affordable housing is no longer the primary focus for new property launches. Instead, developers are increasingly investing in mid-premium and luxury homes, catering to a growing demand from affluent buyers. This move is partly driven by rising costs associated with land acquisition, construction, and regulatory compliance, which make affordable projects less financially attractive. The desire for larger living spaces and enhanced amenities by both domestic high-net-worth individuals and Non-Resident Indians is further fueling this shift.

### A Tale of Two Markets: Sales Diverge

While the overall narrative points to a rise in premium offerings, the actual sales performance across India's top cities presents a more nuanced picture. Markets in the western and northern regions are showing signs of moderation, possibly due to a combination of economic factors and the impact of rising property prices on a broader buyer base. Conversely, cities in the southern part of the country are demonstrating remarkable resilience, indicating sustained demand and a potentially stronger economic footing in those areas. This divergence highlights that a one-size-fits-all approach to the Indian real estate market is no longer applicable.

### Shifting Shares: The Numbers Tell the Story

The data from CareEdge Ratings paints a compelling picture of this structural change. Homes priced below ₹1.5 crore, once the dominant force, have seen their share in new launches shrink dramatically from 85% in early 2022 to just 47% by the first quarter of 2026. Meanwhile, the ₹1.5 crore to ₹4 crore bracket has surged, capturing 44% of new launches in early 2026, a significant leap from 14% in 2022. Even the ultra-luxury segment, with properties exceeding ₹4 crore, has expanded its presence, now making up 9% of new developments compared to a mere 1% just four years prior.

#### What To Watch

Expect developers to continue prioritizing higher-margin luxury and premium projects. Buyers looking for more affordable options might face fewer new launches, potentially impacting their choices. The economic health of different regions will likely continue to dictate the strength of their respective housing markets.
