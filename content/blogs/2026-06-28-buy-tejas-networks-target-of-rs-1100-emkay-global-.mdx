## Tejas Networks Ignites Investor Interest as Emkay Global Boosts Price Target

Telecom infrastructure player Tejas Networks is making headlines as a prominent financial firm, Emkay Global Financial, issues a strong "Buy" recommendation. With a significantly revised price target, analysts believe the company is well-positioned for substantial growth, driven by key government initiatives and expanding market opportunities.

### Strong Execution Fuels Upward Momentum

Emkay Global's latest research report, dated April 23, 2024, highlights Tejas Networks' impressive performance, particularly its revenue and margin figures which exceeded expectations. This positive beat is largely attributed to the company's effective execution of the BSNL 4G project. Analysts are pointing to several factors underpinning this optimism, including the Indian government's strong push for domestic manufacturing through schemes like Production Linked Incentive (PLI). Furthermore, significant investments in crucial projects like BSNL, BharatNet, and railway communications are creating a fertile ground for Tejas Networks' offerings. The company is also benefiting from its growing list of clients, including major players like TCom and TCS, and a global trend to diversify away from Chinese telecom equipment suppliers.

### Projected Growth and Revised Valuation

Looking ahead, Emkay Global forecasts a remarkable revenue surge for Tejas Networks in fiscal year 2025, potentially quadrupling its fiscal year 2024 revenue, primarily due to the ongoing BSNL and BharatNet projects. This robust outlook has led to upward revisions in revenue estimates for both FY25 and FY26, with increases of 6% and 7% respectively. The firm has also adjusted its Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization (EBITDA) margin expectations upwards by 10 to 150 basis points, reflecting the strong margin performance observed. Based on their Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) methodology, Emkay Global has revised its target price for Tejas Networks shares to Rs 1,100, representing a significant 21% potential upside from its previous Rs 975 target.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring Tejas Networks' continued execution on its large government-backed projects, particularly the progress of the BSNL 4G rollout and the BharatNet initiative. The company's ability to secure new clients and expand its international presence, especially in markets looking to replace existing Chinese equipment, will also be critical. Furthermore, any updates on the government's telecom policy landscape and its impact on domestic manufacturers will be keenly observed.
