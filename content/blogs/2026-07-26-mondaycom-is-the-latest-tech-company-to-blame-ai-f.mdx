## Monday.com Joins the Ranks of Tech Giants Pointing to AI in Layoffs

The tech industry is once again grappling with significant job cuts, and this time, artificial intelligence is a prominent factor. Monday.com, a prominent work management software company, has announced substantial layoffs, citing its strategic pivot towards an AI-first future. This move by Monday.com adds it to a growing list of tech firms navigating the complex landscape of AI integration and workforce adjustments.

### A "Leaner, More Focused" Future

Monday.com, headquartered in Tel Aviv, revealed plans to eliminate approximately 20% of its workforce, impacting over 600 employees. In a filing with the Securities and Exchange Commission, the company described the move as a "restructuring plan" aimed at adapting its product, marketing, and sales strategies for a "leaner, more focused operating model." This restructuring is directly linked to the company's significant investments in its "AI-driven growth strategy."

Co-founder Eran Zinman communicated to employees that these layoffs are not about immediate cost reduction or direct replacement by AI. Instead, he framed the decision as a necessary step to align the organization with an AI-centric vision that was introduced about a year ago. This vision prompted a company-wide rebranding to emphasize its platform's advanced AI capabilities. Despite these changes, Monday.com anticipates robust revenue growth in the coming year, projecting a 20% increase.

### The Broader AI Layoff Trend

Monday.com's announcement is part of a larger trend in the tech sector. Analysis from the Financial Times reveals that nearly 140,000 jobs have been cut by U.S. tech companies since the beginning of 2024. Giants like Amazon, Oracle, Meta, and Microsoft alone have shed close to 50,000 roles, partly to fund massive investments in AI data centers. Interestingly, the FT's research also suggests that companies explicitly citing AI in their layoff announcements have seen their stock performance lag behind the Nasdaq index in the month following such news. This indicates that the market may not always be convinced by the AI rationale for workforce reductions.

However, the narrative isn't entirely negative. The AI revolution is also a powerful engine for job creation in specialized areas. Companies like Anthropic and OpenAI are actively expanding their teams, absorbing talent that has become available due to these broader industry shifts. Furthermore, within some of the companies implementing cuts, there are instances where roles are being reallocated rather than eliminated entirely, with employees transitioning to AI-focused initiatives.

### What To Watch

Investors and industry observers will be closely monitoring how Monday.com's restructuring impacts its operational efficiency and market competitiveness. The company's ability to translate its AI investments into tangible product improvements and sustained revenue growth will be key. Additionally, the broader tech landscape will continue to watch for further shifts as companies balance AI adoption with their workforce strategies, and whether the market's skepticism towards AI-driven layoffs persists.
