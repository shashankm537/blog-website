## XDOF Poised for Massive Valuation Jump Just Months After Launch

In a whirlwind of rapid growth, emerging robotics startup XDOF is reportedly in advanced negotiations for a Series B funding round that could catapult its valuation to an impressive $1.2 billion. This comes as a stunning development, considering the company only emerged from stealth mode a mere three months ago.

### The Data Gold Rush for General-Purpose Robots

XDOF is carving out a critical niche in the burgeoning field of robotics by focusing on the collection of real-world teleoperation data. This data is essential for training the next generation of versatile, general-purpose robots that can perform a wide array of tasks. The company aims to provide the vital infrastructure – the data pipelines, collection tools, and annotation systems – that many advanced AI and robotics firms struggle to develop internally. Essentially, XDOF is positioning itself as the indispensable outsourced data supplier for the physical robotics industry, a crucial element often overlooked in the pursuit of advanced AI.

### From Academic Research to Billion-Dollar Potential

The genesis of XDOF lies in the academic pursuits of its co-founders, UC Berkeley researchers Philipp Wu and Fred Shentu. Recognizing a significant bottleneck in robotics research – the scarcity of large-scale datasets – Wu, as a PhD student, collaborated with Shentu on a project called GELLO. This innovative, low-cost teleoperation system allowed human operators to remotely control robotic arms, thereby generating valuable training data. Their foundational research, which culminated in an influential paper, directly paved the way for XDOF. Investors are now drawing parallels to data-labeling giants like Scale AI and Mercor, highlighting XDOF's potential to be the enabler of a similar boom in physical robotics.

### What To Watch

All eyes will be on the finalization of this Series B round and the official confirmation of the $1.2 billion valuation. Investors will be keen to see how XDOF plans to leverage this significant capital infusion to further scale its operations and solidify its position as a leader in robotics data infrastructure. The terms of the deal, while reportedly in late stages, could still evolve, making any official announcement a key development to monitor.
