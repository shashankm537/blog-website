## Greek PM Admits AI's "Uncharted Territory" During San Francisco Visit

Greek Prime Minister Kyriakos Mitsotakis made waves in San Francisco this week, not just by pitching his nation as a prime investment destination, but by candidly admitting that even global leaders are grappling with the rapid rise of artificial intelligence. Speaking to a prominent group of tech leaders and investors, Mitsotakis revealed that he, like many, is seeking answers to the complex questions AI presents.

### A "Homecoming" with a Tech Focus

Mitsotakis's visit to the Bay Area was billed as a chance to promote Greece, a nation poised to regain its developed market status next year. However, the trip also served as a crucial fact-finding mission. After touring tech giants like Tesla and visiting venture capital firm Sequoia Capital, the Prime Minister expressed his desire to build bridges with the tech world, hoping to attract new businesses to Greece. He described his return to the Bay Area, where he previously studied at Stanford, as a "homecoming."

### Greece's Economic Comeback on Display

Beyond his reflections on AI, Mitsotakis was eager to highlight Greece's remarkable economic recovery. He proudly pointed out that the country is aggressively paying down its debt and is now borrowing at rates lower than the United States. This significant turnaround, he suggested with a smile, is a testament to Greece no longer being viewed as a "special case." While acknowledging that eurozone interest rates play a role, the comparison of Greece's 10-year bond yield to that of U.S. Treasuries offers a powerful illustration of the nation's improved financial standing, a stark contrast to the soaring yields during the debt crisis of 2012.

### Facing the AI Challenge Head-On

Despite the economic successes, Mitsotakis didn't shy away from the challenges posed by artificial intelligence. He acknowledged that discussions around AI regulation and its societal impact are ongoing and complex, even at the highest levels of government. The Prime Minister's openness signals a recognition that proactively addressing the implications of AI, rather than reacting to past issues, is the critical path forward for nations worldwide.

### What to Watch

As Prime Minister Mitsotakis heads to the UN General Assembly, expect him to continue advocating for Greece as a stable and growing economic partner. His candid remarks on AI also suggest a potential shift towards more proactive discussions on global technology governance. Keep an eye on how Greece leverages its economic resurgence to attract forward-thinking tech investment.
