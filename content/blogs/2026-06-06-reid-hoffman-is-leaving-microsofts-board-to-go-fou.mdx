## Tech Titan Reid Hoffman Trades Boardroom for Startup Lab

Reid Hoffman, a prominent figure in the tech world, is stepping down from Microsoft’s board after a decade-long tenure. This significant move signals his deep dive back into the entrepreneurial spirit, as he aims to dedicate his focus to his new AI startup, Manus.

### A Decade at Microsoft's Helm

Hoffman’s journey with Microsoft began in 2016 following the tech giant's landmark acquisition of his professional networking platform, LinkedIn, for a staggering $26.2 billion. During his time on the board, he witnessed and participated in some of Microsoft’s most impactful strategic decisions. This included the company’s initial $1 billion investment in OpenAI in 2019, a move that has since reshaped the artificial intelligence landscape. He also played a role during Microsoft's strategic acquisition of his AI startup, Inflection AI, which brought Inflection co-founder Mustafa Suleyman into the Microsoft fold.

### "Founder Mode" Activated for AI Drug Discovery

The driving force behind Hoffman’s departure is his commitment to Manus, a promising AI startup focused on drug discovery. He expressed his desire to re-enter "founder mode," emphasizing the rapid progress being made at Manus. The company, which secured over $50 million in seed funding last year, is pioneering what Hoffman refers to as "Move 37" AI. This innovative approach aims to leverage artificial intelligence to surpass human creativity in the field of chemistry, with a particular focus on developing new treatments for various cancers. While not the CEO, Hoffman co-founded Manus and serves as its chairman, working alongside Dr. Siddhartha Mukherjee, a renowned physician, biologist, and Pulitzer Prize-winning author.

### What To Watch

With Hoffman now fully immersed in the world of AI-driven drug discovery, all eyes will be on Manus to see how its "Move 37" AI technology progresses. The success of this venture could have profound implications for the future of pharmaceutical research and the fight against cancer. Furthermore, his departure from Microsoft's board may signal future strategic shifts for both entities, as Hoffman, a seasoned innovator, channels his energy into this cutting-edge startup.
