## New Banking Trojan "TCLBANKER" Spreading Through WhatsApp and Outlook

Cybersecurity experts are sounding the alarm about a sophisticated new banking trojan, dubbed TCLBANKER, that's making its rounds with a cunning dual-pronged attack. This potent malware, previously unknown to researchers, has been observed targeting a wide array of financial platforms, including traditional banks, fintech companies, and cryptocurrency exchanges, demonstrating a significant threat to users' sensitive data.

### A Familiar Foe, Reimagined

Security researchers at Elastic Security Labs have identified TCLBANKER as a substantial evolution of the Maverick malware family, also linked to the notorious "Water Saci" threat group. The key innovation lies in its propagation methods. TCLBANKER leverages a worm component, previously seen with the SORVEPOTEL worm, which exploits WhatsApp Web to spread rapidly to a victim's contacts, essentially turning their own address book into a vector for further infections. This makes it incredibly difficult for individuals to distinguish between legitimate messages and malicious ones.

### Stealthy Infiltration and Robust Defenses

The initial entry point for TCLBANKER is a deceptive package: a ZIP file containing a malicious MSI installer that masquerades as a legitimate Logitech program called "Logi AI Prompt Builder." Once executed, the malware employs a robust loader with advanced anti-analysis capabilities, designed to evade detection by security software. This loader is programmed to meticulously monitor for and disable any tools used by security professionals to investigate malware, including sandboxes, debuggers, and antivirus programs. The malicious DLL is only activated under specific conditions, further complicating analysis and detection efforts.

### Expanding its Reach Through Trusted Channels

Beyond its WhatsApp capabilities, TCLBANKER also utilizes Microsoft Outlook to broaden its attack surface. This dual-pronged approach ensures that the malware can infect users through multiple common communication channels, increasing its potential reach and impact. By embedding itself within familiar applications and communication methods, TCLBANKER aims to exploit user trust and achieve widespread compromise of financial accounts.

### What To Watch

Expect to see continued efforts from cybersecurity firms to analyze TCLBANKER's full capabilities and develop more effective countermeasures. Financial institutions and users alike should remain vigilant, practicing cautious online behavior and ensuring their security software is up-to-date to defend against this evolving threat.
