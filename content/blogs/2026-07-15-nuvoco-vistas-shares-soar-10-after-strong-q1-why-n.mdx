# Nuvoco Vistas Shares Skyrocket on Stellar Q1 Performance; Analysts Eye Significant Upside

Nuvoco Vistas witnessed a remarkable surge in its share price, jumping 10% to Rs 375 on Wednesday following an impressive first-quarter earnings report. This strong financial showing has prompted several prominent brokerage firms to issue optimistic outlooks, with some predicting as much as a 47% increase in the stock's value.

## Robust Financials Fuel Market Confidence

The building materials giant announced a significant 20% rise in net profit, reaching Rs 160 crore, alongside a healthy 9% growth in revenue, which climbed to Rs 3,129 crore for the first quarter of FY27. This impressive performance was attributed to a resilient execution despite a challenging economic landscape, with the company reporting a 5% year-on-year volume growth. Furthermore, Nuvoco Vistas achieved its strongest-ever first-quarter EBITDA, growing 7% year-on-year, a testament to their sustained cost discipline and operational efficiencies.

The company highlighted that while cement demand saw an improvement during the June quarter, some regions experienced temporary dips due to election-related activities. However, strong governmental investment in capital expenditure, which saw a 13% year-on-year increase, along with continued progress in infrastructure and housing projects, provided a solid foundation for sustained demand.

## Brokerages Bullish on Nuvoco's Growth Trajectory

Following the positive earnings, analysts at Choice Institutional Equities have reiterated their 'Buy' rating for Nuvoco Vistas, raising their target price to Rs 500, signaling a potential 47% upside. Their optimism is rooted in the company's consistent pricing power, stringent cost management, and a robust pipeline for capacity-driven growth. While they anticipate a slight cost inflation of approximately Rs 100 per tonne in the second quarter, they believe that fuel optimization efforts, increased use of alternative fuels, and stable cement prices will effectively mitigate any margin pressures.

Moreover, the anticipated early commissioning of Nuvoco's 2 million tonnes per annum grinding unit in Surat is expected to bolster their presence in western India and contribute significantly to long-term growth.

### What To Watch

Investors will be keen to observe how Nuvoco Vistas navigates the upcoming quarters, particularly in light of evolving cost dynamics and the full impact of its new Surat facility. The company's ability to maintain its pricing power and operational efficiencies amidst potential market fluctuations will be crucial for sustaining this upward momentum.
