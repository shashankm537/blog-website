## Tejas Networks Soars on Strong Performance; Emkay Global Financial Sets Ambitious Target

Tejas Networks is capturing the attention of investors following a robust performance that has prompted Emkay Global Financial to issue a strong "Buy" recommendation. The research firm sees significant upside potential for the telecom equipment manufacturer, backing its optimism with a new price target of Rs 1100.

### BSNL's 4G Project Fuels Revenue Surge

A primary driver behind Emkay Global's bullish outlook is Tejas Networks' impressive execution of the BSNL 4G project. This significant undertaking has not only surpassed revenue expectations but also boosted profit margins, demonstrating the company's operational strength. Analysts are particularly impressed by the company's ability to capitalize on India's focus on domestic manufacturing, further bolstered by government initiatives like the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme.

Beyond the BSNL contract, Tejas Networks is well-positioned to benefit from substantial investments in national digital infrastructure projects like BharatNet and railway network upgrades. Furthermore, strategic partnerships with major players like TCom and TCS are opening doors to new client acquisitions, expanding the company's market reach. The global trend of diversifying away from Chinese telecom equipment also presents a significant tailwind.

### Unlocking Growth Potential and Revised Valuation

Emkay Global's report projects a dramatic revenue increase for Tejas Networks in fiscal year 2025, anticipating a four-fold jump compared to fiscal year 2024, driven by ongoing BSNL and BharatNet project completions. To reflect this accelerated growth, the firm has revised its revenue estimates upward for fiscal years 2025 and 2026. Additionally, the margin beat observed in recent performance has led to an upward revision in the estimated EBITDA margin, signaling improved profitability. This revised financial outlook underpins Emkay Global's increased target price for Tejas Networks' stock.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely observing Tejas Networks' continued progress on its large-scale government projects, as well as its success in securing new international and domestic clients. The company's ability to maintain its operational efficiency and capitalize on the evolving global telecom landscape will be key indicators of its future performance. The market will also be keen to see how Tejas Networks navigates any potential supply chain challenges or competitive pressures.
