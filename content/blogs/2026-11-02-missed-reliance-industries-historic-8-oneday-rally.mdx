## Reliance Industries Skyrockets: What's Driving the 8% Surge?

Reliance Industries experienced a dramatic surge on Monday, with shares jumping a remarkable 8% to hit an intraday high of Rs 1,260. This marks the conglomerate's most significant single-day gain in approximately seven years, a powerful move that has captured the attention of investors and analysts alike, following a robust quarterly earnings report.

### Technical Indicators Signal Strong Momentum

Following the release of its Q2 earnings, where Reliance Industries reported a significant 41% year-on-year increase in consolidated net profit, technical analysts are pointing to a strong bullish sentiment. Charts indicate a notable recovery, with a powerful bullish candle accompanied by substantial trading volume, suggesting renewed investor confidence. The stock is now attempting to break free from a downward trend, with key moving averages showing positive crossovers and the Relative Strength Index (RSI) indicating improved momentum above the 60-mark.

### Brokerages Bullish on Future Growth

The positive earnings, bolstered by strong performance from the oil-to-chemicals and digital services divisions, have led several brokerages to adopt a more optimistic outlook on Reliance Industries. The company's revenue also saw a healthy 29% year-on-year increase. Analysts highlight that the oil-to-chemicals segment, in particular, was a major contributor to earnings growth, with EBITDA reaching a record high. This robust financial performance underpins the bullish technical signals and suggests that the recent rally could be the start of a more sustained upward trend.

### What To Watch

Investors will be closely monitoring if Reliance Industries can sustain its break above the Rs 1,255 resistance level. A continued upward trajectory could see the stock testing further highs, with Rs 1,346 being a potential next target. Conversely, the Rs 1,250 level is expected to act as a crucial support zone. The market will also be watching for continued positive commentary from brokerages and further confirmation of the earnings growth drivers.
