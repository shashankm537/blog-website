## HDFC Bank Stock: ICICI Securities Sees Big Upside, Upgrades to 'Buy'

Investors are buzzing as ICICI Securities has thrown its support behind HDFC Bank, recommending a "Buy" rating and setting an ambitious target price of Rs 1,850. This optimistic outlook comes on the heels of significant post-merger adjustments within the bank's financial structure, signaling a potentially strong growth trajectory ahead.

### Navigating the Post-Merger Landscape

The recent merger has brought about notable changes to HDFC Bank's balance sheet. We're seeing a pronounced increase in the Loan-to-Deposit Ratio (LDR) and a dip in the share of low-cost Current Account Savings Account (CASA) deposits, alongside a significant rise in borrowings. While the merger has boosted the bank's loan market share by a substantial 330 basis points to 14.5%, its deposit market share growth has been more measured, increasing by 110 basis points year-on-year to 11.0%. Despite these shifts, ICICI Securities anticipates robust deposit growth, estimating an 18% compound annual growth rate (CAGR) for FY24-26, translating to an absolute increase of INR 4.0-5.0 trillion annually.

### Growth Projections and Stock Re-Rating

Looking ahead, ICICI Securities projects a more balanced approach to LDR and borrowing costs. Consequently, their loan growth estimates have been revised downwards to a 13% CAGR for FY24-26, with the LDR expected to ease to 100% for FY25 and 96% for FY26, down from the current 104%. The key variable influencing future growth and Net Interest Margins (NIMs) will be the continued accretion of deposits. While the Q4FY24 NIM performance was slightly better than anticipated, it may have been influenced by seasonal factors. The firm has adjusted its Earnings Per Share (EPS) estimates for FY25 and FY26 downwards by 5%, primarily due to the recalibrated growth forecasts.

### What To Watch

With HDFC Bank's stock price experiencing a notable correction of 10% year-to-date, ICICI Securities sees this as an opportune moment to upgrade. Investors will be closely watching the bank's ability to grow its deposits effectively while managing its loan growth and borrowing costs. The gradual nature of borrowing substitution and credit growth relative to the broader system will be crucial indicators for the bank's re-rating potential.
