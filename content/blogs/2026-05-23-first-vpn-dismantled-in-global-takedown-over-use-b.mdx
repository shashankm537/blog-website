## Elite VPN Used by Ransomware Gangs Shut Down in Major International Crackdown

In a significant blow to the shadowy world of cybercrime, law enforcement agencies across Europe and North America have successfully dismantled a virtual private network (VPN) service, dubbed "First VPN," which served as a critical tool for over two dozen ransomware operations. This coordinated takedown, codenamed Operation Saffron, marks a major victory in the ongoing fight against sophisticated cybercriminal enterprises.

### A Shield for Criminals Unveiled

For years, First VPN operated as a clandestine service, specifically catering to the needs of cybercriminals. Its infrastructure was meticulously designed to provide anonymity, allowing paying users to mask their identities while conducting illicit activities. This included ransomware attacks that have plagued businesses worldwide, large-scale data theft operations, and disruptive denial-of-service attacks. The VPN service actively marketed itself on dark web forums frequented by cybercriminals, explicitly promising evasion of law enforcement and a commitment to non-cooperation with any judicial authority.

### Global Effort Leads to Wide-Reaching Disruption

Operation Saffron, a multi-national effort spearheaded by France and the Netherlands, involved a swift and decisive series of actions. Between May 19th and 20th, authorities executed warrants, interviewed the VPN service's administrator, and conducted searches, including a significant raid in Ukraine. This culminated in the seizure of 33 servers and the takedown of critical infrastructure essential for First VPN's global operations. The implications are far-reaching, with Europol confirming that users of the now-defunct service have been notified, and authorities are now aware of their identities.

### What To Watch

The dismantling of First VPN is expected to significantly complicate operations for numerous ransomware groups, potentially increasing their risk of detection and capture. Law enforcement agencies worldwide will likely be scrutinizing the data seized during the operation for further leads and potential arrests. The success of Operation Saffron also sends a clear message to other illicit service providers that their days of operating with impunity are numbered.
