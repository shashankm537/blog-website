## From Unicorn Hunter to Fund Leader: Yvonne Bajela Launches $17M European Venture Fund

After more than a decade scouting and backing some of Europe's most promising startups, veteran investor Yvonne Bajela has officially launched her own venture capital firm, Evertrue. The newly formed fund has already secured a significant $17 million, signaling a strong vote of confidence in Bajela's vision and her ability to identify the next wave of innovation.

### A Deep Well of Experience Fuels New Ventures

Bajela isn't new to the high-stakes world of venture capital. Her previous tenures at prominent firms like LocalGlobe and Impact X Capital Partners saw her play a crucial role in the success stories of now-household names such as fintech giant Wise and insurtech innovator Marshmallow. This extensive background, marked by a keen eye for disruptive technologies, provides a solid foundation for Evertrue's mission. "After more than a decade backing founders who bet everything on their ideas, it felt like time to make a bet of my own," Bajela stated, emphasizing her desire for the independence to "act on those convictions and build something that reflected my own approach to investing."

### Fueling Early-Stage Innovation Across Europe

Evertrue is poised to make a significant impact on the early-stage investment landscape, focusing its capital on nascent companies across the UK and Europe. The firm has already made initial investments in seven promising startups and plans to back approximately 20 companies over the next three years, with an average check size of around £350,000. Bajela points to the current AI boom as a catalyst for this timely launch, noting how it has not only "democratized the ability to build" but also created fertile ground for early-stage investors to find ventures with the potential for enduring success. This strategic focus aligns with the thriving tech ecosystem in the UK, which continues to produce globally significant companies and attract top talent.

### What To Watch

With $17 million at its disposal and a founder with a proven track record, all eyes will be on Evertrue as it deploys its capital. Investors and founders alike will be keen to see which sectors and specific companies Bajela's fund chooses to support, and whether Evertrue can replicate the success of her previous investments in identifying and nurturing the next generation of European tech leaders. The firm's early moves will offer a glimpse into its investment thesis and its impact on the competitive European startup scene.
