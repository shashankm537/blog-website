## HDFC Bank: Analysts Eye Significant Upside Despite Post-Merger Adjustments

A prominent financial institution, ICICI Securities, has issued a strong "buy" recommendation for HDFC Bank, setting an ambitious target price of Rs 1,850. This optimistic outlook comes in the wake of the bank's recent merger and the subsequent adjustments to its financial structure, with analysts believing the current market valuation presents a compelling opportunity for investors.

### Navigating Post-Merger Balance Sheet Shifts

The integration of HDFC into the bank has led to noticeable changes in its balance sheet. Key among these are an increased loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) and a dip in the share of low-cost current account and savings account (CASA) deposits, alongside a rise in borrowing. While the merger has boosted HDFC Bank's loan market share significantly, its deposit market share growth has been more measured.

ICICI Securities anticipates substantial deposit growth for the bank over the next two fiscal years, projecting an absolute rise of INR 4-5 trillion annually. Despite this strong deposit accretion, the bank is expected to focus on maintaining a more balanced LDR and managing its borrowings. Consequently, loan growth estimates have been revised downwards, with a projected LDR easing in the coming years.

### Growth Prospects and Analyst Outlook

While the fourth quarter Net Interest Margin (NIM) performance exceeded expectations, analysts attribute this partly to seasonal factors. A slight reduction in earnings per share (EPS) estimates for the next two fiscal years is noted, primarily due to the recalibrated growth projections. However, the bank is still expected to deliver robust Return on Assets (RoA) and Return on Equity (RoE) figures.

The brokerage has maintained its target price of Rs 1,850, and the decision to upgrade the stock to "buy" is driven by a recent significant correction in its share price, which has seen a 10% year-to-date decline. The analysts suggest that a re-rating of the stock might be gradual, as factors like credit growth relative to the system and the pace of borrowing substitution will be closely watched by the market.

### What to Watch

Investors will be keen to observe how HDFC Bank manages its loan growth in conjunction with its strong deposit-gathering capabilities. The evolution of its NIM and the bank's strategy for borrowing substitution will also be critical indicators of its future financial performance and the potential realization of the Rs 1,850 target.
